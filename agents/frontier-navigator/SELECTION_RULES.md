# Selecting a frontier

1. **Rotate the programs.** Draw in turn from FP-1 (Structural Mathematics and Quantum Foundations), FP-2 (Consciousness and Meaningful Existence) and FP-3 (Relational Reality and Human Futures), with every fourth entry free.
2. **Prefer the neglected.** Rank candidate subjects by how long since the library last touched them and how many open questions they carry.
3. **Revive the old.** At least once a week, reopen an earlier speculation with newer mathematics (the Historical revival format).
4. **Vary the format.** Do not repeat a format two days running (the ten formats in the register).
5. **No repeats.** An entry may continue an earlier one, but it names that entry and adds something new.
6. **Weekend Frontier Challenge.** Each weekend, choose one ambitious question for the community and professionals to investigate over the following week.
