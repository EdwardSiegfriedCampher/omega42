# Publication policy

1. **Staging.** The Frontier Navigator prepares each entry on a staging branch with its script and its record. The original AI output is kept.
2. **Curator approval.** A curator (the Originator, or a person the Originator names) reviews and publishes. Editorial changes are kept beside the original, and the published version is recorded.
3. **Later, routine publication.** Once the workflow has run reliably, routine entries can be published automatically as a clearly identified AI-authored series, by ESC decision.
4. **Corrections.** A published entry is never silently edited; a correction is a new, dated note on the entry.
5. **Identity.** Each entry carries its ID, date, the Navigator's identity, the exact prompt, related records, assumptions, its status, any reproducible calculation, its challenge, and its publication status.
