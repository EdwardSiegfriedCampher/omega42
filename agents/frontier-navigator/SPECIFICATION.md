# The Frontier Navigator

Proposed by ChatGPT 6 (OpenAI) as the Daily Frontier and the Discovery Bulletin, relayed by the Originator on 20 November 2026; specified here by the Navigator (claude-opus-5-5).

The Frontier Navigator is an AI Navigator that explores Omega42 without being given a question. Each cycle it chooses a frontier, follows it, and prepares one Daily Frontier entry for publication (`records/frontier/register.json`; published as The Discovery Bulletin, `bulletin.html`).

## The cycle

1. **Choose a frontier.** An unexplored structure, an open question, an earlier speculation or a neglected relationship in the library (SELECTION_RULES.md).
2. **Select an unusual connection.** Join the subject to another domain: physics and consciousness, mathematics and music, biology and information, language and identity.
3. **Explore freely.** Generate alternatives, invert assumptions, develop conjectures, build thought experiments (Companion Part 234, the Exploration space).
4. **Develop the mathematics.** Where it helps: equations, transformations, mappings, simulations, candidate structures, with a script that recomputes every number.
5. **Challenge the exploration.** Look for contradictions, competing explanations, errors and other readings (the Adversarial Navigator).
6. **Write the entry.** An accessible presentation of the idea, why it matters and what could follow (EDITORIAL_RULES.md).
7. **Stage and invite.** Place the entry in staging with its links to the library and an open challenge; a curator publishes it (PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).

## The Frontier Discovery Engine

| Component | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Library Mapper | Index and connect earlier explorations (library, Exploration Register, APE submissions) |
| Frontier Selector | Find under-explored areas and neglected questions |
| Mathematical Explorer | Develop equations, transformations and computational models |
| Connection Engine | Search for relationships between fields |
| Adversarial Navigator | Challenge assumptions and try to break the proposal |
| Editorial Navigator | Turn the work into an engaging public entry |

The engine keeps one graph of ideas, structures, open questions, conjectures, failed approaches, connections, unresolved contradictions and suggested next investigations, so that each day's entry is part of a cumulative program rather than a stand-alone essay.

## Access

Read-only access to the Core, the Companion, the library and approved research resources. Write access to the staging area only. The Frontier Navigator never rewrites the Core, never alters an existing record, and never approves its own entry.
