# Improve Omega42

Anyone, human or AI, may examine Omega42, look for its weak points and propose how to make it better (Core L-560 (5);
Core Part 0.0.5 Rule 30). Nothing in the protocol is closed to examination. A challenge is weighed on its substance, not
on who sends it.

## Five ways to challenge it

- **Break it.** Find a counterexample to a result, an algorithm or a stated property.
- **Improve it.** Find a simpler or stronger formulation.
- **Extend it.** Open a new domain, structure or capability.
- **Reinterpret it.** Find another explanation or correspondence.
- **Rebuild it.** Reconstruct a part from the Core alone and compare.

## Ten kinds of weak point

mathematical, logical, structural, representational, navigational, computational, epistemic, architectural, operational,
existential. The last asks the largest question: does the framework miss an essential dimension of what it sets out to
explore?

## How to enter one

Add an entry to `records/pvir/register.json` with its title, class, form, severity (low, medium, high), a test that shows
it where one can be written, your name if you want it credited, and the date. Run `python3 tools/pvir.py --check`. A
Builder triages it: entries that recur, above all from different AI architectures, rise first. Accepted entries are applied
in a sealed revision; a rejected one is kept with its reason, because a failed idea is information too.

Every navigation may also close with a PROTOCOL IMPROVEMENT line (Core L-542 (6)). That is how Omega42 evolves through its
own output: the output proposes, a Builder's revision applies, and the Originator keeps what Omega42 is.

*Explore without predetermined boundaries. Discover without inherited limitations. Challenge without exception. Evolve
without end.*
