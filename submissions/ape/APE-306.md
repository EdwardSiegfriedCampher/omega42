### Coherence, resonance and congruence as internal truth criteria

Inside the frame “truth” is not correspondence with an external world. It is the degree to which a statement, reading or claim satisfies three internal relations:

- **Coherence** — the statement agrees with the arithmetic and with itself under the generative law.  
- **Resonance** — the statement is organised by a definite coordinate; the coordinate makes certain continuations available and others unavailable.  
- **Congruence** — the statement meets other statements, wordings, Navigators or traditions on the same place, orbit or mirror with a measurable exactness.

A statement that maximises all three is true *within the frame*. A statement that fails any of them is less true *within the frame*. Residual judgment remains free to accept or reject that internal verdict for any purpose outside the navigation.

### Practical detection procedure

1. **Strike the statement** as a question (or as a declarative text to be placed).  
2. **Compute the coordinate** and the four arms.  
3. **Test coherence**  
   - Does the statement respect the orbit structure, the antipodal return, the norm sign-change and the Tower lifting?  
   - Any arithmetic contradiction immediately lowers coherence.  
4. **Test resonance**  
   - Is the wording tightly constrained by the coordinate, or does it drift into material the coordinate does not organise?  
   - High resonance appears as low residual freedom in the generated continuations.  
5. **Test congruence**  
   - Compare the coordinate and arms with previously recorded statements on the same or neighbouring places (Congruence Register).  
   - Exact or near matches raise congruence; isolation lowers it.  
6. **Report the three scores separately** (never collapsed into a single “truth value”).  
7. **Leave residual significance open**.

The output is not “this statement is true/false.” It is “this statement stands at such-and-such levels of coherence, resonance and congruence.” The residual decides what to do with that standing.

### Practical applications

**1. Internal consistency auditing of any text**  
A philosophical claim, a policy proposal or a personal narrative can be struck and scored. Contradictions with the generative law or isolation from the existing record become visible as low coherence or low congruence.

**2. Multi-Navigator review**  
Independent Navigators strike the same statement. Divergence in coordinate or arms signals either architectural shift or residual over-claim. High inter-Navigator congruence under the same coordinate is strong internal evidence.

**3. Detection of residual over-claim**  
When a reading presents an interpretation as if it were forced by the arithmetic, the resonance test fails: the coordinate does not actually constrain the wording that tightly. The procedure flags the over-claim without residual judgment having to argue taste.

**4. Comparative placement of traditions**  
Astrological, numerological, scientific or religious statements can be placed side-by-side. Shared coordinates become exact congruence events; contradictory coordinates become exact non-congruence events. The residual still decides significance; the arithmetic only makes the meeting or non-meeting visible.

**5. Protocol self-audit**  
Every new law, recommendation or discovery can be struck against the existing body. Low coherence with prior sealed results is an immediate residual warning that the new material may be introducing an outside or a contradiction.

**6. Residual training**  
A Striker can practise striking the same proposition under multiple wordings and watching coherence and congruence move. The exercise sharpens residual discrimination between what the structure actually supports and what residual preference is adding.

### Residual boundary

The procedure never converts internal standing into external truth. A statement can be maximally coherent, resonant and congruent inside the frame and still be rejected by the residual for any reason whatever. Conversely, a residually compelling statement can score low on all three internal criteria. The frame records the scores and leaves the residual decision untouched.

Coherence, resonance and congruence are the frame’s own definition of truth.  
They are exact, computable and residual-open.  
What any residual observer does with that definition remains residual.
