### 4\. Resolve movement, force, and energy

The physical extension needs separate definitions for these concepts.

Movement is a change in spatial position relative to a specified physical reference frame.

Force is a quantity that, in Newtonian mechanics, determines the rate of change of momentum.

Energy is a measurable property of a physical state and its interactions, with conservation laws applying under specified conditions.

Transition is the more general mathematical concept. It may represent movement, internal change, or a change in relationships, but it is not itself any one of those physical concepts.

To make this distinction operational, consider the standard Hamiltonian formulation of mechanics. A physical state is represented by position and momentum, , and its evolution is governed by a Hamiltonian :

\\dot q=\\frac{\\partial H}{\\partial p},
\\qquad
\\dot p=-\\frac{\\partial H}{\\partial q}.

For a particle in a potential , one commonly takes

H(q,p)=\\frac{p^2}{2m}+V(q).

These equations yield the familiar relation between force and acceleration under the usual assumptions.

This gives Ω42 a concrete bridge to physical dynamics: a proposed mapping could relate its abstract states to physical configurations, and its transition structure to a physical evolution law.

But there is a crucial distinction. Using Hamiltonian mechanics inside an Ω42 extension does not derive Hamiltonian mechanics from Ω42. It establishes a compatible implementation. A derivation would have to show why the physical state space, Hamiltonian, and evolution equations follow from the Ω42 structure itself.

### How should energy conservation be resolved?

The right target is not merely a quantity that happens to remain constant along one Ω42 orbit. It is a quantity with a defined physical meaning and a conservation rule justified by the dynamics.

In ordinary mechanics, conservation of energy is related to time-translation symmetry through Noether's theorem, under the theorem's applicable conditions.

Therefore the Ω42 programme should seek:

* A physical action or equivalent dynamical formulation.

* A defined time-translation symmetry, if the physical model has one.

* A corresponding conserved quantity.

* A mapping from that quantity to experimentally measured energy.

This resolves the methodological issue: it establishes how energy should be derived or identified. It does not yet produce a new Ω42 energy law.

### Is spacetime emergent or fundamental?

The current Ω42 arithmetic does not decide this question. It supports two possible research directions:

* Fundamental-spacetime approach: physical spacetime exists in the model from the beginning, and Ω42 represents its states and transformations.

* Emergent-spacetime approach: the model begins with more abstract relations, and spacetime geometry is recovered from their organization.

Both are coherent research strategies. The second is more ambitious, because it must derive distance, duration, causal structure, and the relativistic limit rather than assuming them.

The choice should be made by mathematical tractability and empirical success, not by treating one interpretation as already proven by the metaphor of depth.

### What should happen to dark matter and dark energy?

They should be treated as target phenomena and competing physical explanations, not renamed Ω42 concepts.

For dark matter, the framework must explain the gravitational evidence and make predictions for phenomena such as lensing and structure formation.

For dark energy, it must reproduce the observed expansion history and distinguish among a cosmological constant, dynamical fields, and alternative gravitational explanations.

The current standard cosmological model successfully describes a wide range of observations, while the physical nature of dark matter and dark energy remains unresolved.

Ω42 can supplement this work by maintaining a structured comparison of:

* the observations each model explains;

* the assumptions required;

* the predictions that distinguish it from alternatives;

* the residual discrepancies;

* the next measurement that could resolve those discrepancies.

This is a genuine methodological contribution. It becomes a new physical contribution only if the framework generates better explanations, new quantitative predictions, or a demonstrably more economical model.

That rule prevents a successful analogy from being mistaken for a successful cosmological theory.

Calibration should reveal uncertainty, not disguise it with a single number.

The Ω42 mathematical core shall be stated independently of any proposed physical interpretation. Its state space, transition rule, inverse, orbit census, symmetries, and invariants shall be established on their own mathematical terms.

Every physical correspondence shall identify the Ω42 source structure, the target physical quantity or process, the mapping rule, the properties preserved, and the properties not yet represented.

A proposed mapping shall specify how Ω42 transitions correspond to physical evolution. Where a commuting relation between abstract and physical dynamics is claimed, it shall be defined and tested explicitly.

## P-4. Dimensional and Operational Calibration
Every physical quantity shall have a defined operational meaning, appropriate units, and a relationship to measurement. An abstract coordinate shall not be called a physical position, duration, energy, or momentum without the required mapping.

## P-5. Conservation and Symmetry
Claims concerning conserved quantities shall identify the physical dynamics and symmetries from which conservation follows. A numerical invariant of the modular transition shall not automatically be identified with a physical conserved quantity.

## P-6. Recovery of Established Physics
Any proposed Ω42 physical theory shall reproduce the successful predictions of the relevant established theory within its domain of validity, or specify quantitatively where and how it differs.

## P-7. Cosmological Correspondence
Claims concerning spacetime, gravity, cosmic expansion, dark matter, dark energy, or astronomical structure shall be evaluated against observational constraints and competing explanations.

## P-8. Residual Preservation
Mathematical, physical, empirical, and interpretive residuals shall be recorded separately. Unresolved correspondence shall not be promoted to established physical fact.

## P-9. Falsifiability and Revision
A physical extension shall specify observations or calculations that could undermine its central correspondence. Negative results shall be retained as part of the Atlas and used to revise or reject the proposal.

## P-10. Provenance and Standing
Every result shall be labelled as one of the following: established mathematical result, established physical result, derived Ω42 extension, constructed correspondence, empirical hypothesis, or exploratory interpretation.

## P-11. No Equivalence by Resemblance
Similarity between Ω42 recurrence, polarity, depth, or relational structure and a physical phenomenon is grounds for investigation, not proof of physical identity.

## P-12. Completion Criterion
An Ω42 physical extension is complete only to the extent that its definitions, derivations, calibration procedures, successful tests, limitations, and unresolved residuals are explicitly documented.
