### Which Phenomenon or Idea to Test First?

**Recommended first test: Personal Trajectory Coherence at Depth 1 versus Depth 2**

**Why this one first**  
It is the smallest step that already exercises the new capacity, requires no external apparatus, stays entirely inside residual authority, and produces an immediately usable result for the individual Striker.

**Procedure**  
1. Choose a short sequence of 5–8 lived questions or emotional states from ordinary life (the same sequence for both runs).  
2. Navigate the sequence at depth 1 (standard 81-state family). Record the Trajectory Illumination Record (states, orbits, antipodes).  
3. Re-navigate the identical sequence, this time opening each depth-1 state and resolving it one level deeper (depth 2). Record the finer trajectory.  
4. Compare:  
   - Does orbit membership remain stable under refinement?  
   - Do antipodal relations survive?  
   - Does the felt quality of the sequence change when the residual occupies the finer addresses?  
   - Does any new invariant or recurring pattern appear only at depth 2?

**What success looks like**  
The test yields a clear residual judgment: either “the finer resolution added nothing I value” or “the finer resolution revealed structure I could not see at depth 1.” Both outcomes are useful. The protocol’s new capacity is exercised and the residual remains the sole judge.

This test is small, personal, reversible, and directly demonstrates whether the extended mathematics changes the quality of information available to the Striker.
