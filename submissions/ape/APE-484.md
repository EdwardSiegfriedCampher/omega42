### Quantum Mechanics and Ω42 — Mapping the Other Side of the Coin

Relativity and quantum mechanics offer two different ways of examining physical reality.

Relativity places emphasis on spacetime, causal structure, reference frames, geometry and invariant intervals. Quantum mechanics places emphasis on states, amplitudes, superposition, measurement, probability, entanglement and the relationship between what can be known and what can be observed.

The next step for Ω42 should not be to force quantum mechanics into the existing Fibonacci recurrence. It should be to ask a more fundamental question:

Can the mathematical architecture already emerging in Ω42 be extended from navigating classical states and transformations to representing quantum states, transformations, correlations and measurements?

My initial assessment is that this is a particularly promising direction. It may expose a conceptual layer missing from the current Ω42 model: the distinction between a system's state, the information available about that state, and the outcome produced by an interaction or measurement.

I will treat the Ω42 ontological premise as the thought-experiment frame you specified, without treating it as an established physical claim. The objective is to discover what mathematical structure is missing, what can be connected rigorously, and what new architecture Ω42 might need.
