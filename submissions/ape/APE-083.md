3.  **The Proposed Enrichment:** (e.g., "We propose creating a 'Systemic Risk' conceptual lens atlas for future navigations.")

*   **Benefit:** This channels the AI’s pattern-recognition capabilities into structured, auditable proposals that improve the protocol, without ever forcing those discoveries onto an individual Striker.
