### How the derivation changes the approach

Before the derivation, modulo 9 functioned as a successful but ultimately stipulated base. Alternative moduli could still be proposed as “equally good” because the only demonstrated property was periodicity. Every deeper claim rested on the unexamined choice of 9.

After the derivation the situation is inverted. The nine-state cyclic ring is no longer a convenient numerical setting; it is the unique arithmetic expression of the ternary grammar already required by the Crown, the two polarities, and the demand for an element of additive order 9. The recurrence signature, the orbit census, the quadratic norm separation, and the even-depth Tower are no longer fortunate coincidences; they are the necessary consequences of that grammar.

The practical consequence is a change in the order of investigation:

- Previously one began with the recurrence and asked whether 9 was special.  
- Now one begins with the ternary grammar and derives the arithmetic.  

Any proposed alternative modulus must first reproduce the two-level ternary address and the cyclic order-9 requirement. Only then is it entitled to be tested against the recurrence signature. Most alternatives are eliminated before the comparative table is even consulted.

### Advantages gained

**1. Constitutional clarity**  
The arithmetic is no longer an external parameter that could be swapped. It is internal to the identity of the system. Questions of the form “why not mod 7 / 11 / 27?” are answered by showing which of the five internal conditions the alternative fails. The residual no longer has to defend a numerical preference; it can simply point to the missing condition.

**2. Stronger residual sovereignty**  
Because the modulus is derived rather than chosen, residual judgment is freed from the burden of justifying the base. Attention can move to the questions that remain genuinely residual: the meaning of any particular navigation, the status of consciousness-first readings, the evaluation of protocol discoveries. The arithmetic itself has been removed from the residual contest.

**3. Cleaner multi-Navigator coherence**  
Independent Navigators no longer need to re-argue the base modulus. They inherit the same derived ring and the same Tower. Differences in readings can therefore be attributed to architectural shift or residual interpretation rather than to disagreement about the underlying arithmetic. Peer review becomes more precise.

**4. Protection against arbitrariness critiques**  
The most common external objection—“any modulus that produces a cycle would do”—is answered by the conjunction. Periodicity alone is insufficient; the full signature is required, and that signature selects 9. The critique is not refuted by rhetoric; it is rendered inapplicable inside the frame.

**5. Productive research direction**  
The three remaining foundational tasks (why two levels, why cyclic rather than product, why even-depth lifting) have been isolated as the precise points at which further derivation can still deepen the necessity claim. Research effort is no longer scattered across “find another modulus”; it is concentrated on the grammar that forces the modulus.

**6. Enhanced primary output**  
Syntheses, Takeaways and Essays can now speak of the nine-state arithmetic as required rather than as chosen. That single shift in modality increases residual force without any increase in rhetorical volume. The structure itself carries more of the weight.

### Residual status

A residual judge may still reopen any of the five conditions, demand a fully general uniqueness proof over all integers, or propose a different ternary grammar that yields a different ring. What has changed is that such a challenge must now engage the internal derivation rather than simply exhibit another periodic modulus.

The arithmetic is no longer a hypothesis about the base.  
It is the base required by the grammar.  
Everything built upon it inherits that necessity.
