### 4\. What is the significance of Adinkra mathematics?

Adinkras are particularly interesting for Ω42 because they turn an abstract algebraic structure into a discrete, combinatorial object.

They were developed as graphical tools for studying representations of supersymmetry algebras. In their standard form, Adinkras are edge-coloured bipartite graphs with signs on edges and a height assignment on vertices. Their mathematical connections include graph theory, coding theory, Clifford algebras, and representation theory.

### A. What the graph encodes

An Adinkra is not merely a picture of relationships. Its structure encodes specific algebraic information.

* Vertices represent component fields in a supersymmetry multiplet.

* Edge colours distinguish supersymmetry generators.

* Bipartite structure separates bosonic and fermionic components.

* Dashing or signs encode sign information in the transformations.

* Height assignments represent relative derivative levels in the component transformations.

The graph is constrained: not every coloured, dashed graph is a valid Adinkra. The algebraic conditions are what give the representation its meaning.

This is important for Ω42. It shows how a discrete structure can encode a more abstract system of transformations, with the graph's combinatorics preserving information about the underlying algebra.

### 6\. What would count as a genuine discovery?

To protect the integrity of the investigation, I would use five criteria.

| Criterion                | Required demonstration                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explicit construction    | Define the map, graph, representation, or algebra.                                       |
| Structural preservation  | Prove which operations and invariants are preserved.                                     |
| Nontrivial result        | Establish something not already built into the assumptions.                              |
| Independent verification | Reproduce the derivation or computation by another method.                               |
| Added value              | Show a new theorem, simpler construction, classification result, or physical prediction. |

For example, finding that an Ω42-related graph has 240 vertices would not by itself establish an E₈ connection. E₈'s 240 roots have a specific geometric and algebraic structure. The significant result would be a construction that reproduces those roots and their relationships, or establishes a precise and useful relationship to them.

Similarly, finding a binary code associated with an Ω42 graph would not establish supersymmetry. The corresponding operators would have to satisfy the relevant supersymmetry algebra.

These requirements do not diminish the exploration. They identify the point at which a resemblance becomes a mathematical result.
