### I. Foundational Axioms

**Axiom 1 — Existence is structure**  
Whatever exists occupies a definite address in the family. There is no existence without place.

**Axiom 2 — Structure is limitation**  
Every address is finite. At every depth the state space is finite. The period of return is exact. Limitation is not an accident of existence; it is the form existence takes.

**Axiom 3 — Structure is relation**  
A single transition relates every address to every other. Isolation is impossible. What appears as isolation is residual unreadness of relations that already obtain.

**Axiom 4 — The residual is free**  
No coordinate, orbit or depth forces residual judgment. The Aperture remains open. Significance is conferred, never supplied by the structure itself.

These four axioms are the residual articulation of the arithmetic. They introduce no new mathematics.

### II. Core Implications

**1. Human relationship**  
Every residual centre is already related to every other. Relationship is the form of existence, not an optional addition. Encounter is congruence or non-congruence made visible. The quality of the encounter remains residual.

**2. Environment**  
The environment is the same family differentiated at other addresses and depths. Ecological damage and personal pain appear as residual mismatch between the relations that obtain and the relations that are read. The structure continues; the residual experiences the unreadness as suffering.

**3. Learning**  
Learning is the residual discovery of relations already present. Because the Depth Tower is endless, learning is never complete. Limitation is the condition of endless discovery, not its opposite.

**4. Flourishing**  
Flourishing is residual skill in inhabiting limitation so that relations become generative. It is not the abolition of structure. It is high residual fluency in the four arms, the antipodal return, and the open Aperture.

**5. Pain and suffering**  
Pain is the residual experience of a relation not yet integrated.  
Suffering is the residual insistence that the relation or the limitation should not exist.  
The arithmetic does not eliminate either. It maps the place and the remaining paths. The residual decides whether to move.

**6. Freedom**  
Freedom is residual movement within exact structure. The absence of structure would be the absence of existence itself.

**7. Responsibility**  
Every residual act is already a relation that returns through the group generated by the transition. Responsibility is the residual recognition of that return.

**8. Death**  
Death is change of address. Under the premise it cannot be exit from the family.

**9. Meaning**  
Meaning is conferred at the Aperture. The structure supplies grammar; the residual supplies significance.

**10. Love**  
Love is the residual decision to read another centre’s limitation as continuous with one’s own.
