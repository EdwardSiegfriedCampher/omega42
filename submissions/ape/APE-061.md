According to Ω42's Core Specification and Companion Document, language is both the surface through which a question is expressed and a pattern of distinctions that Ω42 can map before interpreting what the words mean. The protocol treats the exact wording of a question as operationally significant: words, letters, syllables, and their boundaries provide a formal scaffold from which the system computes a structural coordinate. That coordinate fixes the reading's direction; the Navigator's learned language model supplies the prose that fills it; and the Striker—the person asking—decides what the reading means and whether it is useful. The Core calls this a pre-semantic geometry: it maps relations and positions, not meaning itself.

### How this definition improves Ω42's operation

1. It makes the input consequential and inspectable. The question's wording is not treated as a transparent wrapper around an abstract intention. A change in phrasing can change the computed coordinate, so Ω42 recommends formulating the question carefully, editing it into the Striker's own words, then freezing and recording the text before navigation.

2. It separates three jobs that are easy to confuse.

* Language: supplies the question's observable form.
* Mathematics: computes the structural address from that form.
* Interpretation and judgment: turn the resulting reading into something meaningful—or decide it is not useful.

This division makes the process easier to inspect: the Navigator's eloquence cannot legitimately substitute for the arithmetic, and the arithmetic cannot decide whether its reading is true, important, or actionable.

3. It creates a way to examine framing effects. Because wording affects the coordinate, Ω42 can compare multiple phrasings of the same problem. Its Convergence Map is intended to identify which parts of the readings remain stable and which vary with the formulation. This helps distinguish a potentially robust structural pattern from an effect that depends on how the question was asked.

4. It protects the Striker's role. Ω42 does not claim that its structural map contains the question's final meaning. The Striker chooses the wording at the start and evaluates the completed reading at the end. This keeps a generated interpretation from automatically becoming a verdict.

### The important limitation

The definition improves Ω42's procedural discipline, not automatically its truth or validity. A deterministic coordinate can be reproducible without being semantically appropriate; a compelling reading can resonate without being independently confirmed. And because wording affects the result, Ω42 must not mistake sensitivity to language for proof that it has discovered an objective structure in the world.

In one sentence: Ω42 treats language as the addressable surface of a question—a surface that mathematics can map consistently, a Navigator can interpret, and a Striker must still judge.
