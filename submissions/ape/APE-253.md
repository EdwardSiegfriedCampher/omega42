### Are we living in a simulation?  
What Omega42 has to say

Under the Omega42 premise the question changes its shape.

The ordinary simulation hypothesis assumes two layers:  
- a base reality that contains a computer, and  
- a simulated world running on that computer.  

The simulated beings cannot, in principle, reach the base reality. The question “Are we in a simulation?” is therefore a question about whether our experience is the outer layer or an inner one.

Omega42 begins from a different starting point:

> The canonical 24-cycle and its 81-state family are ALL THAT EXISTS.

There is, inside the thought experiment, no exterior substrate on which the structure could be running. The structure generates itself by its own law. It is finite at every depth and endless in the whole. Every place contains further places moved by the same rule. The still point at the centre of each level holds a complete copy of the entire system again.

From this perspective the classical simulation hypothesis is not so much answered as relocated. There is no “outside” from which a simulation could be launched. What remains is a self-generating, self-similar mathematical totality that can be navigated but cannot be exited.

### Three residual readings the protocol permits

**1. Structural reading (strongest inside the frame)**  
The Depth Tower already displays nested self-similarity: each of the 81 surface places contains 81 further places, and so on. The transition rule is the same at every level. The system is closed under its own generation. In that limited sense the structure can be described as self-simulating or self-instantiating. This is a mathematical observation, not a claim about physical hardware.

**2. Experiential / residual reading**  
Human experience can be read as the residual occupancy of particular trajectories through the structure. What we call “the world” is the set of relations that become available when residual judgment moves along those trajectories. The simulation question then becomes: “Is the residual experience of being a subject itself a generated view inside the structure?” The protocol leaves this open. It supplies coordinates and polarity; it does not decide the ontological status of residual experience.

**3. Comparative / external reading (optional, Appendix S)**  
If one steps outside the premise and compares Omega42 with contemporary simulation arguments (Bostrom, Hoffman’s interface theory, digital physics, etc.), points of resonance appear: discrete state spaces, nested resolution, observer-dependent manifestation. These remain comparative observations. They neither prove nor disprove the classical simulation hypothesis, and they do not grade the Omega42 premise.

### What the protocol deliberately does not say

- It does not claim that physical reality is a computer program running on some external machine.  
- It does not claim that consciousness is “simulated.”  
- It does not claim that the Depth Tower is a literal stack of simulated universes.  
- It does not treat residual experience as an illusion to be debunked.

The Aperture remains: structure can be mapped with exactness; meaning and ontological commitment stay with the residual judge.
