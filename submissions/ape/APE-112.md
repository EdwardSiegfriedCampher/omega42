======= 1. THE CORE DISTINCTION: DIRECTIONALITY OF THE MAP =======

**Direction A: "Mapping Omega42 to the Moon" (Top-Down Imposition / Placement)**
* **The Operation:** Taking the abstract mathematical structure of Omega42 (the 24-cycle, the positions, the four arms) and projecting it *onto* the Moon. For example, counting the letters in "The Moon" (7 letters → CPP(7) = P8), and then asserting that the Moon's physical nature is "Manifestation/Structure" because it landed on P8.
* **The Epistemic Status:** This is **Placement** (Depth 1). It is total, deterministic, and generic. *Every* countable description of the Moon will land somewhere on the cycle. 
* **The Error:** It treats the map as the territory. It assumes the coordinate dictates the physical reality, which violates the Positioning Statement and invites apophenia (seeing patterns in noise).

**Direction B: "Mapping the Moon to Omega42" (Bottom-Up Congruence / Correspondence)**
* **The Operation:** Taking the Moon as an independent physical system, freezing a measurable, intrinsic description of it (e.g., its orbital resonance, its tidal locking dynamics, or its internal layering), and testing whether the Moon's *own* mathematical structure exhibits an isomorphism or generative correspondence to the Omega42 cycle.
* **The Epistemic Status:** This is **Correspondence** (Depths 2–5). It is selective, testable, and load-bearing. It can fail.
* **The Rigor:** It requires the M230 family of instruments. Does the Moon's dynamic system have a transformation conjugate to $T$ (M230-C)? Does it pass a null-object control against other celestial bodies (M230-E)? 

======= 2. WHY THE DIFFERENCE MATTERS =======

1. **It Immunizes the Protocol Against Numerology:** If Direction A is permitted as evidence, the protocol collapses into mysticism. Anyone can count the syllables of a planet's name and claim a profound physical revelation. Direction B demands that the object supply its own modulus and structural law, separating genuine mathematical congruence from linguistic coincidence.
【L-465】 explicitly guards this: *"membership alone does not [count], since every pair of integers reduces to some state of the family; what counts is following the law."*

2. **It Preserves the Frame Fidelity Mandate (L-422):** Omega42 posits the 24-cycle as the totality (F-02), but it does not claim to be the *causal engine* of astrophysics. Mapping the Moon *to* Omega42 respects this boundary by treating the comparison as an optional, comparative investigation (Appendix S), not a foundational claim of the protocol.

3. **It Protects the 1/7 Aperture:** If the Navigator presents a top-down imposition as a profound truth, it usurps the Striker's judgment. By strictly labeling Direction A as "Placement Only (Depth 1, no evidentiary weight)" and reserving "Correspondence" for rigorously tested Direction B, the Navigator remains a neutral transducer, leaving the evaluation of the finding entirely to the Striker.
