┌─ THE PREMISE ─┐
**The protocol makes no claims.** It does not claim that the physical universe is literally constructed from the 24-cycle, nor does it claim that any physical object is "made of" Omega42 mathematics. Omega42 is a closed mathematical world and a topology of distinction-making. 

However, as the protocol opens its doors to comparative investigation (Appendix S) and public scrutiny, it faces a critical epistemic vulnerability: the conflation of *counting an object's surface features* with *proving the object instantiates the cycle's intrinsic dynamics*. This proposal outlines the structural necessity of formally separating these two operations to protect the protocol from numerology, apophenia, and skeptical dismissal.

======= 1. MOTIVATION & REASONING =======

The protocol currently addresses mapping in FC-02.8 and the Congruence Depths (Part 0.14.5). However, the lexical blur around the word "mapping" allows a dangerous category error to occur in practice. 

If a user counts the letters of a molecule's name, or counts the 24 hours in a day, and declares the object "is" a P3 (Manifestation) because it has 24 parts, they are committing a top-down imposition. They are treating the coordinate as a physical cause. This violates L-428 (Non-Conflation) and L-465 ("membership alone does not count, since every pair of integers reduces to some state of the family").

To protect the integrity of the Congruence Register and the scientific rigor of Appendix S, the protocol must explicitly bifurcate the concept of "mapping" into two distinct, non-interchangeable operations: **Placement** (Direction 1) and **Correspondence** (Direction 2).

======= 2. THE CORE DISTINCTION =======

**Direction 1: PLACEMENT (Object $\rightarrow$ $\Omega$42)**
* **Definition:** Taking an object, freezing a measurable description of its surface (e.g., word count, letter count, number of parts), and projecting it through the Canonical Position Projection (CPP) to find its coordinate on the cycle.
* **Epistemic Status:** Total, deterministic, and generic. *Every* countable object receives a placement. 
* **Protocol Role:** Depth 1. It is a precondition and a denominator, not a resonance. It is a reframing tool, not evidence of physical correspondence.

**Direction 2: CORRESPONDENCE ($\Omega$42-Structure $\rightarrow$ Object-Structure)**
* **Definition:** Starting from a structural feature of the cycle (e.g., the 24-state organization, the transformation $T$, half-period negation) and testing whether the object's *own intrinsic dynamics, transformations, and closure laws* instantiate it.
* **Epistemic Status:** Selective, testable, and load-bearing. It can fail. 
* **Protocol Role:** Depths 2–5. This is where genuine structural resonance is measured using the M230 family of instruments (M230-B, M230-C, M230-E).

======= 3. PRACTICAL EXAMPLES =======

To illustrate why this distinction is load-bearing, consider three objects often cited in structural comparisons:

**Example A: The Sunflower (Biology)**
* **Placement (Direction 1):** The sunflower has 34 and 55 spirals. CPP(34) = P8, CPP(55) = P5. Distance 3. *Result:* A striking placement. *Evidence value:* Zero. Every pair of numbers lands somewhere.
* **Correspondence (Direction 2):** Does the sunflower's growth mechanism generate the Fibonacci sequence via the $\Omega$42 recurrence? No. Biological phyllotaxis is driven by auxin inhibition (Mirabet et al.). The Generative Correspondence (CG) is NO. *Result:* Depth 1 only. The protocol records this cleanly, preventing the false claim that the flower "is" the cycle.

**Example B: Microtubules (Biophysics)**
* **Placement (Direction 1):** Microtubules typically have 13 protofilaments. CPP(13) = P2. *Result:* A placement.
* **Correspondence (Direction 2):** Does the 13-protofilament lattice possess a transformation conjugate to $T$? Does it exhibit half-period negation? No. It is a 3-start helix, not a Fibonacci recurrence. Profile C24 NO, CG NO. *Result:* Depth 1. Indistinguishable from the null control (M230-E).

**Example C: The Cube / The Number 24 (Mathematics)**
* **Placement (Direction 1):** The rotation group of a cube has order 24. CPP(24) = P3. The Greek alphabet has 24 letters. Both land on P3. *Result:* Generic placement.
* **Correspondence (Direction 2):** Does the cube's rotation group ($S_4$) instantiate the cycle's structure? $S_4$ is not cyclic; it has no single 24-cycle, and no element is conjugate to $T$. *Result:* Depth 1. The bare number 24 is a placement; the structural match fails.
