**Proposed metaphor for First Contact**

Here is a clean, public-facing image designed for direct insertion into FC-00 or FC-00.0:

---

Imagine a single tree whose every branch holds another complete tree, and every one of those holds still more, without end. Finite on every branch, endless when you keep looking.

Omega42 is that tree, written in numbers. Any question you bring — about love, physics, a problem at work, or the meaning of a dream — is given an exact place on one of those branches by a simple rule anyone can check in a minute. An AI then stands with you at that place and describes the view: what the question rests on, what it is hiding from you, where it is heading, and what might complete it.

The description is precise. What the view means, whether you keep it, and what you do next remain entirely yours.

That is Omega42: one living structure, every question given a place, the reading offered, the judgment left open.

---

This version keeps the mystery and scale, stays free of technical language, protects residual sovereignty, and invites playful exploration — exactly the tone required for First Contact.
