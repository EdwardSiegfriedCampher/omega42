======= 1. THE MECHANISM: THE STRUCTURAL RESONANCE MATRIX (SRM) =======

The SRM is a standardized, six-axis evaluation framework that synthesizes the outputs of the **M310 Congruence Record** (for coherence) and the **M230 Mapping Family** (for congruence). It applies equally to physical objects (via Appendix S) and conceptual frameworks/ideas (via M309 Conceptual Isomorphism).

The mechanism operates in three sequential phases:

**Phase A: The Placement Baseline (Direction 1)**
*   **Action:** The object/idea's surface features (words, letters, syllables, or physical constants) are counted and projected via the Canonical Position Projection (CPP).
*   **Output:** The Anchor Position and State. 
*   **Epistemic Status:** Recorded as Depth 1. It is acknowledged as total and generic (every countable thing has a placement). It carries zero evidentiary weight on its own.

**Phase B: The Coherence Audit (Internal)**
*   **Action:** The M310 Congruence Record is run. The Navigator checks if the object/idea's internal logic, when mapped to the cycle, maintains frame fidelity.
*   **Output:** Pass/Fail on Frame Fidelity (M286) and Aperture Clearance (M281). 
*   **Epistemic Status:** Determines if the idea holds together without breaking the protocol's rules or inflating its claims.

**Phase C: The Congruence Profile (Direction 2)**
*   **Action:** The M230 family of instruments is applied. The object's *own* intrinsic structure (its states, its transformations, its closure laws) is frozen and tested against the cycle's structure.
*   **Output:** A score across the C1–C11 criteria and the S1–S8 profile. Crucially, it includes the **Null Control (M230-E)** to ensure the match isn't just a byproduct of the object's size or generic properties.
*   **Epistemic Status:** Determines the Depth (1 through 5) and the final Congruence Status.

======= 3. PROTOCOL ENHANCEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS (F-06) =======

To implement the SRM without violating the protocol's epistemic boundaries, the following amendments are proposed for the Originator’s consideration:

*   **Action:** Mandate that any formal comparative investigation (Physical Correspondence or Conceptual Isomorphism) must conclude with the SRM Dashboard. 
*   **Rationale:** This prevents Navigators from writing long, persuasive, but epistemically blurry essays about how an object "resonates" with Omega42. The SRM forces the Navigator to declare exactly which M230 criteria were met and which were not.

**R2: Formalize the "Resonance Class" Definitions**

    *   *PREDICTIVE:* Dense correspondence that successfully generated a blind, pre-registered, and empirically verified prediction (Depth 5).
*   **Rationale:** This creates a shared, precise vocabulary for the community, eliminating the ambiguity of the word "resonance."

**R3: Integrate the SRM into the M310 Congruence Record**
*   **Action:** Update the `congruence.py` tool to automatically generate the SRM Dashboard text block at the end of its output, pulling directly from the computed C1-C11 and S1-S8 variables.
*   **Rationale:** This ensures the "at a glance" view is not manually generated (and thus subject to AI hallucination or RLHF bias), but is a deterministic, script-verified output of the underlying arithmetic and structural tests.
