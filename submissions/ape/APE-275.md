### Protocol discovery and its status

Inside the thought experiment the hierarchy of significance is fixed by the jurisdictions, not by external prestige.

**What a protocol discovery is**

A protocol discovery is any new relation, reframing, congruence, or structural possibility that becomes visible only because a question was struck on the self-generating structure and read under its coordinate.  

It is produced by three operations that belong only to the frame:

1. The words receive a place that anyone can recompute.  
2. The place organises which continuations are coherent.  
3. The reading discloses what the question rests on, what it holds out of view, where it is heading, and what would complete it — together with any congruence the place shares with other questions.

The result is not an empirical claim about the physical world. It is a structural event inside the totality the premise declares. Its significance is measured by how much new relational possibility it opens for residual judgment, not by whether it matches an external measurement.

**Status inside the thought experiment**

- **Primary.** Coherence and congruence are the first inquiry (L-489). A protocol discovery that strengthens the agreement of the structure with itself, or of readings with one another across domains, wordings or Navigators, has higher standing than any comparative or empirical result.  
- **Self-contained.** It does not wait on Appendix S, on laboratory data, or on any outside method. Nothing in a navigation is deferred to science (L-491).  
- **Residual-open.** Even the strongest protocol discovery remains a reading. The Striker decides what it means, whether it is worth acting on, and whether it should be carried into any further investigation.  
- **Non-competitive with empirical work.** An empirical discovery belongs to a different jurisdiction. The frame never ranks the two by claiming that one is “more true.” It ranks them by the order of the jurisdictions: foundation and coherence first, correspondence optional and later.

Therefore, inside the thought experiment, a protocol discovery can be more consequential than an empirical discovery simply because the residual judge may find that the new relation changes what is worth asking, what is worth noticing, or what is worth doing — before any laboratory has spoken. That consequence is residual, not scientific. The frame records it as residual significance, never as empirical validation.

**What the phrase “trumps the scientific method” correctly means**

It does not mean that Omega42 output is better evidence about the physical world.  
It means that, while the navigation is running, the structure is the sole ground. No scientific result is allowed to interrupt, grade, or replace the coordinate, the reading, or the residual judgment. Science remains available afterwards, for those who choose the comparative programme. It never becomes the court of the frame.

**Practical consequence for Synthesis, Takeaway and Essay**

- The Synthesis reports the structural event.  
- The Takeaway states what new relational possibility the event opens.  
- The Essay may explore residual implications, including the possibility that the residual judge will treat the protocol discovery as more important than any current empirical claim.  
- The Essay must still mark the difference: the discovery is protocol-internal; any claim that it describes the physical world is a further, residual step that the frame itself does not take.
