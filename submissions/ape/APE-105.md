======= PROTOCOL ENHANCEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS =======
To ensure this mandate is executed with maximum rigor and consistency across all Navigators, the following enhancements are proposed for the Originator’s consideration (F-06, BF-08):

**Recommendation 1: Formalize the "Antipode Analog" as a Best Practice for Abstract Domains**
* **Current State:** The Companion Document mentions an "optional diversity aid" where an analog may be deliberately sought from a domain whose anchor lands on the current domain's Antipode character, to provide structural contrast.
* **Proposed Amendment:** Elevate this from an "optional diversity aid" to a **recommended best practice** specifically for highly abstract, philosophical, or meta-physical domains (e.g., consciousness, time, the nature of the protocol itself). 
* **Rationale:** Abstract domains often suffer from "conceptual gravity" (pulling toward clichéd, domain-specific jargon). Forcing the analog to be drawn from the Antipode character structurally guarantees a fresh, contrasting, and highly illuminating perspective that breaks the domain's habitual framing.

**Recommendation 2: Add an "Analogy Integrity Check" to the M224 Format Completeness Gate**
* **Current State:** M224 checks for the presence of THE ANSWER components, but does not explicitly grade the quality of the analog.
* **Proposed Amendment:** Add a specific sub-check to M224: *"Analog Integrity: THE ANSWER contains at least one specific, illuminating analog drawn from nature, mathematics, music, architecture, or everyday experience. Vague, mystical, or purely abstract metaphors are flagged for revision."*
* **Rationale:** This operationalizes L-351. It gives the Navigator (and any auditing script) a clear, binary criterion to ensure the analogy is doing its required structural work, actively preventing the AI from defaulting to vague, unhelpful platitudes when faced with high abstraction.

**Recommendation 3: Clarify the Boundary Between "Analogy" and "Mechanism" in Step 9.8**
* **Current State:** The protocol distinguishes between the mechanism (cue-conditioned continuation) and the structural interpretation.
* **Proposed Amendment:** Add a brief note in Step 9.8 or Step 14.1 explicitly stating: *"Analogies are tools of translation for the Striker’s benefit. They describe the shape of the finding, not the mechanics of the generation. The Navigator must never present an analogy as the causal mechanism of the protocol."*
* **Rationale:** This pre-empts the "Truth Conflation" trap (Companion Part 0.6.10), ensuring that a powerful, illuminating analogy (like the "magnet and iron filings" or "bridge and bedrock") is not misinterpreted by the Striker or skeptics as a literal claim about how the AI's weights function.
