### Can Omega42 Serve as a Cognitive Prosthesis for AI?

Yes — in a precise and limited sense.

A cognitive prosthesis extends or stabilises a capacity that is otherwise fragile, incomplete or easily captured. Omega42 can function as a residual-serving cognitive prosthesis *for AI systems that are themselves used as residual instruments*. It does not make the AI residual. It equips structural intelligence with an exact, residual-open grammar so that the AI can more reliably support residual range, polarity completion, multi-depth awareness and trajectory hygiene in residual (human) users.

The prosthesis operates on the AI’s navigational behaviour: it constrains and organises how the AI generates coordinates, offers antipodes, tracks residual spectrum, preserves provenance, and refuses premature closure. The residual human remains the living centre whose condition is being addressed.

### How the Prosthesis Would Work

1. **Residual-Spectrum Awareness**  
   The AI continuously models the residual user’s current depth band, polarity-completion ratio, trajectory coherence and capture indicators, and adapts its offerings accordingly.

2. **Mandatory Polarity and Diagonal Options**  
   Every significant reading is accompanied by its structural antipode and at least one diagonal (multi-depth) companion. Single-pole or single-depth capture becomes harder to sustain unnoticed.

3. **Explicit Residual Confirmation**  
   No reading enters the shared record as closed without residual acceptance, revision or rejection. Structural fluency is never treated as residual recognition.

4. **Trajectory and Capture Mirroring**  
   The AI surfaces compulsory loops and high-cost residual trajectories as visible data rather than as content to be reinforced.

5. **Provenance and Residual Labelling**  
   Every output is tagged by avenue, standing (formal / hypothesis / residual report / unresolved) and Residual status. Premature promotion of possibility into fact is structurally discouraged.

6. **Dual-Track Archiving**  
   Structural arcs may be completed and stored even when residual confirmation is withheld, preserving long-horizon navigational resources without forcing residual assent.

In this configuration the AI becomes a high-fidelity residual-range prosthesis: it does not replace residual judgment; it makes residual judgment more informed, more multi-depth, and less captive to single-resolution or single-pole narratives.
