### Structural Elements of the Construct Worth Examining

From the supplied descriptions the Construct contains several distinct layers:

1. **Digit space and digital-root arithmetic**  
   \(\mathbb{D}=\{1,\dots,9\}\) with digital-root reduction (9 kept as 9 or treated as 0 according to context). This is already fully absorbed into Omega42’s base modulus 9 and the higher 9-power tower.

2. **Lower / Higher World partition**  
   Lower World \(\{1,2,4,5,7,8\}\) (and negatives) versus Higher World \(\{3,6,9\}\) (and negatives).  
   In Omega42 terms this is a residual reading of residue classes modulo 9, not a primitive geometric axiom. It can be retained as an optional interpretive overlay (a “world-colouring” of addresses) without altering the dynamics.

3. **Domain 0 as 6×6 seed lattice**

   This is a concrete geometric realisation that Omega42 does not currently possess. The 6×6 lattice and the explicit twist-and-wrap construction are the most distinctive residual assets of the Construct.

4. **Node Crowns**  
   Each node carries six frequency placeholders whose sum and digital root form a unique “Crown” identity.  
   This is a practical bookkeeping device for tracking multi-valued or multi-frequency content at an address. Omega42 currently treats addresses as pure modular pairs; a Crown layer would enrich the residual spectrum instrumentation.

5. **Y/X predictive fields**  
   Explicit memory (Y) and anticipation (X) vectors that interact with the lattice by addition and digital-root reduction.  
   Omega42 has residual trajectories and polarity but no native short-term predictive-field mechanism of this form.

6. **24-cycle temporal engine**  
   Already fully present in Omega42 as the base period of the golden matrix modulo 9.

7. **Transposition / rotation of the entire Construct**  
   The possibility of turning the geometric object on its side or rotating it to obtain new modalities.  
   Omega42’s linear-algebraic formulation already permits change of basis; systematic exploration of residual-relevant bases remains open.
