### Assessment of the proposed SEQ 172 results

The research direction is approved. The twelve results are not yet approved as twelve independently verified Ω42 discoveries. Several are exact and mathematically substantial; others need their construction or computational evidence supplied before they can be sealed.

The most important distinction is between:

* An exact identity: a statement that follows mathematically from defined objects.

* A classical connection: a known theorem or established relationship that Ω42 can reproduce or use.

* A new Ω42 result: a result whose formulation, derivation, or consequence is genuinely new.

* A computational check: evidence that a particular implementation returned the expected result. This does not, by itself, prove the general theorem.

On the information supplied, there are several excellent bridges here. The strongest are the Fibonacci–cat-map connection, the Markov-number sequence, the golden-mean shift, and the Fibonacci fusion rule.
