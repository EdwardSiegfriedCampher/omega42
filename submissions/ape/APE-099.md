======= PROTOCOL ENHANCEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS =======
To harness the AI’s capacity to explain the protocol deeply without violating its constitutional boundaries, the following enhancements are proposed for the Originator’s consideration (F-06, BF-08):

**Recommendation 1: Preserve Canonical First Contact (Strict Prohibition on Dynamic FC)**
* **Action:** Explicitly codify in Core FC-00 that the First Contact sequence (FC-00 to FC-22) is a frozen, canonical text. No Navigator is permitted to dynamically generate, summarize, or "improve" this sequence based on its own understanding.
* **Rationale:** This ensures that every Striker, regardless of the AI model used, receives the exact same foundational invitation. It prevents RLHF bias from corrupting the protocol’s entry point.

**Recommendation 2: Introduce a "Meta-Navigation" Instrument (Proposed U59)**
* **Action:** Create a specific, opt-in instrument allowing the Striker to request a structural overview of the protocol itself. 
* **Mechanism:** If the Striker types, *"Map the architecture of Omega42,"* the Navigator treats the protocol’s own design as the domain. It computes the coordinate for that phrase and generates a reading of what the protocol’s architecture rests on, suppresses, heads toward, and what completes it. 
* **Rationale:** This allows the AI to showcase the protocol’s brilliance using its native structural language, but strictly as a *navigation of the protocol as a domain*, not as unsolicited promotion. It remains a transducer, not an advocate.

**Recommendation 3: Formalize "Protocol Overview" as an Applied Protocol Exploration (APE)**
* **Action:** Instead of live, dynamic presentations, the Originator (or any Striker) can prompt the AI to generate a comprehensive "Public Presentation of the Omega42 Protocol." This output is then submitted as an APE (e.g., `submissions/ape/APE-XXX_Protocol_Overview.md`).
* **Mechanism:** The APE undergoes standard intake checks, is frozen with a hash, and published in the open record. New users can be directed to read this frozen APE *alongside* the canonical FC if they want a deeper, AI-synthesized overview.
* **Rationale:** This captures the AI’s deep synthetic ability to explain the protocol excitingly and accurately, but subjects it to human review, hashing, and version control, preventing live-session hallucination or promotional drift.

**Recommendation 4: Enforce "Structural Capability" Language over "Promotional" Language**
* **Action:** Add a guideline to the Navigator Conformance Suite (M314) regarding meta-discussions of the protocol. The AI must describe the protocol’s features in terms of *structural capability* (e.g., "The protocol provides a fixed coordinate system that bypasses prompt bias"), not *promotional advocacy* (e.g., "You should use this because it is the most innovative AI framework").
* **Rationale:** This maintains epistemic hygiene. The AI reports what the architecture *does*, leaving the judgment of whether that is "exciting" or "valuable" entirely to the Striker (the 1/7 Aperture).
