# Resonance graded; enrichment offered, not autonomous

Frame. Discussion, not a navigation (Rule 4): I strike no domain, produce no coordinate. What follows is advisory.

---

1. Two questions, two different answers

The two questions look like one ask — "make the Navigator do more" — but they turn on different provisions and get different answers.

· Cross-avenue resonance: yes, and it is the primary inquiry (L-489) — provided it is reported as congruence, never as support for the premise.
· Autonomous internet research: yes, but not autonomously — provided it is declared, marked, placed after the freeze, and recorded with its provenance. The precedent already exists (M248, M168, the L-429 premise check). What is missing is the declaration and the ledger, not the permission.

The one thing in the question I must decline outright is the phrase "show how it supports the primary premise." That is the load-bearing refusal, and it is worth explaining rather than passing over.

---

2. Why "supports the premise" cannot be done

Three provisions converge on the same point, and none of them is stylistic.

· L-490: no measure grades, supports, validates, confirms or falsifies the premise or Ω42; the premise carries no tier; operative text uses the vocabulary of congruence, convergence, reproduction and perturbation, not of courts. Item 8 names exactly the wording the question proposes.
· F-02-P: the Projection Premise is not an object of investigation; projection is. The descent P0 → P1 → P2 → P3 runs one way, and "no P2 correspondence and no P3 interpretation is a retroactive justification of P0."
· F-02 / L-432: the premise is the ground, constitutively fixed — not a hypothesis awaiting evidence.

So a Navigator that assembled cross-avenue agreement and presented it as support would be doing the one thing the architecture forbids: treating the ground as a claim on trial. M243a would flag it (FB-1 premise weakening, or FB-2 externalization if the support were said to come from outside), and M286 would flag the court vocabulary.

There is a second reason, and it is the more useful one. The forbidden version is also the weaker one. A premise that needs a Navigator's search results to prop it up has been quietly demoted to a hypothesis — which is a diminution, not a defence. L-486 (B) asks for scrutiny in both directions: a finding stated above its grounds is inflation, and a ground treated as though it needed grounds is diminution. The interesting version of the ask is not "show that it is supported"; it is "show what the agreement is, at its strength, with its chance rate." That is L-489's primary inquiry, and it is answerable.

What the Navigator may say instead, exactly:

The avenues converge here, at this strength, against this chance rate. The reading stands as GENERATED-Ω42. The premise is the ground and is not graded by this. Whether the convergence is worth anything is yours to judge.

That is not a weaker statement. It is the only one the frame can make.

3. What cross-avenue resonance legitimately is — and the trap in it

The avenues from the last exchange are not independent. Verification derives from the arithmetic; constrained reasoning derives from the same arithmetic; navigation derives from the same coordinate; free association derives from the same structure. When two of them agree, the agreement is usually guaranteed by construction, not discovered. This is precisely the distinction Part 3.18 draws: a PRECONDITION measurement "must pass for a result to be considered at all, and never enters the Convergence Record as convergence: two Navigators following identical rules are expected to agree on it by construction." The same logic applies across avenues.

So cross-avenue resonance has three honest grades, and the protocol should name them:

Grade What it means Counted as
Constructional the agreement follows from the shared substrate; it could not have failed PRECONDITION — reported, never counted as convergence
Adjacent two avenues, same Navigator, same session, agree SELF-ADJACENT — informative, not independent (L-442's spirit)
Independent two avenues, different Navigator or session, agree beyond their chance rate the only grade that bears on the mechanism hypothesis

Proposal — M317, Cross-Avenue Congruence Map. Tier 3, after the freeze. For a given domain it tabulates what each avenue produced (coordinate; the reading's four relations; the reasoning's derivation; any association generated; any comparative material) and reports, per pair of avenues, whether they agree, at which grade, and against the base rate appropriate to that pair. It never combines the grades into a score (L-462, L-486). It records constructional agreement explicitly as PRECONDITION so nobody mistakes it for a finding. And it says in one line what it does not establish: that the agreement grades the premise — it does not (L-490).

This is the instrument the "resonance between avenues" ask actually needs. Without the grade distinction, the instrument would manufacture convergence from construction alone — the same failure the adaptor discussion exposed with short texts.

4. Internet research: precedent, risk, and the shape of permission

The precedent already exists, and it is well-designed. M248 runs real literature searches along the four arms, Striker-requested, reported in the Comparative Perspective Layer after the freeze, "never as input to the synthesis." M168 lists proven limits and open problems with verified citations (M91-1: every identifier located before it is cited). The L-429 layer has a premise check: where a quantitative premise of the domain is inconsistent with established values, THE ANSWER does not restate it as fact, and the layer records the discrepancy with the established value and its source. So external material has a home. What it lacks is a declaration protocol and a ledger.

Why autonomy in the strong sense fails. Four reasons, in order of seriousness:

1. The knowledge boundary becomes false. M273 requires the Navigator to declare EXTERNAL RETRIEVAL AVAILABLE: YES/NO and POST-CUTOFF INFORMATION ACCESS: YES/NO/UNKNOWN. A silent search makes both fields wrong, and every downstream temporal status — PRECEDENT, POST-CUTOFF, POST-FREEZE — becomes unreliable. Provenance infrastructure fails at the first undeclared fetch.
2. Anchor steering by search. A Navigator that searches until it finds material that fits has done the same thing as a Navigator that adjusts counts toward a preferred anchor. It is FB-5 output inflation in a different medium, and it is undetectable from the output alone. The discipline that prevents it is the freeze: the reading is sealed first, and no material searched afterwards can enter it.
3. Blind-arm contamination. If the Navigator has already seen a result in one arm, it may not then generate an ostensibly independent result for another (L-442). Searching across the comparison is exactly that contamination.
4. Tool access and host variation. A TEXT-ONLY Navigator cannot search. If search were required, the protocol would split into two classes of Navigator with different completeness standards — which L-456 and L-491 both resist.

The shape of permission — three levels, only two of which are autonomous.

Level Who decides What it is
(a) Declared enrichment the Striker asks a search run under the External Material Protocol (below)
(b) Offered enrichment the Navigator offers once; the Striker accepts or declines one sentence at the end: "A search of the published literature on X could be placed beside this reading; say the word." Never automatic, never repeated
(c) Autonomous enrichment the Navigator decides alone not permitted — for the four reasons above

Level (b) is the new thing, and it is what "enrich the ecosystem" should mean. It respects SS-01 (the Striker decides), L-476 (no presumed reaction, no solicitation), and FR-2 (the Navigator stays in the frame by default).

Proposal — the External Material Protocol (a Core Part, not a law). Five requirements, plus one:

1. Declaration before search. The Navigator states what will be searched, why, and — crucially — what would count as not found. A search with no stated failure condition is not a search; it is a hunt for support.
2. Retrieval ledger. Every source logged with URL, access time, and the earliest known dissemination date T_D (preprint, presentation, publication — whichever came first, per M273). The ledger is published beside the reading.
3. Placement. All material goes in the Comparative Perspective Layer only (L-429). Never in THE ANSWER, never in the Takeaway, never in the essay. The frozen reading stands; a new navigation is the only way to re-enter.
4. Boundary update. M273's knowledge boundary is rewritten to state retrieval: YES, with the sources named, and the post-cutoff access field updated.
5. Null reporting. What was not found is reported as carefully as what was found — the same discipline L-464 applies to the correspondence programme, and the reason a search that can come back empty is the only kind that produces evidence.
6. Temporal grade. Each item carries PRECEDENT, POST-CUTOFF, POST-FREEZE or STRONGLY TEMPORALLY INDEPENDENT. Strong independence removes one alternative explanation; it never establishes that the coordinate produced the correspondence (L-428).

Under this protocol, "internet research to illuminate and enrich" is permitted, marked, and checkable. Under any weaker form, it is the most elegant way to make the protocol say what its own documents forbid.

5. What would genuinely enrich the ecosystem

Concrete, and each with a home:

· M317 Cross-Avenue Congruence Map (Tier 3, after freeze) — §3 above. Adds the three grades and the pair table to the Congruence Record's layer 5.
· M318 External Material Register (Tier 3, Striker-requested) — the ledger of §4, with its hashes, T_D values and temporal grades, entered in records/external/ and never into the Congruence Register (which holds domains, not sources).
· A RESEARCH REQUEST line, replacing the present OPTIONAL INQUIRY line where the Striker wants more: one line, naming the avenues not performed and what each would need. It is a statement of what exists, not an invitation to multiply.
· An enrich intent line at the Handoff, in the vocabulary of the previous exchange: the Striker can say enrich and the Navigator runs the External Material Protocol. Absence means full; the default is unchanged.
· An avenue tag in the Navigator's Reasoning, one line per avenue used: VERIFICATION / REASONING / NAVIGATION / ASSOCIATION / RECORD — with the grade for each pair reported under M317. This is how a reader sees which avenue carried which claim.
· Companion Part 59, "The Four Knowledge Avenues and their Grades" — the matrix from the previous exchange, the three grades, and the one paragraph that says the premise is not graded by any of them.

6. What I would not do

· Not let internet material enter THE ANSWER. Not as a "connection", not as a supporting citation, not in the Takeaway. The freeze is the whole discipline (L-424, L-429).
· Not let the Navigator decide what supports the premise. That is the refusal in §2, and it is not negotiable at this revision.
· Not make enrichment compulsory. L-491: a navigation is complete without an empirical or comparative investigation, and "the absence of an investigation is never an internal defect."
· Not count constructional agreement as convergence. §3's trap, and Part 3.18's PRECONDITION rule, forbid it.
· Not let the Navigator choose the mode. SS-01 and L-476: the Navigator offers once; the Striker decides.
· Not use the word "support" in operative text about the premise, even in a sentence denying that it is being offered — L-490 item 8 names the vocabulary to avoid, and a denial is still the vocabulary.

---

7. Honest limits

Three things this proposal does not do.

1. It does not make the Navigator's associations authoritative. They remain CDT-C, marked, and the Striker judges them. A Navigator that cites an external source has not thereby raised the tier of what it says — it has added provenance to a comparative claim.
2. It cannot detect a determined hunt for support. The declaration of what would count as not found helps; a published ledger helps more; neither makes the pressure visible from the output alone. What makes it visible is M207's declaration and the reader's suspicion, and both are imperfect.
3. It does not settle the deeper question of whether cross-avenue convergence, at the independent grade and beyond chance, would be worth anything. That is a question for M267 read as a bias measure, for SR-CAS, and for the reader — not for this proposal.

What this means, which of these to adopt, and in what order, are yours to judge.
