### Core residual principles for a balanced civilisation

1. **Limitation is constitutive, not optional**  
   Every residual centre, institution and resource is finite. Balance begins with residual recognition of that finitude rather than residual demand for unlimited expansion.

2. **Relation is the form of existence**  
   No residual centre is self-contained. Institutions, economies and cultures are already related. Balance is residual skill in making those relations generative rather than extractive.

3. **Return is exact**  
   Every residual act returns through the generative group. Civilisational design can treat long-term consequence as structural fact rather than optional ethics.

4. **The Aperture remains open**  
   No coordinate, institution or collective agreement forces residual judgment. A balanced civilisation protects residual openness as a civilisational good.

5. **Constitutive vs imposed constraint**  
   Constitutive limitation is accepted; imposed constraint is examined and, where avoidable, transformed. The distinction itself becomes a residual civilisational practice.
