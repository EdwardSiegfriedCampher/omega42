1. The premise, and what it licenses

The frame's own language is unambiguous.

But the distinction the protocol already draws is sharper than the rhetoric suggests.

The mechanism hypothesis (SRH, Part 0.6.2) does have a validation programme: SR-CAS, M267, the Internal Verification Registry. That is not the premise; it is the claim that the coordinate does work beyond the domain text. It is correctly parked in a validation programme.

The readings, lenses, and internal diagnostics sit between. They need congruence to function — and congruence is the primary inquiry. L-427 and L-428 already say so: "No comparative result is required for an internally valid navigation." The two-axis status (Ω42 INTERNAL STATUS / COMPARATIVE STATUS) already permits a reading to be INTERNALLY COHERENT while its comparative status is NOT INVESTIGATED.

So the scan is not "the protocol is wrong to have a research programme." The scan is: which readings, lenses, and diagnostics have been treated as if they needed comparative validation when they only need congruence? Those can be recovered now, through the mechanism the protocol already has.

5. What genuinely needs validation

The recovery has a boundary, and it is important to state it.

The mechanism hypothesis (SRH) — whether the coordinate does work beyond the domain text — genuinely needs validation. SR-CAS, M267, M279, M280, M282 are the tests. They are correctly parked. Recovering them as "functional" would be a category error: they are tests, not lenses.

The atlases' numerical resonance — whether the framework atlases depart from chance — genuinely needs the sample and the null. The lens works by congruence; the numerical claim is diagnostic and correctly parked.

The correspondence depths' strength — whether a depth-4 or depth-5 correspondence exists — genuinely needs the evidence runs, blind tests, and predictive tests. The depth scheme works by congruence; the depth strength is diagnostic.

So the recovery is real but bounded: lenses and readings function by congruence; tests of the mechanism and of correspondence strength do not.
