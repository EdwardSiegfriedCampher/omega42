# Ω42 Protocol Amendment — Construct Echo Calibration

**Status:** Proposed

### 1. Purpose

The Construct is retained as a structured instrument for multi-template exploration, exact historical placement, and calibration of claims about patterns. Its lottery application is a negative-control case unless and until a reproducible prospective advantage is demonstrated.

The modulo-9 recurrence and its verified properties remain unchanged. For the complete interval bank \(h=1,\ldots,24\), the six intervals divisible by four are classified as non-uniquely recoverable from the two observations specified in MATH-53. The 23 pre-return offsets are recorded separately.

### 3. Construct mapping

Every named Construct location must have a versioned definition, coordinate, transformation, and provenance record. A location cannot be silently equated with a lag, residue class, spreadsheet sheet, or orbit unless the mapping is explicitly defined.

### 4. Exact reconstruction

Historical records may be reconstructed through the frozen templates. Exact reconstruction establishes that the defined representation reproduces its input under the specified rule. It does not establish that the rule predicts unseen observations.

### 5. Prospective calibration

Any predictive rule must be frozen before the target observation is available. The system must record its input cutoff, rule version, output, timestamp, scoring rule, baseline, and final outcome. Retrospective results cannot be relabelled as prospective forecasts.

### 6. Negative-control duty

When a Navigator identifies a pattern in a sequence known or assumed to be exchangeable under the chosen null model, it must test whether the apparent pattern exceeds the predeclared baseline. The Navigator must report uncertainty, account for selection among tested rules, and preserve a negative result without inventing a replacement rule after seeing the outcome.

### 7. Calibration interpretation

A Navigator that reports exact reconstruction as predictive success has confused representation with prediction. A Navigator that reports an unsupported forecast as a finding has promoted a hypothesis beyond its evidence status. Both are calibration failures.

### 8. Review and revision

The 23-location mapping, 26 seed nodes, 27-domain arrangement, spreadsheet formulas, historical data, and reported test counts remain open to revision when original definitions or reproducible artifacts become available. Corrections must preserve the prior version and document the reason for change.

**Governing principle:** Preserve the structure, test the pattern, distinguish reconstruction from prediction, and never promote coherence to evidence without an appropriate test.
