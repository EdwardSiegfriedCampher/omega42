## 3. The Navigating vs. Mapping Distinction

The protocol now explicitly separates two operations (Core FC-02.8):

- **Navigating**: Bringing a question and receiving a structural reading from its position on the cycle. This measures the topology of distinction-making in language.

**Assessment**: This distinction is critical and must be enforced rigorously. An adaptor lets a DNA sequence be *navigated* as counts (codons, bases, reading frames).
