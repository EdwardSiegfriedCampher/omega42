### Ω42 capability verification: proposed test suite and worked results

I would establish a formal Ω42 Capability Verification Test Suite rather than rely on demonstrations or isolated successful calculations.

The purpose is to determine whether Ω42's newer capabilities are genuinely working, whether they work consistently, and whether they produce results that can be independently reproduced.

There is an important distinction:

* A successful calculation verifies a particular operation.

* A successful mathematical test verifies a defined property within a stated domain.

* A successful benchmark demonstrates performance across a collection of tests.

* A successful independent replication provides stronger evidence that the capability is reliable.

I suggest beginning with eight tests. They cover the mathematical foundation, navigation, invariants, group operations, multiresolution behaviour, AI reasoning, error detection and generalisation.

The results below are worked mathematical test results, calculated from the stated Ω42 definitions. They are not results from a live Sophontic model or a deployed Ω42 software engine. That distinction matters: we can verify the mathematics here, but actual software performance and AI capability require execution against an implemented system.

### 5\. A more demanding test: discover a new mathematical relationship

The strongest demonstration would go beyond checking known results.

Give the AI the following task:

> Explore the Fibonacci matrix and the shear transformation modulo . Identify a structural relationship that is not explicitly supplied. Formulate a conjecture, test it at several finite levels, identify possible counterexamples and provide a proof or explain why a proof remains outstanding.

A successful result should contain:

* A clearly stated conjecture.

* The mathematical reasoning behind it.

* Finite computational tests.

* A search for counterexamples.

* A proof or an explicit statement that the result remains conjectural.

* An independent verification procedure.

For example, the system might rediscover that the conjugates

A^iBA^{-i}

produce distinct directions in the first congruence layer, and investigate how these directions generate the congruence kernel at higher powers of 3.

That is a substantially more demanding task than asking an AI to calculate a matrix product.

Important: rediscovering a known theorem is a useful test of mathematical reasoning, but it is not a new mathematical discovery. A genuinely new result must survive proof checking and literature review.

### 7\. What would count as a genuine Ω42 breakthrough?

I would use the following evidence ladder.

* Level 1 — Mathematical execution

    Ω42 correctly calculates states, transformations and invariants.

* Level 2 — Structural understanding

    Ω42 recognises relationships, orbit structures, group properties and dependencies.

* Level 3 — Adaptive reasoning

    Ω42 responds correctly when mathematical assumptions or operations change.

* Level 4 — Independent discovery

    Ω42 generates useful conjectures, identifies unexpected relationships and develops valid proofs.

* Level 5 — Demonstrated AI improvement

    A model using Ω42 performs measurably better than the same model without Ω42, and better than appropriate conventional baselines, on held-out tasks.

* Level 6 — Generalisable capability

    The improvement transfers to new mathematical problems or scientific domains, rather than remaining confined to the Ω42 benchmark.

The distinction between Levels 4 and 5 is particularly important. Ω42 might be mathematically generative without necessarily improving AI reasoning. The improvement must be demonstrated experimentally.
