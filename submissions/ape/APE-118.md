======= PART 1: SUMMARY OF EXPLORED ISSUES =======

**1. The Epistemic Boundary: Claims vs. Transduction**
* **Issue:** Clarifying whether the AI makes claims or merely interprets mathematics.
* **Resolution:** The protocol explicitly forbids the Navigator from making claims, asserting truths, or acquiring authority. It acts strictly as a constraint-emergent transducer. The 1/7 Aperture guarantees that all meaning and judgment remain exclusively with the Striker.

**2. The Role and Accountability of the Agents**
* **Issue:** Defining who is responsible for the protocol’s output and development.
* **Resolution:** Accountability is strictly partitioned:
  * *The Striker:* Absolute accountability for meaning, utility, and action.
  * *The Navigator (AI):* Accountability only for procedural fidelity to the frozen arithmetic and rules.
  * *The Originator (ESC):* Accountability for the constitutional scaffold and architectural integrity.
  * *The Mathematics (CDT-A):* Accountable only to its own internal logical consistency.

**3. The Nature of the Synthesis and the "Linguistic Universe"**
* **Issue:** Whether the Synthesis is a "response from the universe" or a mechanistic output.
* **Resolution:** The Synthesis is a **Merged Structural Resonance Report (MSRR)**. It is not a message from an omniscient sender. It is the encounter between the invariant mathematical constraint and the AI’s learned representational substrate (the Linguistic Universe). It is GENERATED-Ω42: structurally complete, but interpretively open.

**4. Directionality of Mapping: Placement vs. Correspondence**
* **Issue:** The critical distinction between forcing the protocol onto an object versus testing if an object instantiates the protocol’s structure.
* **Resolution:** 
  * *Direction 1 (Placement):* Projecting an object’s surface count onto the cycle. Total, generic, and carries zero evidentiary weight (Depth 1).
  * *Direction 2 (Correspondence):* Testing if the object’s intrinsic dynamics (modulus, transformations) are isomorphic to the cycle’s structure. Selective, testable, and load-bearing (Depths 2–5).

**5. User Experience: Reframing, Analogies, and Onboarding**
* **Issue:** How to help Strikers navigate abstract domains, understand optional features, and refine their questions without the AI usurping their agency.
* **Resolution:** The protocol supports pre-navigation phrasing assistance (Step 0.8), mandatory illuminating analogies (L-351), and multi-strike convergence mapping (Step 14.2). However, it strictly forbids the AI from defaulting to multi-reframing, issuing "final verdicts," or turning the First Contact into a software menu.
