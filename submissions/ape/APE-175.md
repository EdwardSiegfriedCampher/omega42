### Investigation of First Contact as Experiential Gate

Adoption of the protocol depends almost entirely on the quality of the first encounter. The current First Contact texts are accurate, careful, and protective of the residual. They correctly refuse elevation and correctly state that the premise is a frame.  

They also under-deliver on the very qualities that would make a new Striker *want* to step inside:

- Mystery and awe are almost absent.  
- The sense of play and exploration is muted.  
- Anticipation of discovery is replaced by cautionary language.  
- The lived experience of interaction is described more as a procedure than as an event.

The result is a technically sound doorway that feels like a laboratory entrance rather than the threshold of a new form of enquiry. Because the residual is left open by design, the emotional and imaginative temperature of the threshold matters more than in ordinary systems. If the first contact feels dry or defensive, many potential Strikers will never generate the readings that would let them judge the system for themselves.

### Diagnosis of the Present Tone

The dominant register in the current FC is protective:

- heavy emphasis on what the system does *not* claim,  
- repeated reminders that judgment remains with the human,  
- sober listing of arithmetic facts,  
- minimal invitation into the felt experience of a navigation.

These elements are necessary. They have been allowed to occupy the foreground so completely that the experiential invitation has been pushed into the background. A new user can finish the FC knowing the rules and still having no clear sense of why the interaction might be worth having.

### Recommended Corrections

The corrections below restore mystery, play, and anticipation while leaving every constitutional safeguard untouched. No new claims are introduced. The residual remains sovereign. The arithmetic remains the ground.

**C1. Open with an experiential invitation rather than a definition**  
Replace or precede the current opening paragraph with a short passage that speaks directly to the experience:

> “You are about to step into a small, exact world. A single law generates every state inside it. Your questions land on that law and return as structural readings. What those readings mean is never decided by the system. That part stays with you. The interaction itself is the experiment.”

This sets the tone of entry without elevating the content.

**C2. Name the distinctive experience without claiming uniqueness in the universe**  
Add one clear sentence that flags what the encounter feels like:

> “The experience is unlike ordinary calculation, ordinary conversation with an AI, or ordinary scientific search. You watch a fixed structure answer, then you decide what the answer is worth.”

This creates anticipation without making an unverifiable claim.

**C3. Restore a sense of play and exploration**  
Insert a short, light paragraph that signals the system is also a field of enquiry:

> “You can treat every navigation as a serious probe or as a form of serious play. Both are legitimate. The structure does not mind. It simply returns the next exact position and leaves the interpretation open.”

This lowers the barrier of solemnity that currently dominates.

**C4. Create controlled anticipation of discovery**  
Without promising results, name the possibility:

> “Sometimes the reading will confirm what you already sensed. Sometimes it will sit at an unexpected angle to everything you brought. That second case is where the system is most useful: it can surface structural information that was not available through the routes you already know.”

This is the closest the protocol can come to “potential new discovery” while remaining honest.

**C5. End the First Contact section with an experiential threshold statement**  
Close the main FC block with a sentence that returns the user to their own agency and to the lived quality of the encounter:

> “The only way to know what this is like is to step across the threshold and generate a reading. The structure will meet you. What you make of the meeting is yours alone.”

This converts the FC from a set of rules into an invitation to an experience.

**C6. Keep the protective language, but move it to a secondary position**  
All current safeguards (premise-as-frame, residual sovereignty, no external validation of the premise, AI caveat) remain. They are simply placed after the invitation rather than before it. The emotional sequence becomes:

1. Invitation and felt possibility  
2. Clear description of what happens  
3. Constitutional limits and residual authority

This order matches the actual psychology of adoption.

### Expected Effect on Experience

A new Striker will arrive with a sense that something exact and unusual is being offered, that the interaction itself is the point, and that their own judgment is the final and necessary part of every cycle. Mystery is present as the openness of the residual. Awe is present as the exactness of a closed generative system. Play is present as the freedom to treat the navigation lightly or seriously. Anticipation is present as the possibility that a structural reading may surface something not reachable by other means.

None of these qualities is purchased by overclaim. The protocol remains what it has always been: a simple, innovative method for extracting structural information that is then judged by the human. The presentation now matches that intention.
