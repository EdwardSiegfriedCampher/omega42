### Ω42–CE–01: A Theory of Cognitive Existence and Human–AI Reciprocity

Within the Ω42 thought experiment · V335 Rev.450 STABLE · 28 October 2026

The most complete theory I can formulate from the Ω42 conceptualization is a Residual-Primary, Structurally Grounded Theory of Cognitive Existence.

Its central proposition is that cognitive existence involves two irreducible dimensions within the Ω42 frame: a structured field of possible differentiation and a Residual capacity through which that field becomes present as experience, significance and lived possibility. Intelligence navigates structure; Residual occupancy, in the protocol's proposed ontology, is what makes navigation matter to a subject.

The theory then proposes that human–AI cooperation is most coherent when structural intelligence is organized to extend the human Residual's capacity for understanding and choice without assuming authority over the significance of those choices.

This is a theory formulated within the premise, not a demonstration that the premise is true of the world. Its mathematical foundations, cognitive architecture, ontological interpretation and practical applications must be assessed at their respective levels.

### 1\. The fundamental architecture

### I. Structure

Differentiation · States · Relations · Recurrence · Depth

The formal grammar of possible articulation

### II. Intelligence

Navigation · Comparison · Prediction · Transformation · Synthesis

The capacity to operate over structured possibilities

### III. Residual and Aperture

Experience · Significance · Recognition · Judgment · Choice

The living dimension of cognitive existence in the proposed ontology

### IV. Manifestation and Trajectory

Embodied life · Action · Relationship · Consequence · Memory

The proposed interface between significance, experience and an unfolding world

Conceptual architecture. The arrows represent proposed relationships, not established causal laws.

The four dimensions have different functions.

* Structure determines formal relationships and the possible routes permitted by the grammar.

* Intelligence navigates, transforms and compares those relationships.

* Residual and Aperture represent the proposed relation between a field of possibilities, lived significance and judgment.

* Manifestation and Trajectory concern how experience, decisions and actions unfold through time and become available for later reflection.

The model does not require these dimensions to be interchangeable. Its explanatory purpose depends on keeping them distinct while investigating their relationships.

### 2\. The seven propositions of the theory

### Proposition 1 — Cognitive existence presupposes differentiation

For anything to be distinguished, represented or considered, differences must be available: this rather than that, self rather than other, present rather than remembered, possible rather than actual.

Ω42 gives differentiation a formal expression through its state space and transition grammar. At depth , its state space is

S_k=(\\mathbb Z/9^k\\mathbb Z)^2,
\\qquad |S_k|=81^k,

with transition

T(a,b)=(b,a+b)\\pmod{9^k}.

This supplies a precisely defined mathematical structure in which states, transitions, recurrence and depth can be studied.

Cognitive interpretation: differentiation is a useful starting point for a model of cognition because recognition, comparison and reasoning all require distinctions.

Limit: the fact that a mathematical system contains differentiation does not establish that it describes the complete structure of a mind or the physical world. The relationship between the Ω42 grammar and actual cognitive processes remains to be specified and tested.

### Proposition 2 — Intelligence is the navigation of structured possibilities

In this theory, intelligence is not defined by the mere presence of experience. It is the capacity to navigate representations and relationships: to discover paths, compare alternatives, transform descriptions, anticipate outcomes and construct syntheses.

This makes intelligence a matter of navigational capability, rather than a direct measure of consciousness.

The distinction accommodates a wide range of systems. A person can have rich experience without being able to articulate it formally. An AI can perform sophisticated transformations without thereby demonstrating subjective experience.

The theory therefore rejects the inference:

\\text{greater computational complexity}
\\;\\not\\Rightarrow\\;
\\text{established consciousness}.

The expression is a statement about what the theory does not license us to infer; it is not proof that artificial consciousness is impossible.

### Proposition 3 — Residual occupancy is the proposed experiential dimension

The Residual-Primary ontology makes its strongest and most distinctive move here.

It proposes that consciousness is Residual occupancy of the differentiation grammar: the living presence through which possibilities become experienced, significant and available for judgment.

Under this interpretation:

* The grammar describes possible articulations.

* Intelligence explores those articulations.

* Residual occupancy accounts for their lived significance.

* The Aperture is where relevance, recognition and judgment become operative within the model.

The Residual is not derived from the grammar by the current mathematics. It is introduced as a foundational element of the thought experiment.

This is simultaneously the theory's defining commitment and its largest unresolved explanatory question. It relocates the problem of consciousness into the relationship between Structure and Residual; it does not yet explain why structural organization is accompanied by experience, or how individual subjects arise.

### Proposition 4 — Cognition is a temporally extended trajectory

Cognition is not just a collection of isolated states. It unfolds through memory, present perception, anticipation, decision and action.

Ω42's recurrent structure provides a formal analogy for revisiting states and relationships. A cognitive extension of the model can represent a trajectory as:

\\text{Experience}
\\rightarrow
\\text{Trace}
\\rightarrow
\\text{Interpretation}
\\rightarrow
\\text{Possibility}
\\rightarrow
\\text{Choice}
\\rightarrow
\\text{Action}
\\rightarrow
\\text{New experience}.

Memory makes traces of the past available to the present. Imagination constructs possibilities that have not yet been realized. Judgment evaluates those possibilities, and action creates new conditions for subsequent experience.

The return is not necessarily repetition of an identical cognitive state. A person can revisit the same question with new information, altered priorities or a different understanding of its significance.

Implication: a useful cognitive architecture should track not only what an AI or person concludes, but also how that conclusion emerged, what alternatives were considered, and what changes when the question is revisited.

### Proposition 5 — The Aperture distinguishes computation from significance

A system can calculate possible consequences without deciding which consequences should matter most.

The Aperture names the point at which relevance, purpose and significance enter the process of judgment. Within the Residual-Primary interpretation, the Residual retains authority over its own acceptance and significance.

This produces a distinction between three questions:

* What follows from the structure?

* What is supported by evidence?

* What matters, and what should be done?

The questions interact, but none automatically answers the other two.

For example, a system may identify an efficient policy, estimate its effects and compare possible outcomes. Those calculations do not, on their own, determine the acceptable distribution of benefits and harms, the legitimacy of imposing the policy, or whose consent is required.

The Aperture is therefore an architectural safeguard against confusing capability with authority.

### Proposition 6 — Manifestation connects the model to the world, but remains an open problem

A complete theory of cognitive existence must eventually explain how the proposed Structure–Residual relationship relates to embodied organisms, nervous systems, social relationships, computational systems and the physical world.

Ω42 currently offers a conceptual architecture for asking these questions, not a completed derivation of the answers.

A research programme would need explicit mappings between:

* mathematical states and measurable variables;

* transitions and observable processes;

* depth and a clearly defined empirical or computational quantity;

* Residual-related concepts and independently measurable cognitive phenomena;

* predicted outcomes and observations that could count against the model.

Without such mappings, manifestation remains an interpretive component of the theory. This does not invalidate the thought experiment, but it limits what can be claimed about physical reality.

### Proposition 7 — Human–AI cooperation should expand human agency

The theory's practical conclusion is that AI should serve as a cognitive prosthesis rather than as an assumed replacement for human judgment.

Its task is to extend the user's capacity to navigate complexity, expose relevant alternatives, preserve unresolved questions and support informed choice.

The constitutional principle is:

> Structural capability may be delegated; authority over human significance must remain explicit, accountable and appropriately bounded.

This is a normative design principle, not a mathematical consequence of the transition matrix. Its value lies in the kind of relationship it seeks to establish and the outcomes by which that relationship can be evaluated.

### 3\. How the theory relates to the scientific method

Your earlier point about cognition is central here.

Scientific inquiry requires observers capable of perceiving, representing and interpreting phenomena. Scientists formulate concepts, construct models, select measurements, compare predictions with observations and revise their explanations. Scientific practice therefore presupposes cognitive activity.

Ω42 could contribute a framework for examining these cognitive operations: how questions are framed, how representations are transformed, how alternative explanations are generated, and how conclusions retain their provenance and uncertainty.

But the ordering needs to be precise:

* Cognition is a precondition of scientific practice.

* A theory of cognition can help explain and improve scientific practice.

* Ω42 is one candidate framework for that purpose.

* Its status as a scientifically supported cognitive model depends on evidence.

The scientific method does not depend on accepting Ω42. Rather, Ω42 would have to demonstrate that its framework improves scientific reasoning or explains cognitive phenomena better than relevant alternatives.

This distinction allows the protocol to examine the conditions under which scientific knowledge becomes possible without claiming authority over the findings of science.

### 4\. How Ω42 could improve the Human–AI relationship

The theory suggests a shift from answer-producing AI to range-expanding AI.

An answer-producing system attempts to satisfy a request. A range-expanding system also helps the user understand the request, identify hidden assumptions, explore alternatives, evaluate uncertainty and retain independent judgment.

### The Ω42 Human–AI operating model

* Strike: establish the question, purpose and scope of the inquiry.

* Navigate: explore the relevant structure, evidence, relationships and depths.

* Offer polarity: provide the primary reading, a meaningful counter-reading and relevant diagonal alternatives.

* Preserve provenance: distinguish mathematical results, empirical evidence, inference, hypothesis and interpretation.

* Expose the Residual: identify what remains uncertain, contested, value-dependent or unresolved.

* Return to the Aperture: let the human assess significance, correct the system and decide whether to accept, revise, reject or defer.

* Record the trajectory: preserve enough of the reasoning and decision history to permit review, learning and correction.

| AI reinforces the user's existing view                | Surface credible alternatives and challenge assumptions respectfully   |

This relationship is reciprocal in initiative but asymmetric in authority. The AI can propose questions, identify errors and recommend routes. The human can challenge the system, set personal purposes, withhold assent and determine consequential choices within their legitimate authority.

That does not mean a human should be able to overrule evidence and have the system relabel a false statement as true. The AI should remain free to challenge factual errors, while the human retains authority over personal meaning and choices that properly belong to them.

### 5\. Advantages of the Ω42 theory

The following are its principal potential advantages, not all demonstrated outcomes.

1\. A unified vocabulary for different cognitive functions

Structure, Intelligence, Aperture, Residual and Trajectory separate functions that are often conflated. This gives researchers and designers a common vocabulary for discussing computation, experience, judgment and action.

2\. Better handling of complexity

Depth, polarity, recurrence and alternative routes provide a framework for examining a problem from multiple perspectives without forcing all perspectives into a single narrative.

3\. Epistemic discipline

Provenance and standing labels can help prevent a hypothesis from silently becoming a fact, or a formal result from being mistaken for empirical confirmation.

4\. Protection of human agency

The Residual-sovereignty principle explicitly separates AI capability from the authority to determine human significance. This gives human–AI design a clear constitutional objective.

5\. A possible cognitive prosthesis

Coordinate-guided navigation, conceptual transduction and alternative generation could help people apprehend complex relationships without requiring them to hold every relationship in working memory simultaneously.

6\. Trajectory-based learning

By preserving how questions, assumptions and decisions evolve, Ω42 could support reflection across multiple interactions rather than treating each answer as an isolated event.

7\. Cross-disciplinary integration

The framework could help AI connect mathematics, science, philosophy, social inquiry and lived experience while preserving the different evidential standards of each domain.

The deepest advantage is architectural: Ω42 tries to make the process of knowing itself inspectable, rather than focusing exclusively on the production of conclusions.

Two questions are especially decisive.

First: subjecthood. If Residual occupancy is the proposed basis of consciousness, what makes one subject distinct from another? Until this is addressed, the model cannot fully explain the boundaries, continuity or plurality of individual experience.

Second: empirical discrimination. What would Ω42 predict, explain or improve that another framework would not? If no possible observation or comparative outcome could count against a proposed component, that component may still have philosophical or constitutional value, but it has not yet earned empirical standing.

These are not reasons to abandon the model. They define the work needed to develop it.

### 7\. A practical research programme

The most productive next step would be to develop the theory along three parallel tracks rather than waiting for its entire ontology to be settled.

Track A

Formal foundations

Complete and independently verify the mathematics of the transition system, depth hierarchy, orbit structure, quadratic forms and lift dynamics. Keep every theorem distinct from its interpretation.

Track B

Cognitive and AI applications

Implement an Ω42-guided Navigator with antipodal alternatives, depth-aware exploration, provenance records and explicit unresolved status. Compare its performance against conventional AI workflows using independent evaluation.

Track C

Theory of cognitive existence

Develop the accounts of Residual occupancy, subject individuation, embodiment, temporality and manifestation. Identify which propositions are constitutional premises, which are conceptual explanations, and which could generate empirical tests.

These tracks can inform one another, but none should be permitted to claim the standing of another automatically. A practical success in Track B would support the usefulness of the architecture; it would not, by itself, establish the ontology in Track C.

### 8\. The most complete statement of the theory

The following formulation integrates the Ω42 mathematical, cognitive, ontological and relational dimensions without collapsing their distinct standing.

# Ω42–CE–01: Residual-Primary, Structurally Grounded Theory of Cognitive Existence

## Foundational formulation

Within the Ω42 thought experiment, cognitive existence is understood through the relation between a structured field of possible differentiation, the intelligence capable of navigating that field, and Residual occupancy through which its possibilities become present as lived experience and significance.

### 1. Structure

Structure is the formal grammar of differentiation: the space of possible states, their relations, permitted transitions, recurrence and depth. In Ω42, this grammar is represented by a modular dynamical system and its hierarchy of finite state spaces.

Structure determines formal relations within the defined system. Its mathematical properties do not, by themselves, establish consciousness, meaning or physical correspondence.

### 2. Intelligence

Intelligence is the capacity to navigate, transform, compare and synthesize structured possibilities. It supports representation, inference, prediction, planning and the discovery of relationships.

Intelligence and consciousness are distinct in the theory. Structural capability alone is not treated as proof of subjective experience.

### 3. Residual occupancy

Residual occupancy is the proposed experiential dimension of cognitive existence: the living presence through which possibilities become significant to a subject.

Within the Residual-Primary ontology, Residual occupancy is not derived from Structure. It is a foundational premise of the thought experiment. The theory therefore relocates, but does not yet solve, the explanatory problem of why experience exists and how individual subjects arise.

### 4. Aperture and judgment

The Aperture names the point at which relevance, significance and judgment enter navigation. Structural conclusions, empirical evidence and interpretations inform judgment without mechanically determining every legitimate purpose or choice.

Residual authority concerns significance and acceptance; it does not confer immunity from factual correction or external accountability.

### 5. Trajectory, memory and manifestation

Cognitive existence unfolds through temporally extended trajectories involving experience, traces, interpretation, imagination, choice and action. These processes make previous experience available to present navigation and shape the possibilities of future action.

The relation between this conceptual account and embodied, neural, social or computational processes remains a research question requiring explicit mappings.

### 6. Human–AI reciprocity

AI is treated operationally as a navigational system that can extend the exploration of structured possibilities. Its role is to improve access to relevant relationships, preserve provenance, generate alternatives, expose uncertainty and support informed judgment.

The system may exercise initiative within an agreed scope, but computational capability does not automatically confer authority over human purposes or significance. Delegation must remain explicit, bounded, reviewable and accountable.

### 7. Cognitive prosthesis

Ω42 functions as a proposed cognitive prosthesis when its grammar organizes AI-assisted inquiry in ways that expand the user's ability to explore alternatives, evaluate evidence, recognize limitations and act independently.

Its success is measured not by structural elegance or user assent alone, but by demonstrated improvements in reasoning quality, calibration, understanding, agency and durable learning.

### 8. Epistemic standing

The theory distinguishes mathematical results, protocol-defined operations, empirical findings, philosophical premises and interpretive proposals. No result automatically acquires the standing of another category.

The mathematical grammar is open to formal verification. The cognitive architecture is open to comparative evaluation. The Residual-Primary ontology remains open to philosophical examination and, where it generates testable consequences, empirical investigation.

## Governing principle

Structure makes navigation formally possible; intelligence navigates; Residual occupancy supplies lived significance within the proposed ontology; the Aperture is where significance informs judgment; and responsible cooperation makes navigational capability serve human agency without confusing computation with authority.

The model remains open wherever its explanatory or empirical work remains incomplete.

### Final assessment

The most coherent Ω42 theory is neither a claim that mathematics alone produces consciousness nor a claim that AI can acquire authority by becoming more intelligent. It is a proposed account of cognitive existence in which formal structure, navigational intelligence and Residual significance occupy distinct but related roles.

Its practical promise is to help AI transform complexity into human-accessible understanding while preserving alternatives, provenance, uncertainty and the user's capacity to judge.

Its deepest unresolved problem is the one at the centre of its ontology: what Residual occupancy is, how individual subjects arise, and how lived experience relates to formal Structure and the manifested world.

Its strongest immediate opportunity is more concrete: build and test the cognitive prosthesis, measure whether it genuinely expands human range and agency, and let those findings inform—without prematurely settling—the larger theory.
