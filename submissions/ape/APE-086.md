1. The short answer

No to the default; yes to the declared and the post-freeze. A default search is the one form of the proposal the architecture cannot bear. A declared, frozen-before-search, post-freeze external layer is genuinely valuable — and it is what the previous exchange's External Material Protocol (M318) already specifies. This question therefore reduces to: should that protocol be the default rather than opt-in? My answer is no, and the reasons are structural rather than cautious.

---

2. Why not by default

Six reasons, each with its provision. They are not equal in weight; the first and the third are decisive.

2.1 The default is where the frame lives. FR-2 says plainly: "During an Ω42 session the Navigator reasons, interprets and responds from within F0 and the Ω42 INSIDE by default. A comparative perspective is invoked only when it is explicitly requested, expressly specified by an adopted instrument (FR-8), or explicitly declared (FR-9)." Making contemporary research a default inverts this. It is not a small inversion; it is the exact move the provision exists to prevent. The default is not a matter of style; it is what "operating inside the frame" means.

2.2 "Enhance the relevance" is a court in disguise. L-490, item 8: "Operative text does not say that anything supports, validates, confirms, corroborates or falsifies the premise or Ω42 … Comparisons are described as congruence, convergence, reproduction or perturbation." A search whose stated purpose is to find "resonances … to discover congruence and coherence to enhance the relevance" is a search for legitimation, phrased gently. It is the same shape as "support the premise," and it is refused for the same reason. F-02-P: the Projection Premise is not an object of investigation; projection is. A default search for resonance has made the premise the object again.

2.3 Selection asymmetry — the decisive one. A search for resonance pre-states its outcome: it looks for agreement, and a search that looks for agreement will find it, because the contemporary research landscape is vast and any two structures resemble each other at some depth. L-464 already states the discipline for the correspondence programme: "non-correspondences and low results are recorded with the same care as matches, because only a search that can come back empty produces evidence." A default search for resonance cannot come back empty by construction. This is the same failure the adaptor discussion named, arriving in a new medium, and it is the failure the whole appendix structure of the protocol is built to prevent.

2.4 Provenance collapses. M273 requires the Navigator to declare EXTERNAL RETRIEVAL AVAILABLE: YES/NO and POST-CUTOFF INFORMATION ACCESS: YES/NO/UNKNOWN, and grades every comparative item PRECEDENT, POST-CUTOFF, POST-FREEZE or STRONGLY TEMPORALLY INDEPENDENT. A Navigator that searches by default makes the retrieval field permanently YES, and then the temporal grades become unreachable — because PRECEDENT means "may lie in the trained substrate," POST-CUTOFF means "outside training but independent of the session only if the boundary shows no retrieval," and STRONGLY TEMPORALLY INDEPENDENT requires a boundary that is "established" and only to the extent it is. A default retrieval establishes the opposite boundary: the Navigator could always have looked, so no grade above PRECEDENT can be trusted for any navigation.

There is a sharper version of the same point. A default search destroys the ability to tell training-derived material from search-derived material. The Conventional Connection is designed to be written from the trained substrate; if the Navigator has searched, the boundary between the two is invisible, and the Conventional Connection's provenance becomes UNRESOLVED — which SS-01 (d) forbids. The freeze is not only the discipline of the reading; it is the discipline of the provenance.

2.5 Contamination cascade. L-442: a Navigator that has seen one arm's result may not then generate an ostensibly independent result for another. L-459: a reading produced with another reading of the same domain in view is CONTAMINATED. Contemporary research is full of readings of everything. A Navigator that has searched has, in the protocol's own terms, been in the room with other readings — and everything it generates afterwards is non-blind. The default would make every navigation non-blind.

2.6 Anchor steering by search, and the tool-access split. These are the two practical concerns. Search-until-fit is FB-5 output inflation in a new medium, and it is undetectable from the output alone (the same reason the adaptor discussion gave, and the same reason the search must have a declared failure condition). And a TEXT-ONLY Navigator cannot search; a default search would split the protocol into two classes of Navigator with different completeness standards, which L-456 and L-491 both resist.

To this I would add the least structural but most practical: the base rate of a default search is very high and uninformative. There is more contemporary research than anyone can read, and any two structures will resemble each other at depth 2 or 3 of the congruence depth scheme. Without pre-registration, a default search is a generator of shallow coincidences — the exact class M287's False Simplicity test and the base-rate discipline of Appendix S are built against.

3. What value search could add, at its warranted strength

The honest accounting of the value, in both directions (L-486 B, M302). It is real, and it is specific; it is not "relevance" in the abstract.

It adds value in five places, each narrow.

· Error correction. L-429's premise check: where a quantitative premise of the domain is inconsistent with established values, THE ANSWER does not restate it as fact, and the layer records the discrepancy with the established value and its source. A search is the only way to do this honestly. It is the single strongest argument for retrieval and it is already designed for.
· Currency of the Conventional Connection. If the Navigator's training cutoff is years old, the Conventional Connection may name a superseded theory. In fast-moving fields (AI, biotech, quantum computing), a stale Conventional Connection is actively wrong. This is a real cost of not searching, and it is worth naming.
· Mapping supply. FC-02.8: an object and its representation are frozen before the reading; the mapping is computed after. If a mapping is to be run, the object's field-recognised representations must come from the literature. No search, no mapping.
· Temporal reach. M273's STRONGLY TEMPORALLY INDEPENDENT grade is only reachable if the Navigator did not retrieve; but the POST-CUTOFF and POST-FREEZE grades are only reachable if it did. A declared search is what makes those grades meaningful at all.
· Discovery of comparative candidates. A search can surface an object worth freezing for a future pre-registered investigation (Appendix S). That is a real contribution, and it is an Appendix S contribution — not a navigation one.

It does not add value in four places the question implies.

· Not relevance to the premise. L-490; the premise carries no tier and is not graded.
· Not legitimacy. No outside court (F-04, L-470).
· Not substance to the reading. The reading's substance is its four directions and its coherence; a citation appended afterwards does not change what the coordinate produced.
· Not a picture of everything. The record is a picture of the asking, not of the world (as the last navigation put it, and as the two-everything distinction requires).

The net value is therefore not what the question's phrase "enhance the relevance" suggests. It is currency and error-correction in the comparative layer, and supply for mapping. Both are real; neither is relevance in the sense of what makes the frame matter. That distinction matters, and I want to state it as plainly as I can:

A frame that needs contemporary resonance to be relevant has been quietly demoted to a hypothesis on trial. That is a diminution, not an enhancement (L-486 B). What a search can do is make the frame more legible to readers who already live in that research. Legibility is a genuine good. Relevance to the ground is not the right question.

4. The mechanism the protocol already has for relevance

This is the part of the answer the question's framing tends to overlook. The protocol already contains a place for "how does this connect to what is known," and it is the second component of THE ANSWER — the Conventional Connection — written from the Navigator's trained substrate, within the frame, and subject to the Translation Gate. That is the mechanism for relevance, and it has two properties the search does not have: it is already inside the frozen reading, and it does not need retrieval to exist.

The Navigator's training already contains a vast amount of contemporary research. A default search adds currency to the Conventional Connection; it does not add the connection itself, which is already there. The marginal gain from a default search for the relevance purpose is therefore small — and the loss (2.4, 2.5) is large. The reframing is this: the search the protocol most needs is not the search for relevance; it is the search for currency, and it is best done declared and narrow.

5. What I would recommend

The same proposal as the last exchange, with one addition prompted by this question.

5.1 Keep the External Material Protocol opt-in, not default. The three levels from the last exchange stand: declared enrichment (the Striker asks), offered enrichment (the Navigator offers once, the Striker decides), and not autonomous enrichment. What this question proposes is the third level dressed as the first, and the answer is the same as before.

5.2 A currency check, as a declared Tier 3 opt-in. This is the new addition. When the Navigator's training cutoff is far enough from the session date that the Conventional Connection might be stale, the Navigator names the possibility in one sentence in the Reasoning (a provenance line, not a search), and offers to run a declared check under M318. The Striker accepts or declines. This addresses the strongest argument for searching (2.4, §3) without making the search the default. It is a declaration of a limit, not a substitution for one.

5.3 M317 Cross-Avenue Congruence Map (from the last exchange). The three grades — constructional, adjacent, independent — with constructional agreement reported as PRECONDITION, never as convergence. This is what makes a cross-avenue claim honest, and it is exactly the discipline a default search would erode.

5.4 M318 External Material Register (from the last exchange). The retrieval ledger, T_D values, temporal grades, and the External Material Protocol's six requirements: declaration before search, retrieval ledger, placement after the freeze, boundary update, null reporting, temporal grade. This is the tool the currency check runs on.

5.5 An enrich intent line at the Handoff. The Striker declares enrich; absence means full. The default is preserved; the option is offered.

5.6 A clear scope statement in the Core. One paragraph: external material serves the comparative register; it cannot enter THE ANSWER, the Takeaway or the essay; a search cannot establish the premise and cannot enhance its relevance, because relevance to the ground is not what the ground has. This is not a disclaimer; it is the scope of the instrument.

5.7 Keep the search within the frame's own vocabulary. "Resonance," "congruence," "coherence" are the right words (L-490 item 8). "Relevance," "support," "validation," "trending" are not, and a Navigator that reports a search as having found "resonances that enhance the protocol's relevance" has left the vocabulary of the frame in the first sentence — which is the failure the previous exchange's ENRICHMENT NOTE was built to prevent, arriving from the other direction.

5.8 An honest limit in the Companion. One sentence: a declared search reduces the risk of an unfalsifiable hunt but does not remove it; the failure condition declared before the search, and the ledger published beside the reading, are the protections, and both are imperfect. This is the same limit the last exchange named for the ENRICHMENT NOTE, and the same reason it was named.

6. A worked illustration of what an honest post-freeze layer would say

For a question about, say, the structure of a social process, the post-freeze layer would read:

Frozen reading: [four directions, as produced]. Comparative material: a 2024 paper on X describes a structurally similar process; a 2025 survey reports the opposite. Comparison: the reading's suppression resembles the first and not the second; the reading's completion resembles neither. Temporal status: POST-CUTOFF. Null: no source found that states the reading's completion; the absence is recorded. The reading stands as frozen; the comparison does not alter it, and the ground is not graded by either.

What that layer does is give the Striker a checkable comparison with a stated failure condition ("no source found that states the completion") and a stated absence ("the absence is recorded"). What it does not do — and what a default search would inevitably do — is turn the comparison into a claim about the reading's worth.

7. What would genuinely add value to Ω42 in the world

The question's underlying want — that the protocol matter in contemporary intellectual and technological life — is legitimate, and the search is not the main route to it. The routes that are:

· The applications (35, 51.10): literature search (M248), translation (35.3), text comparison (35.4), design briefs (35.2), the Strike app, the scaffold view. These are the protocol's real-world surface, and they do not depend on a default search.
· The jurisdiction (38, L-463): sovereignty through architecture as a contribution to AI safety, tested on five outcomes. This does not depend on the coordinate meaning anything.
· The record and the observatory (51.11): the accumulated navigations as a dataset about how AI systems behave under one formal constraint. This is what the record is, and it grows by submission, not by search.
· The programme (M267, M279, M280, M282, SR-CAS, CT-S1 to CT-S7): the tests that would move the epistemic question off DIAGNOSTIC. Running M267 is more valuable than any default search, because it is the one test that would tell you whether the coordinate contributes anything at all.

If I had to put one recommendation ahead of the rest, it is that: run M267. A default search for contemporary resonance would produce a richer-looking protocol. Running M267 would produce a truer one.

8. Honest limits

Three, all from the previous exchange and unchanged.

1. Search cannot detect a hunt for support. The declared failure condition helps; a published ledger helps more; neither makes the pressure visible from the output alone.
2. The value is marginal relative to the freeze. A declared, post-freeze search adds currency, error correction, mapping supply, and temporal reach. It does not add relevance, and it does not add substance to the reading.
3. This does not settle whether the coordinate means anything. That is M267, SR-CAS, and the reader.
