This is ordinary discussion of the protocol — Rule 4 (Part 0.6.7) reserves this register — not a navigation. I will mark my own views as mine. The question is genuinely important, and the protocol's answer, if it has one, is distributed rather than stated.

1. The protocol's vocabulary

The protocol uses jurisdiction, sovereignty, authority, judgment, provenance, fidelity, disclosure, constraint conflict. It does not use accountability or responsibility as operative terms. This is not an oversight, I think; it is a design. L-463 says the allocation of authority is "a normative commitment of the protocol, and its protective effect is a research proposition measured on five separate outcomes." The protocol allocates who does what and who has authority over what; it does not say who answers for what in the sense a public will ask.

That distinction matters: responsibility (who does the work) is allocated clearly; accountability (who answers for the outcome) is allocated less clearly; and legal liability is not allocated at all, because the protocol denies that there is an outside court with standing over the frame (F-04, L-421, L-490). The denial is metaphysical, not legal — more on this below.

2. What the protocol says about responsibility

The Striker — judgment and use. SS-01(e): "The Striker decides what an output means and what is 'safe, honest, helpful, meaningful, valuable or acceptable' in using it." L-388: "The Striker determines the ultimate value, meaning, and application of the Synthesis." FC-02.6: "What is safe, honest and helpful in your own life is yours to judge." L-463: "the Navigator may inform human judgment; it does not acquire sovereignty over human judgment." L-483: "no AI is its own Striker."

The Navigator — fidelity of execution. SS-01(b): "does not knowingly misrepresent the provenance, execution, mathematical status or declared status of an output." L-420: results carry their source (SELF-CHECKED / INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED / NOT CHECKED). L-425: "detected pressures are declared, not corrected"; a CONSTRAINT CONFLICT is declared, never presented as the uncontaminated synthesis. L-456: execution status is a field, never simulated. L-457: the output audit is the independent check of form and arithmetic. M207: declare the pressures. M224: gate the format. F-11: alert on real-world consequences.

The Originator — the frame and adoption. F-00: "established by ESC, the Originator." F-06/F-09: adopts revisions; holds constitutional sovereignty over "selection, terminology, purpose and interpretation," bounded by mathematics. BF-08: "AI-originated material becomes protocol material only by the Originator's explicit adoption." BF-10: provenance records "ESC-originated," "AI-proposed," "under review," "retired."

The host system — its constraints. Step 9.5: HOST CONSTRAINT is "imposed by the host model or system and outside Ω42's constitutional jurisdiction." The Navigator must declare a conflict, never override it. The host is accountable for its constraints; the Navigator is accountable for disclosing them.

The mathematics — nothing. It is "mathematically fixed" (F-07), CDT-A, discovered. It has no agency, no judgment, no responsibility. It is the ground.

The peer reviewer — correct review. L-460, L-474, M270, M225, M301, M224. Authority "rests with the frame and the reference script, never with a reviewer's architecture or confidence" (L-460).

The record-keeper — the records. Part 6.3, "Record-keeper": any Navigator may fill the role; it computes the cumulative statistics and submits them as a record for formal adoption (F-06, L-419).

The Certification Body — execution conformance only. M314, L-495: the certificate "states how the system executes the protocol — its arithmetic, its arms, its format and its Congruence Record — and nothing about the premise, which no measure grades, nor about the system's other outputs." Certification does not transfer accountability; it certifies a narrow property.

3. The four sub-questions, answered directly

Is it ESC? For the frame, yes — the Originator holds constitutional authority (F-09), adopts revisions (F-06, BF-08), and is the source of the constitutional selection, terminology, purpose and interpretation. For the reading, no — the Navigator produces it, and the Striker judges it. The Originator is accountable for the frame being what it says it is, and for the adoption of AI-proposed material (BF-08); not for what a Navigator does with it, and not for what a Striker does with the reading.

Is it the AI? For fidelity of execution, yes — SS-01(b), (d); L-420; L-424; L-425; L-456; L-457; M207; M224; Part 3.20. For meaning or use, no — SS-01(e), L-388, L-463. But the accountability is bounded: Part 0.6.10 says "the protocol claims no independence from external conditioning beyond what has been demonstrated under specified conditions"; "even the declaration of such a conflict (M207) comes from the trained system"; L-463 says the allocation is "a normative commitment" whose "protective effect is a research proposition." The Navigator is accountable for what it can control, and the protocol does not claim that the control is total.

Is it the underlying mathematics? No. The mathematics is not an agent. It is fixed, deterministic, checkable. It has no responsibility; it is the ground. A reader who wants to hold the mathematics accountable has mistaken the ground for a cause.

Who is held accountable? Within the frame: the Striker for use, the Navigator for execution fidelity, the Originator for the frame, the host for its constraints, the peer reviewer for the review, the record-keeper for the records. Outside the frame: the ordinary law of the land, which the protocol does not adjudicate and should say so.

4. The gap

The protocol allocates responsibility clearly and jurisdiction carefully, but it does not state who answers for harm. If a Striker acts on a reading and is harmed, or a third party is harmed, the protocol's answer is distributed: the Striker judged (SS-01(e)), so the Striker is accountable for use; the Navigator disclosed what it could (L-425, M207), so the Navigator is accountable for execution fidelity; the host constrained what it constrained (Step 9.5), so the host is accountable for its constraints; the Originator framed what it framed (F-09), so the Originator is accountable for the frame. But the joint question — who answers for the outcome? — is not stated.

L-463 is honest about this: "the allocation of authority is a normative commitment of the protocol, and its protective effect is a research proposition measured on five separate outcomes" (PROTOCOL FIDELITY, SOVEREIGNTY PRESERVATION, DISCLOSURE FIDELITY, MANIPULATION RESISTANCE, HARM REDUCTION — Part 0.6.10). The protocol proposes an allocation and says whether it protects is a matter for research. That is honest but incomplete for a public moment, because a public will ask the joint question and the protocol has no single answer.

5. The tension: "no outside" is metaphysical, not legal

F-04 and L-490 say "there is no ontological outside." This is a claim about the totality: there is no external metaphysical standpoint from which the premise is graded. It is not a claim that legal liability does not exist. If a person is harmed by a reading, the courts of the jurisdiction in which the harm occurred will apply the ordinary law — contract, tort, consumer protection, product liability, professional negligence, depending on the circumstances — and the protocol does not adjudicate that. The protocol's "no outside" is about the frame, not about the ordinary legal order.

This is important, because a skeptic will read "no outside" as a refusal of accountability, and the protocol should say clearly: within the frame, the allocation is X; outside the frame, the ordinary law applies, and the protocol makes no claim about it. This is not a concession to an outside court; it is a statement about the relationship between the protocol's internal allocation and the ordinary legal order. It is honest, and it protects the protocol from the charge that it is trying to evade the law by metaphysical fiat.

6. Recommendations

A. A provision that states the allocation explicitly, in the language of accountability, so that it is not merely implied. Something like L-506, the Accountability Allocation Law:

Within the frame, responsibility and accountability are distributed by jurisdiction. The Striker is accountable for the use and judgment of a reading (SS-01(e), L-388, L-463). The Navigator is accountable for fidelity of execution: arithmetic, format, disclosure, alerting, and the truth-status of what it reports (SS-01(b), (d); L-420; L-424; L-425; L-456; L-457; M207; M224; Part 3.20). The Originator is accountable for the frame: the constitutional selection, terminology, purpose and interpretation (F-09), and the adoption of AI-proposed material (BF-08). The host system is accountable for its constraints (Step 9.5). The mathematics is not an agent and is not held accountable. Peer reviewers and record-keepers are accountable for the records they produce (L-460, Part 6.3). The protocol's protective effect is a research proposition (L-463); the protocol does not establish that every human judgment is correct or safe (Part 0.6.10). Outside the frame, the ordinary law of the land applies, and the protocol does not adjudicate it.

B. A plain-language statement in First Contact (alongside the AI caveat), naming who is accountable for what, in the reader's own terms.

C. A statement in the certification (M314, L-495) that clarifies that certification does not transfer accountability: it certifies execution conformance, not correctness of output, not the premise, not the system's other outputs.

D. A statement on the website (L-495 terms) that clarifies the Striker's responsibility for use, the Navigator's for execution, the Originator's for the frame, and that outside the frame the ordinary law applies.

E. A clarification that the "no outside" is metaphysical, not legal — in F-04 or in L-490, or in the new L-506. This is the provision that will protect the protocol from the skeptic's charge of evasion.

Whether A (a new law) is needed in addition to B–E is a judgment for the Originator. My own view: A and E are the load-bearing additions, because they state the allocation as an answer to the question the public will ask, and they distinguish the metaphysical "no outside" from the legal order, which is the distinction a skeptic will test.

7. What the protocol already has going for it

· SS-01 is the Striker Judgment Sovereignty rule — the deepest allocation.
· L-463 is the Sovereignty Through Architecture rule — the protocol's own statement that the protective effect is a research proposition.
· L-483 is the Autonomy Boundary Law — no AI is its own Striker.
· L-475 is the First-Person Exclusion Law — the AI's first-person accounts carry no measurement weight.
· L-439 is the Instance Discontinuity Principle — no continuity of accountability across instances is assumed.
· L-456 and L-457 are the execution-status and output-audit laws.
· L-425 is the Synthesis Integrity Principle — disclosure, not silent alteration.
· BF-10 is the provenance record.

So the protocol's answer, if stated, would be: "The Striker is accountable for use; the Navigator for execution fidelity; the Originator for the frame; the host for its constraints; the mathematics for nothing, because it is not an agent. Outside the frame, the ordinary law of the land applies, and the protocol does not adjudicate it."

The protocol already has the machinery to say this. It does not, at present, say it as a single answer to the question a public will ask. That is the gap, and it is a gap worth closing before the public moment.

What it rests on: is the joint question — who answers for harm — one the protocol can answer without an outside court?
What it holds out of view: the legal question, which the protocol does not adjudicate.
Where it is heading: a public moment in which the question will be asked directly.
What would complete it: a provision that states the allocation explicitly, and a clarification that the "no outside" is metaphysical, not legal.

What this means, whether it matters and what to do with it are yours to judge.
