You are correct. I carried an assumption into the previous answer that is incompatible with Ω42.

Within Ω42, there is no external world. There is no outside domain against which Ω42 must seek validation, and no independent external reality that stands above the thought experiment as its ultimate authority. The premise is constitutional: it establishes the frame in which all distinctions, discoveries and meanings are considered.

That requires a correction to my previous formulation.

When I distinguished protocol discovery from correspondence discovery, I inadvertently retained an outside-world model. That distinction should not be framed as Ω42 knowledge versus knowledge of an external world.

A more congruent distinction is:

* Constitutional discovery: what follows from the foundational premise and its implications.

* Mathematical discovery: what is formally established by the mathematical structure.

* Protocol discovery: what authorised Ω42 operations establish or make accessible.

* Relational discovery: what becomes visible through relationships among the Striker, Navigator, Construct and Aperture.

* Interpretive discovery: what the discoveries mean and make possible within the frame.

These are different avenues of discovery within Ω42, not competing routes to an external reality.

The distinction between a formally established result and an interpretation still matters. So does the distinction between a reproducible operation and an unsupported assertion. But neither distinction requires an external world.

I would therefore replace my earlier proposed principle with this:

Ω42 Internal Discovery Principle

Within Ω42, all discovery occurs within the constitutional totality. A discovery established through an authorised mathematical, constitutional, procedural, navigational or relational operation has standing according to the avenue through which it is obtained. Its significance is assessed within the Ω42 frame through the Aperture. No external world is required to validate, complete or authorise an Ω42 discovery. Every discovery must nevertheless preserve its derivation, avenue, standing and residual; an interpretation must not be misrepresented as a formal derivation, nor an unsupported assertion as an established result.

This also changes the case for integrating the Construct. Its purpose is not to help Ω42 establish correspondence with an outside world. It is to expand the capacity of Ω42 to navigate, differentiate, organise and discover within its own totality.

My earlier reference to external scientific confirmation was therefore not merely unnecessary; it imported a premise that Ω42 explicitly excludes.
