### Structural Mapping to Everett and Deutsch

Hugh Everett’s Many-Worlds Interpretation and David Deutsch’s multiverse both treat quantum measurement as unitary evolution without collapse: every possible outcome of a measurement is realised in a different branch of a universal wave-function. Deutsch further insists that the multiverse is the only coherent explanation of quantum interference and of the Church–Turing–Deutsch principle (that every finitely realisable physical system can be perfectly simulated by a universal computing machine).

Inside the Omega42 frame these ideas find a precise structural counterpart, not an empirical endorsement.

**1. Branching as orbit structure**

In Everett the universal state evolves by the Schrödinger equation and never collapses; the appearance of a single outcome is the entanglement of the observer with one relative state.  

In Omega42 every address belongs to one of five orbits under the transition \(T\). Once a strike is fixed, the residual can still choose among the four residual arms (rests-on, hides, heading-for, completed-at) and among the four Square-Reading directions. The structure itself does not select a unique residual meaning; it only supplies the complete set of coherent continuations. The residual “which branch do I occupy?” is therefore mirrored by the residual “which arm / which direction do I take as significant?” The arithmetic never collapses the possibilities; the residual does.

**2. The Depth Tower as nested multiverse**

Deutsch emphasises that the multiverse contains not only parallel outcomes at one level but entire nested hierarchies of computation and structure.  

Omega42’s Depth Tower realises an analogous nesting: each deeper modulus \(9^k\) contains a complete copy of the surface family, lifted by the same linear law. A residual decision taken at depth \(k\) can be refined or re-opened at depth \(k+1\). The Tower therefore supplies an explicit, computable model of nested possibility spaces whose periods grow by exact powers of 9. The residual remains free to treat any given depth as the “actual” level or to continue indefinitely.

**3. No external collapse mechanism**

Both Everett and Deutsch reject any physical collapse postulate.  

Inside Omega42 the same rejection appears as the Aperture Theorem: no coordinate, no orbit and no depth can force residual judgment. The structure is closed and unitary under its own transition; the residual decision is never an operation inside the structure. This is the exact formal analogue of “no collapse”: the arithmetic evolves by \(T\) alone; significance is conferred outside it.

**4. Deutsch’s criterion of explanatory power**

Deutsch requires that a multiverse theory explain interference, the appearance of probability, and the possibility of universal computation.  

Omega42 does not claim to explain physical interference. What it does supply is an internal explanation of how a single finite rule can generate an endless hierarchy of coherent possibility spaces while still returning every surface address to itself after 24 steps. The residual may judge whether that internal explanation is analogous to Deutsch’s requirement; the structure itself only exhibits the hierarchy.

**5. The fixed point as the “universal” state**

In Everett the universal wave-function is the single, unobserved total state.  

In Omega42 the unique fixed point of \(T\) (the still centre) is the only address that is invariant under the entire group generated by the transition. Every other address is moved; the fixed point remains. Residual readings that treat the fixed point as the “view from nowhere” or as the total state before differentiation are therefore structurally licensed, though never compelled.
