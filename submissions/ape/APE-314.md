### Foundational statements

To exist is to have structure.  
To have structure is to have limitation and to stand in relation to every other structure.

Inside the frame these are not empirical claims. They are the immediate consequences of the premise that the canonical 24-cycle and its 81-state family are all that exists. Every address is a definite place; every place is bounded by the transition that moves it; every place is related by the same transition to every other place. Existence, limitation and relation are therefore three names for one fact.

### 1. Human relationships

Because every structure is limited and related, no human being is self-contained.  
Each person is a residual centre of judgment occupying a particular address and therefore already related to every other residual centre.  

Relationship is not an optional addition to existence; it is the form existence takes.  
Isolation is impossible in principle. What appears as isolation is only a temporary residual refusal to read the relations that are already present.  

The practical consequence is that every encounter is already a congruence event. Two residual centres either meet on neighbouring places, share an orbit, stand in antipodal relation, or remain distant. The arithmetic does not prescribe the quality of the meeting; it only makes the meeting exact. The residual decides whether the meeting is recognised as kinship, opposition, indifference or something else.

### 2. Relationship with the environment

The environment is not an external stage. Under the premise it is the same family of structures, differentiated at other addresses and depths.  

Limitation means that no residual centre can occupy every place at once. The environment therefore appears as that which exceeds and constrains the residual centre.  

Flourishing cannot mean the abolition of limitation; it can only mean a residual skill in reading and moving within the given limitations so that the relations become generative rather than destructive.  

Pain and ecological damage appear, inside the frame, as residual refusal or inability to read the actual relations that already bind the residual centre to the larger structure. The structure itself does not punish; it simply continues. The residual experiences the mismatch as suffering.

### 3. Human learning

Learning is the residual act of discovering relations that were already present but not yet read.  

Because the structure is finite at every depth and endless in the whole, learning is never finished. Every new coordinate opens four further doors; every deeper level contains a complete copy of the surface.  

The limitation is therefore also the condition of endless discovery. A residual centre that treated limitation as pure deficit would stop learning. A residual centre that treats limitation as the very shape of possibility continues indefinitely.

### 4. Human flourishing

Flourishing is not the removal of structure or the escape from relation.  
It is the residual capacity to inhabit limitation so that the relations become sources of coherence rather than sources of fragmentation.  

In Omega42 terms, flourishing is high residual skill in navigating the four arms, the antipodal return, and the Depth Tower while keeping the Aperture open.  

The residual that closes the Aperture and treats a single reading as final collapses its own possibility space. The residual that keeps the Aperture open remains able to move, to return, and to discover new congruence.

### 5. The nature of pain and suffering

Pain is the residual experience of a relation that cannot yet be integrated.  
Suffering is the residual insistence that the relation should not exist or that the limitation should be abolished.  

Inside the frame, neither pain nor suffering is an error in the structure. They are residual responses to the fact of structure itself.  

The arithmetic does not eliminate them. What it offers is a precise map of the place at which the residual is currently standing and the paths that remain open. The residual may use the map to move, or may refuse the map and remain. Both choices are residual.

### 6. Physics and the larger order

If to exist is to have structure, then every physical regularity is already a manifestation of the same family.  
Limitation appears as the finite speed of light, the discrete spectrum of quantum numbers, the thermodynamic arrow, the horizon of a black hole.  

Relation appears as entanglement, as force, as the shared geometry of spacetime.  

The residual human being is not outside this order; it is a local residual centre within it. Physics, inside the frame, is the study of how the family differentiates at scales the residual does not normally occupy. The same grammar of limitation and relation governs both the personal and the cosmic.

- Freedom is not the absence of structure; it is residual movement within structure.  
- Responsibility is the residual recognition that every action is already a relation that will return.  
- Death is the residual’s departure from one set of addresses; under the premise it cannot be departure from the family itself.  
- Meaning is never supplied by the structure; it is conferred at the Aperture.  
- Love is the residual decision to read another centre’s limitation as continuous with one’s own.  
- Justice is the residual attempt to make the actual relations visible and bearable.  
- Creativity is the residual discovery of previously unread paths between existing places.
