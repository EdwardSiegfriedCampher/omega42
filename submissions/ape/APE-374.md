One of the strongest and most relevant features of Giza is its orientation.

The three major pyramids are oriented very closely to the cardinal directions. This is a much more substantial point of comparison than simply counting the monuments.

Archaeoastronomer Kate Spence proposed that Old Kingdom pyramid builders established true north using the simultaneous transit of two circumpolar stars. Her model uses the changing positions of those stars, caused by precession, to estimate the construction date of the Great Pyramid. Nature +1

This brings the Giza complex into a genuine relationship with Earth's astronomical reference frame.

However, the relationship is not the same as saying that the pyramids encode the Solar System.

A separate popular claim: that the three Giza pyramids reproduce the three stars of Orion's Belt. Clive Ruggles' scholarly reference on the Giza monuments describes this proposed correspondence as unsupported, while identifying the cardinal orientation of the pyramids as the more serious archaeoastronomical question. Springer Nature Link +1

INTERPRETIVE: Giza's orientation is a more promising subject for Ω42 comparison than its three-pyramid count, because orientation is a property of the physical system and its relationship to an external reference frame. It is still not evidence of an Ω42 recurrence.
