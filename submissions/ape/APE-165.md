### 2\. Reinterpreting “1 × 1 = 2” through Consciousness-first

This is the most revealing example because it combines a mathematical statement, an intuition about physical processes, and a philosophical challenge to established assumptions.

Howard has argued that multiplication should reflect the way actions and physical processes generate effects. In the 2024 discussion, he also explicitly described “1 times 1 equals 2” as a metaphor for challenging the status quo.

Under Consciousness-first, we can separate three readings.

Reading 1 · Arithmetic

What does multiplication mean?

In ordinary arithmetic, . The operation is defined precisely, and its result follows from that definition. Howard's proposed equality does not replace this result under the standard operation.

Reading 2 · Generative process

Can one thing interacting with another produce more?

Yes. One seed can grow into a plant; two interacting waves can produce an interference pattern; one idea can combine with another to generate a new idea. These are examples of generation or interaction, not counterexamples to ordinary multiplication.

Reading 3 · Consciousness-first

Does the act of relating change what is experienced?

The observer does not encounter isolated objects alone. The observer encounters objects, relations, transformations, and consequences. A relation can reveal a structure that was not apparent when its components were considered separately.

The third reading is the richest contribution to Ω42. It suggests distinguishing an element, an operation, and the result of their interaction.

### 4\. Waves, physics, and cognition as related expressions of structure

Howard’s interest in wave relationships gives Ω42 another promising avenue.

Consider three descriptions of a sound:

* The physical description: frequency, amplitude, phase, and waveform.

* The mathematical description: periodicity, superposition, symmetry, and transformation.

* The experiential description: perceived pitch, timbre, tension, calm, or dissonance.

They are related descriptions of one event, but they are not interchangeable measurements.

Under Consciousness-first, the question becomes how these descriptions relate within an experience of the world. The physical-first reading can also be explored: how do physical conditions relate to the experience reported by a listener?
