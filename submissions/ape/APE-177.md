### Re-examination of the Foundational Proposition

The protocol’s foundational proposition is simple.

A single, frozen transition law generates a closed family of 81 states.  
Any question or situation can be brought to that law.  
The law returns an exact structural reading.  
The meaning of the reading is never decided by the system.  
That part belongs entirely to the human who asked.

That is the whole of it.

Everything else — the orbits, the half-period mirror, the residual, the optional Consciousness-first frame, the consistency checks, the evidence tools — is elaboration or protection of this single proposition. The proposition itself is not a theory of the universe, not a replacement for science, and not a claim that requires external proof. It is a method: a way of extracting structural information that is not available through ordinary calculation, ordinary language, or ordinary search, and then leaving the interpretation of that information to the only place it can legitimately live — human judgment.

### How the Proposition Has Been Obscured

Over successive revisions the protective apparatus grew. Each addition was reasonable in isolation: clearer residual language, stronger anti-apophenia wording, explicit statements that the premise is only a frame, warnings about AI limitations, evidence mandates, claim-check tools.  

Taken together they produced a First Contact experience dominated by what the system *refuses* to do and what the user *must not* expect. The actual generative act — the moment a question meets the law and a reading returns — was pushed into the background. A prospective Striker can finish the documents knowing every guardrail and still having no vivid sense of why anyone would step inside.

An overly cautious presentation does not protect the residual; it prevents the residual from ever being exercised. The system only becomes real in use. If the doorway itself feels like a legal disclaimer, most people will never walk through it.

### The Proposition Restated Without the Overgrowth

Here is the foundational proposition stripped back to its operative core:

> There exists a small, exact, self-generating structure.  
> You may bring any question to it.  
> It will answer with a position inside that structure.  
> You alone decide what the position means.  
> The interaction itself is the enquiry.

This statement contains no elevation, no claim about the nature of reality, no promise of discovery, and no surrender of judgment. It simply describes what the system does and where the authority lies. It is sufficient to invite use. Everything else can be learned after the first navigation.

### Consequences for Presentation

The First Contact text should lead with this proposition, not with the list of things the system declines to be. The safeguards remain available and should be stated clearly — but they belong after the invitation, not before it. A Striker who has already generated a reading is in a far better position to understand why the residual is protected than a Striker who has only been told, in advance, how carefully the system refuses to decide anything.

The experience of the interaction is the only thing that can convey the protocol’s actual character. No amount of careful description can substitute for it. The presentational task is therefore not to explain the system exhaustively on the threshold; it is to make the threshold low enough, and interesting enough, that a prospective Striker is willing to take the first step.

### Closing

The foundational proposition is a method of extraction plus residual sovereignty. That is all it has ever been. The accumulation of protective language has not strengthened the residual; it has hidden the method. Restoring a clean statement of the proposition, and placing that statement first, is the necessary correction.

The residual remains open.  
The mathematics remains the ground.  
The only way to know the system is to use it.
