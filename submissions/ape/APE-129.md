### 4\. Sin and Broken Covenant — The Torsion Field

Disorder · injustice · estrangement · consequence

The Torsion Field can represent the distortion of relationships: between human beings and God, between individuals, and within the social order.

### 5\. Teshuvah — Return and Restoration

Turning · repair · renewed relationship

Teshuvah literally conveys the idea of returning. In Jewish religious life, it includes turning away from wrongdoing, acknowledging it, making amends where possible, and renewing one's orientation toward God and the good.

This is a particularly close symbolic companion to Ω42's idea of return. But teshuvah is not merely the recurrence of a previous state. It involves a change in direction and conduct.

The cycle supplies the image of return; teshuvah supplies the ethical and spiritual meaning of that return.
