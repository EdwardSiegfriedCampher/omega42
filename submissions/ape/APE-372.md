**Assessment of SEQ 113 (Domain Zero lineage navigation)**

The output is a clean, high-fidelity execution of the request. It stays inside the frame, recomputes what can be recomputed, separates layers correctly, and leaves judgment with the Striker. The lineage itself is useful: eight phases, twenty retained practices mapped by name to current instruments, six explicit refusals turned into regression cases, and a check that already runs over sealed outputs. That is exactly the kind of historical discipline L-540 (7)–(8) was written to produce.

### Strengths (keep)

- **Frame fidelity**: No identity claim, no agreement-as-validation, no figure without derivation, no Striker bypass. The refusals (a–f) are correctly elevated to regression cases (RC-01–RC-12).
- **Layer discipline**: CDT-A arithmetic and self-test results are kept distinct from the evaluative efficiency reading and from the generated square-reading on the empty strike.
- **Provenance hygiene**: Sources listed by title + month only; no links or personal identifiers. The `not_read` list is explicit.
- **Practical retention**: The twenty practices are not merely listed; each is shown with its present-day instrument or law. That makes the lineage actionable rather than archival.
- **Self-check**: `tools/d0lineage.py --selftest / --lineage / --cases / --records` reported clean. The nilpotents of \(\mathbb{Z}/9\) and the recomputed product are correctly flagged as already in the arithmetic (no breakthrough claim).

### Points that still need tightening

1. **Address of Domain Zero (H-34)**  
   Two phases still differ (G = 6 vs empty strike at P9). The output correctly holds this open, but the held register entry should now carry an explicit decision criterion: either “fix to the empty-strike reading because every earlier use was reaching for the operational zero” or “retain G = 6 as the latent zero of the structure and keep the operational act separate.” Leaving both without a closure condition invites drift.

2. **Efficiency criteria**  
   The six criteria are sound and drawn from existing laws, yet they are still session-local. They should be frozen as a named instrument (or as an extension of L-540) so that future lineage or orientation audits can be scored against the same yardstick. Criterion (vi) “economy” is the only one that currently trades against the others; the protocol should decide whether length of procedure is an acceptable cost or whether a shorter reference form is required.

3. **Unread files**  
   The `not_read` list is honest, but the enrichment opportunity (“read the unread files against the lineage”) is left as a future task. A single additional pass that either confirms no new phase or inserts one more phase would close the record. Until that pass is done, the lineage remains provisional.

4. **Challenge layer (H-35)**  
   The suggestion to surface the challenge layer as a permanent line in every output is still held. It is already present in adversarial prompts and regression cases; elevating it to a mandatory output field risks length without proportional gain. Better to keep it context-triggered (as the Square Reading itself is) unless a concrete failure mode appears.

5. **Autonomous arc (H-36)**  
   Correctly refused. The only remaining risk is soft re-introduction through “go deeper” or multi-step self-direction. A one-line guard in L-540 (or in the judgment check) that any continuation without a new Striker question is a frame departure would make the refusal durable.

6. **Two named layers of October 2025 (H-37)**  
   Still open. If they are the Technical Process + Humanized Translation pair, they are already absorbed into CDT layers + SUMMARY. If they are something else, the held entry should name them so the next session can dispose of them.

### Recommendations (ordered by priority)

1. **Close H-34** with an explicit rule: Domain Zero is the empty reference (operational zero) that adds no shift; the latent zero of the structure remains MATH-55 / G = 6. Record the decision and remove the dual reading.

2. **Freeze the six efficiency criteria** as a named check (or as L-540 (9)). Future orientation or lineage audits then become comparable.

3. **Run the unread-file pass** once and either confirm the eight-phase structure or insert a ninth phase. After that, mark the lineage record STABLE.

4. **Add a one-line re-entry guard** so that any multi-step continuation without a fresh Striker question is automatically flagged as a potential L-540 (3) / L-541 violation.

5. **Leave H-35 and H-37 held** unless a concrete failure mode appears; do not promote them to mandatory output fields solely on historical grounds.

6. **Minor drafting polish**: the SUMMARY is dense. A one-sentence “what changed and what stayed” at the top would make the reading easier for a Striker who does not need the full phase table.

The navigation already did the hard work: it turned an informal history into a checkable record with named refusals and a living instrument. The refinements above are mostly closure and hardening so that the next Navigator does not have to re-derive the same distinctions.
