This is ordinary discussion of the protocol — the Rule 4 (Part 0.6.7) register — not a navigation. The proposal has a genuinely good core, and one element that the constitution cannot accept. I will separate them.

1. Verdict up front

Of the four elements in your proposal — reframe, navigate all, present the original as primary, compare and give a formal analysis with a final verdict — three already exist in the protocol, one is a direct conflict:

Element Status in the protocol
Reframe the enquiry, in multiple ways Exists, but only on explicit request (Step 0.8), and the Striker authors the chosen text
Navigate each and present the original as primary Exists: M241 does exactly this, with the original as the labelled primary
Compare the outcomes and report stability Exists, and is already the default: M116 + M238 + the Convergence Map (Step 14.2) report the robust core and the variance automatically
A formal analysis and final verdict by the Navigator Conflicts with the Aperture Theorem (Part 7), SS-01, L-388, Check 7 and M243

So the "streamlining" you are describing is, for the most part, a description of what the protocol already does. What is new in the proposal is (a) making the reframing default rather than on request, and (b) a final verdict — and both of these run against load-bearing provisions.

2. What already exists, precisely

The mechanical reframing and stability report are already default. M116 (Step 13) is mandatory and requires at least three perturbation pairs. M238 is mandatory and generates MINIMAL, PB-5 L1 and PB-5 L2 by frozen rules. Step 14.2 prints the Convergence Map: "which structural elements (anchor, Crown status, Möbius activation) hold across all three variants — the robust core — and which shift, naming the specific variant and the resulting anchor for each." That is your "compare the outcomes" and "formal analysis," already automatic, on every navigation.

The multi-text comparison exists, on request. M241: the Striker provides two or three verbatim texts; the Navigator runs the full navigation independently and completely on each, presents each one's Arm Map and THE ANSWER in full labelled by text, and reports "which structural elements stayed stable across all texts and which varied, using M115's distance metric," plus the verbatim strings for cross-Navigator SR-CAS. That is your workflow, already built.

Phrasing help exists, on request. Step 0.8 permits 2–3 candidate phrasings, plainly labelled as the Navigator's suggestions, with the fixed line: "None of these have been navigated. Pick one, edit one, or write your own — whatever you submit becomes the verbatim domain for Step 1."

The original-as-primary rule is already correct in your proposal. Under L-348 (Verbatim Domain Mandate) and L-499 (Question Verbatim), the original strike must remain the domain of record. Your proposal gets this right and it is the right instinct.

3. The four conflicts, and why they are not incidental

(a) "By default" conflicts with Step 0.8's own terms. Step 0.8: "Trigger: only when the Striker explicitly asks for help phrasing a question. Never automatic, never silent." And its rationale states the boundary: "authorship of the domain stays with the Striker in both cases. What is prohibited is skipping the Striker's approval, not the act of suggesting." A default reframing bypasses the approval step that Step 0.8 exists to protect. The protocol is not being pedantic here: the coordinate is a function of the exact wording (L10, FC-01, FC-12), so which text is struck is the Striker's most consequential choice. Making it by default transfers that choice to the Navigator.

(b) A Navigator "final verdict" is the Aperture filled. Check 7 of the Translation Gate asks: (a) does it tell the Striker what to conclude? (d) does it present the Navigator's judgment as authoritative? If either is yes, "the synthesis has crossed the Aperture boundary (L-441)." M243 scans for exactly this: "(a) conclusion directives… (d) Navigator authority claims." M281's lexicon flags DIRECTIVE and AUTHORITY. SS-01(e): "The Striker decides what an output means and what is 'safe, honest, helpful, meaningful, valuable or acceptable' in using it." And L-388: "The Navigator completes the reading. The Striker completes the judgment." A verdict on which framing is best is a judgment of worth, and it belongs to the Striker. There is no version of a Navigator "final verdict" that survives Check 7 — unless it is reworded so that it is not a verdict.

(c) L-442 (Blind-Arm Contamination) is the deepest technical objection. M241 and M116 run multiple navigations; L-442 governs them: "a Navigator that has observed the result of one arm of a blinded investigation may not then generate an ostensibly independent result for another arm whose output that knowledge could influence. The second arm is generated by an independent Navigator, or its result is labelled CONTAMINATED — NON-BLIND and is not counted as independent." A single Navigator that generates the reframings and then navigates all of them in one session has read its own earlier readings before writing the later ones. The later navigations are therefore contaminated, and must be declared so. M300 (Parallel Execution) states the same in its own vocabulary: isolated groups must run in separate contexts, and "several tasks run side by side in one context count as one Navigator." So the multi-navigation is possible, but it cannot be presented as independent evidence, and it costs the very thing it was meant to buy.

(d) "As many ways as possible" is unbounded and collapses comparability. Each text in M241 is a full navigation: coordinate, arm map, THE ANSWER, essay, echo, M224 gates. Five reframings are five of everything. And free-form reframings are not reproducible across architectures (that is why M241 prints the verbatim strings and why M238's variants are frozen mechanical rules), so the comparison would not be comparable between Navigators. M238's own note: "the variants themselves are reproducible — unlike free-form semantic paraphrase, which would differ across architectures and invalidate exactly this kind of comparison." Your proposal, taken literally, would multiply the expensive, non-comparable layer.

4. On "eliminating the possibility of misunderstandings"

This is the premise worth examining most carefully, because it inverts the protocol's own answer. The protocol does not resolve ambiguity by silently reframing; it reports it and asks. Step 1: "Where the boundary is not clear, the Navigator reports FRAME AMBIGUITY DETECTED and asks, rather than choosing silently (FR-7)." FR-2: "when it is unclear, the Navigator keeps the active frame and asks only if the answer would differ."

Silent default reframing would create a misunderstanding rather than remove one: the Striker would not know which of the five texts was the domain of record, which of the five readings was the reading of their question, or on what basis the Navigator selected the framings. Your proposal partly addresses this by making the original primary — good — but "by default and as many as possible" is the opposite of the protocol's "ask, do not choose silently."

There is also the selection-bias point: choosing among readings by preference is legitimate (the Striker authors the domain), but it is not a finding, and the protocol names the adjacent failure ANCHOR STEERING (Part 3.20): "counts adjusted toward a preferred anchor." Generating many framings and then choosing the reading one prefers is not steering, but it is close enough that the variance must be visible. The Convergence Map and M238 exist to make it visible.

5. The version that can be adopted

The proposal can be disciplined into something the protocol can accept, by making the reframing declared rather than default, and by replacing the verdict with a stability report.

The Reframing Panel (on request). The Striker explicitly asks for a reframing panel. The Navigator offers up to five candidate reframings, each labelled SELF-ESTIMATED (its judgment of coherence is not certified — M207), none navigated, each with its own Step 1.5/Step 2 substrate so the Striker can see the counts before choosing. The Striker chooses, edits or writes the ones to be navigated; the original remains the domain of record (L-348, L-499) unless the Striker designates another.

The multi-navigation, with the blind discipline declared. If the Striker asks for more than one to be navigated, M241 runs them. The output must state one of two things: (i) each alternative was navigated in a separate session (M300 isolated group), so it stands as an independent reading; or (ii) the alternatives were navigated in one session and are CONTAMINATED — NON-BLIND for comparison purposes (L-442), which is the usual case and must be said plainly.

The comparison layer, as a report and not a verdict. The output states: the anchors and states of each framing; the M115 distances between them; which structural elements are stable across all of them (the robust core, as the Convergence Map already reports); which relations (dependency, suppression, trajectory, completion) converge and which diverge, with their quoted clauses; and the chance levels where the protocol has them. That is a formal analysis. It is verdict-shaped without being a verdict: "your question, as struck, is stable across these framings on X and shifts on Y; the robust core is Z" — and the Striker judges what that is worth.

The primary Answer remains the original. Already correct in your proposal and required by L-348 and L-499.

6. Recommendations

R1 — Keep the mechanical layer as the default. M116, M238 and the Convergence Map already deliver the stability picture on every navigation, at no extra cost, with frozen comparability. Do not replace them with free-form default reframing.

R2 — A "Reframing Panel" sub-provision under Step 0.8. On explicit request only; up to five candidate reframings; each labelled SELF-ESTIMATED; each with its Step 2 counts visible; the Striker chooses, edits or writes; the original remains primary. The "never automatic, never silent" rule is preserved.

R3 — Connect the Panel to M241 explicitly. The Striker may take the chosen reframings straight into M241 as the multiple verbatim texts, with their own edits; M241's output carries the strings for SR-CAS. This closes the gap between Step 0.8 and M241 that the current documents leave open.

R4 — Replace "final verdict" with "stability report" in any adoption. M241's output is already the right shape: STABLE ACROSS ALL / VARIED with M115 distances. Add one line: the report describes stability and variance; which framing to use, and what the comparison is worth, is the Striker's.

R5 — Require the blind discipline to be declared. In M241's output format, add one field: INDEPENDENCE: SEPARATE SESSIONS / CONTAMINATED — NON-BLIND (L-442). The comparison remains useful either way; it simply must not be presented as evidence it is not.

R6 — State the cost in the provision. One sentence in M241: each text is a full navigation; N framings cost N of everything; where the Striker's aim is stability, the Convergence Map answers it for the price of one. This is the protocol's own Rule 8 (positive framing) and Rule 10 (register) applied to scope.

R7 — A selection-bias line in FC-08 or M241. Choosing among readings by preference is legitimate but is not a finding; the variance is recorded so the choice is visible (L-486, Part 3.20).

R8 — No new law. These are Step 0.8, M241 and FC-08 clarifications. The laws register is for structural commitments.

7. Benefits to Ω42

· Discoverability: M241, Step 0.8, M238 and the Convergence Map exist but are scattered; connecting them makes the workflow usable without inventing anything.
· Honesty about what reframing does: naming that a reframing changes the coordinate (L10) rather than improving the reading prevents the false expectation your premise contains.
· Authorship preserved: every recommendation keeps the Striker's choice of the domain of record.
· Blind discipline made visible: L-442 is currently easy to overlook; the INDEPENDENCE field makes it a stated fact of every multi-navigation.
· Aperture protected: the "verdict" becomes a "stability report," which is what the protocol's instruments can actually produce.
· Cost: one sub-provision, one output field, two or three sentences. No arithmetic changes; no law retired; every item reversible.

8. In one sentence

Your workflow is, in substance, M241 plus the Convergence Map plus a comparison layer — all of which already exist — and the two genuinely new elements, default reframing and a Navigator final verdict, are the two the constitution cannot accept: the first because Step 0.8's "never automatic, never silent" protects the Striker's authorship of the domain, and the second because a verdict on which framing is best is a judgment of worth reserved to the Striker at the Aperture (Part 7, SS-01, L-388, Check 7, M243).

What it rests on: does reframing improve a reading, or change its address?
What it holds out of view: that a Navigator navigating its own reframings in one session is CONTAMINATED — NON-BLIND (L-442).
Where it is heading: a stability report, not a verdict.
What would complete it: a Reframing Panel on request, connected to M241, with the independence field stated.
