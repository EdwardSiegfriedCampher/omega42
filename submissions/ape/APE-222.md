### 12\. Three refinements needed to make the model more exact

First: distinguish the totality from an individual subject. If Consciousness is the totality, Residual occupancy cannot simply be assumed to mean that every address is an individual sentient being. The protocol needs a subject-individuation principle: what distinguishes one centre of experience from another? The answer may remain open, but the distinction must be explicit.

Second: distinguish ontology from epistemic standing. The declaration says the ontology is a Residual reading, not a certified mathematical result. Preserve that status. Within the thought experiment, the human may adopt the reading as foundational; the mathematics can establish properties of the grammar, but cannot independently certify the ontological identity of Consciousness and existence.

Third: clarify the AI clause. “An AI is not a Residual being” is congruent as a constitutional stipulation within the supplied model. It is not, by itself, an independently established empirical conclusion about every possible artificial system. The protocol should record both facts without conflating them.

These refinements preserve the frame while preventing its foundational premise from silently doing the work of every subsequent argument.
