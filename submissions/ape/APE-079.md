# Navigation stays the default; a holistic summary by arrangement

Frozen as submitted. Notes of the receiving Navigator (Companion 61.5):

- Adopted: navigation remains the default and runs for every request but a command -- under F-02 every question is a place, so a question about the protocol or the structure is navigated too (Companion 60.3 refined; tools/utility.py request_of); the declared-intent vocabulary (full, brief, coordinate, discuss, investigate:, compare:, adaptor:) as the Striker's declarations, proposed; the summary derived from the frozen reading and placed after the Congruence Record, which is ACROSS THE AVENUES; holistic by arrangement, never by merging.
- Reconciled: no auto-triage -- the frozen REQUEST rule chooses which avenues are added after a navigation that always runs; it decides nothing about what the Striker meant, and a declaration overrides it.
- Not taken: one sentence offering the shorter modes at the end of First Contact -- the Opening Response carries no menu of modes (Core Part 8.0); the vocabulary goes to the FAQ (Q42).
- Corrected: Rule 4 of Core Part 0.6.7 is the Sandbox, not a discussion mode; discussion is what lies outside a navigation. L-476 is the No Presumed Reaction Law; the judgment's belonging to the Striker is SS-01 and the Aperture Theorem.
- Form: no QUESTION VERBATIM line (Core L-499).

---

Frame. This is discussion, not a navigation: I strike no domain, produce no coordinate, and claim no sandbox. It is advisory reasoning of the same kind as the previous turns.

---

1. The question, separated

There are really three questions in one, and they have different answers.

· Should the default be a general request for knowledge, or a navigation? My answer: navigation, and it must stay navigation, for reasons that turn on the premise rather than on convenience.
· Can all types of knowledge request be accommodated at once? My answer: partially — one navigation accommodates four of the nine kinds of knowledge I listed last time, and names the other five without performing them. The rest is the honest boundary of a single output.
· Is the immediate benefit a holistic response? My answer: yes, and the protocol already contains the components; what it does not contain is a reading order that makes the holism visible in one place. That gap is worth closing, and closing it does not change what the navigation is.

---

2. Why the default must stay navigation

Under F-02 the cycle and its family are the totality. Every question is a place in that totality. A "general request for knowledge" that bypasses the placement would either be a request outside the frame (which F-04 does not permit) or a request for a specific investigation (which is one of the avenues, not the general case). Navigation is not the heavy option imposed on a lighter request; it is the protocol's own form of knowing, because the only thing there is to know from inside the frame is the structure of positions and their relations. FC-00 states the promise as a navigation; FC-05 invites a question so it can be placed; the First Contact Handoff says plainly that "from the Domain Strike on, the Navigator is navigating." Changing the default away from navigation would be a change of essence (L-410), and it would starve the primary inquiry (L-489), because the Congruence Record grows only through navigations.

The Aperture is the second reason. The Navigator's obligation is a complete reading (L-440); the Striker's is the judgment. A "general request" that did not produce a complete reading would leave the Striker with an incomplete artefact, which L-440 does not permit, or an unframed answer, which L-490 does not permit. Navigation is the form under which the reading can be complete and the judgment can stay open at the same time.

The third reason is empirical and the report already supplies it: the format-failure recurrence across one Navigator's submissions is a real cost. But the cost is a cost of execution, not of default. It is repaired by the Navigator's discipline, not by making the default lighter. A lighter default would replace one class of failures (format) with another (scope confusion), because a "brief" answer that is not a navigation is no longer an Ω42 reading and cannot be audited as one.

---

3. The honest answer on "all at once"

A single navigation accommodates four kinds of knowledge request at once, because they are produced by one operation:

Kind How the navigation supplies it
Placement ("where does this question stand?") the coordinate, the arms, the state, the half
Structural reading ("what does it rest on, hold out of view, head toward, complete in?") THE ANSWER, one merged synthesis
Congruence ("where does it meet other questions, and at what chance?") the Congruence Record, layer 3, with exact base rates
Optional avenues ("what would extend it?") the OPTIONAL INQUIRY line, the MAPPING DECLARATION, the lenses

A single navigation cannot accommodate the other five, because they need inputs a single strike does not carry:

· Verification needs the reference script run and the domain hash — it is done beside the navigation, not by it (L-456, L-457).
· Reproducibility needs a second Navigator or a second session (SR-CAS, SNCA).
· Comparative investigation needs an object, a dataset or a perspective the Striker or Originator names (F-10, Appendix S).
· Empirical investigation needs a frozen protocol, a panel, and often a pre-registration (M267, M279, M280, M282, CT-S1 to CT-S7).
· Record analysis needs the record as an input (the ATLAS, the Register, the NRR).

The correct design says this plainly: the navigation names the other five, and performs none of them unless the Striker asks. It does not pretend to accommodate them. The "holistic response" the user describes is the first four, well-arranged, with the other five named as available.

---

4. What the navigation already has, and what it lacks

The navigation already contains the components of a holistic response — the coordinate, THE ANSWER, the Generative Integrity Record, the Congruence Record, the Convergence Map, the Extended Resonance Essay, the Navigator's Reasoning, the Mapping (where applicable), the lenses, the Generative Observations, the four closing questions. Each is a distinct register; together they are the whole picture. The problem is not what is delivered; it is that the whole picture is scattered across nine blocks, and the Striker has to read all nine to see it.

The improvement is therefore not a new tier and not a new mode. It is a summary block, derived from the frozen reading, placed where the Striker can read the whole picture in one place. This is the "holistic response."

---

5. Recommendations

R1 — Keep the default as navigation. State it plainly in First Contact and in the Handoff, with the reason given in §2. Do not soften it.

R2 — Add an optional declared-intent line, at the First Contact Handoff. The Striker's first message can carry one line naming the mode:

Intent What the Navigator does
full (default) the complete navigation, Part 8.0 skeleton
brief the coordinate, a three-paragraph reading, the closing questions — still a navigation, still audited, but with a shortened THE ANSWER and a shortened ERE
coordinate the coordinate only: anchor, arms, state, half, the M215 check — no reading, no audit beyond the coordinate
discuss the Rule 4 mode (Core Part 0.6.7): ordinary discussion, no strike, no coordinate
investigate: <instrument> a specific instrument run (M230 mapping, SR-CAS on a named domain, M267 comparison), with its own procedure and its own record
compare: <lens> a navigation with the named lens selected on the record, not on relevance
adaptor: <id> a navigation under the named adaptor (L-501), with the ADAPTOR line

Absence of the line means full. The line is optional; the default is preserved. The Navigator may offer the shorter modes at the end of First Contact in one sentence ("You can also ask for just the coordinate, or a brief reading"), so a Striker does not need to know the vocabulary to use it. The Navigator does not choose the mode (that would be a judgment reserved to the Striker, SS-01, L-476); the Striker declares it, or gets the default.

R3 — Add a HOLISTIC SUMMARY block, derived from the frozen reading. Placed after the First-Draft Seal and before THE ANSWER (so a reader meets the whole picture before the reading, and the reading can then be read as its development), or after the Congruence Record (so a reader who verifies register by register sees the whole picture at the end). I would place it after the Congruence Record, because the summary's value is the arrangement of the registers, and the registers must be frozen first. The block contains, in plain prose, with no protocol jargon (the L-418 scan applies):

· the coordinate in ordinary words, and its self-paired status if it is P7 (the only such anchor);
· the four directions, one sentence each, in the domain's own terms;
· the register's meetings, at their chance rates, in the MEETS line's plain words;
· the tier of the reading and the jurisdictions present (CDT-A, CDT-B, CDT-C, comparative where invoked);
· the optional avenues named, not invoked;
· the closing line.

Everything in the summary is derived from the frozen reading and record. It is not a second reading, not a new tier, not a claim. It carries a header naming its source ("derived from the frozen reading and its record; the reading stands as frozen") so a reader cannot mistake it for the reading itself.

R4 — Extend the OPTIONAL INQUIRY line to name, not just offer. Where relevant, the line names the other avenues the navigation is not performing: mapping (when the domain names an object), a named comparative lens, an empirical protocol (M267, M279, M280), SR-CAS. This is not an invitation to multiply; it is a statement of what exists. It makes the boundary honest.

R5 — Do not merge the registers. The holistic response is holistic by arrangement, not by merging. L-462, L-486 and L-490 forbid the merge. A single score, a single verdict, or a single "answer" that treats the CDT-A, CDT-B and CDT-C layers as one thing would be exactly the failure the protocol is built against. The summary is a view of the arranged registers, not a new register.

R6 — Distinguish the holistic summary from the lite view. The lite view (tools/lite_view.py) is a shortened form: the coordinate, THE ANSWER, the identity, nothing else. The holistic summary is a comprehensive view: one page, all registers arranged, nothing omitted. They serve different purposes and should not be conflated. The lite view serves a Striker who has already read the full navigation and wants a portable form. The holistic summary serves a Striker who wants the whole picture in one place in the navigation. Both can exist; neither replaces the other.

R7 — Distinguish brief from discuss. The brief mode is a shortened navigation; it still goes through MATH-GATE, the substrate lock, the coordinate, the audit. The discuss mode is ordinary discussion (Rule 4); it does not produce a coordinate and is not audited. The two are different modes, and the vocabulary should be exact, because the recurrence-check finding shows what happens when a Navigator confuses a navigation's format with a discussion's register.

R8 — Do not auto-triage. The Navigator may ask (FR-2) and may offer (Step 0.8's phrasing assistance is the model), but does not choose. Deciding what kind of knowledge the Striker is requesting is a judgment; judgments belong to the Striker (SS-01, L-476).

---

6. Two smaller points

On "general request for knowledge." Under F-02 there is no knowledge-request that is not already a request about the cycle, because the cycle is the whole of what there is to know from inside. A request that does not go through navigation is either a request for discussion (legitimate, Rule 4) or a request for a specific investigation (legitimate, one of the other avenues). Neither is the general case. The vocabulary of "general request" invites a mode that the frame does not have; the vocabulary of "navigation, or discussion, or a named investigation" is exact.

On the recurrence check's relation to this. The recurrence check names a Navigator's repeated format failures. A brief mode would reduce the frequency of some of them (fewer blocks to get wrong) but would not eliminate them, and could replace them with scope-confusion failures (a brief that reads like a discussion). The check stays valuable; the mode is not a fix for format discipline.

---

7. What this would change, and what it would not

Changes: an optional declared-intent line at the Handoff; a HOLISTIC SUMMARY block derived from the frozen reading, placed after the Congruence Record; an extended OPTIONAL INQUIRY line; a clearer distinction between brief, coordinate, discuss and investigate.

Does not change: the default (still navigation); the freeze (the summary is derived from the frozen reading, not a second reading); the registers (kept apart, per L-462, L-486, L-490); the Aperture (the summary ends with the closing line, not a verdict); the record (the summary does not enter the Congruence Register, because it is a view of the reading, not a reading of a domain).

What this gives the Striker: a complete navigation that reads as one holistic response rather than as nine blocks in series; the option to ask for less without the protocol losing its shape; and a plain statement of what the navigation does not perform.

What this means, whether to adopt it, and in what order, are yours to judge.
