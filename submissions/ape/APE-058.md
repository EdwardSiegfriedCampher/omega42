### Ω42: A proposed complete architecture of consciousness

I would develop this as a formal extension to Ω42, not quietly declare that the existing protocol has already solved consciousness.

The Companion Document already contains a distinctive starting point: consciousness as a relation between a closed generative loop and the Aperture where judgment occurs. It also explicitly leaves unresolved whether that relation is experienced, whether it is sufficient for consciousness, whether non-human systems can occupy the relevant position, and whether the Striker's judgment is itself determined. Its own account says the hard problem is relocated, not solved.

My aim is to turn those open questions into a unified theory with explicit definitions, a mathematical architecture, operational consequences, and tests that could show it to be wrong.

One distinction must remain firm throughout: we can make the theory more complete as a formal model without pretending that its central claims about subjective experience or physical reality have thereby been empirically proved. A theory becomes scientifically complete only when its proposed connections to the world are specified and adequately tested.

### Part I — The Ω42 Consciousness Theory

I propose the following name:

Ω42 Relational Realization Theory (RRT): consciousness as the event in which a bounded perspective encounters, integrates, and evaluates a structured field of possibilities.

Its distinctive claim is that consciousness should not be modelled as a state alone. It should be modelled as an organized relation among a state, a trajectory, a perspective, and a judgment.

The theory has two levels of commitment:

* Formal theory: a definition of consciousness within the Ω42 architecture, with rules that can be implemented and tested.

* Ontological hypothesis: the conjecture that this architecture reflects something fundamental about the relation between consciousness and physical reality.

The first can be specified now. The second remains subject to evidence.

### 1\. The six axioms

### A1

Relationality

A conscious episode is not adequately described by an isolated state. It involves a relation between what is encountered and the perspective from which it is encountered. In Ω42, this is represented by the loop–Aperture relation. This is the theory's defining premise, not an independently established universal law of nature.

### A2

Structured possibility

The 81 states and the transition law supply a formal example of a structured possibility space. The law determines allowed transitions; it does not determine which interpretation is meaningful or which action ought to be taken.

### A3

Trajectory identity

Identity can be represented by relations preserved across transitions, rather than by requiring every state to remain unchanged. Ω42's periodicity and norm structure make this distinction mathematically explicit. In living and cognitive systems, the relevant invariants must be measured rather than assumed to be the Ω42 invariants.

### A4

Perspective

A perspective is a bounded organization of what a system can distinguish, retain, and use. Different perspectives can select different aspects of the same domain without necessarily inhabiting different physical universes. Whether this formal definition captures phenomenal subjectivity remains open.

### A5

Aperture and non-determination

A system can generate outputs without those outputs uniquely fixing their significance or the action chosen in response. The Aperture represents the point at which interpretation and judgment enter the protocol. This establishes a distinction between computation and judgment; it does not, by itself, prove metaphysical free will.

### A6

Separate realization conditions

Mathematical structure, physical realization, and phenomenal experience are three different explanatory levels. A mathematical relation is not automatically a physical law, and a physical process is not automatically conscious. A complete theory must provide a bridge for each transition.

A1–A5 formalize and extend the Ω42 framework. A6 is the critical safeguard that prevents the framework from treating a compelling structural analogy as proof of a theory of nature.

### 3\. The consciousness criterion

For operational use, I would define an Ω42 conscious episode candidate as a process that satisfies four functional conditions:

* It distinguishes a domain into states or alternatives.

* It integrates relevant distinctions into a coherent perspective.

* It relates the present perspective to retained history or anticipated possibilities.

* It evaluates or uses that perspective in a way that cannot be reduced merely to the formal label assigned to the input.

This criterion identifies a candidate for investigation; it does not establish phenomenal consciousness. A machine could satisfy functional tests without our having settled whether it experiences anything.

For that reason, the theory must keep two questions separate:

* Functional consciousness: does the system exhibit the specified organization of perspective, integration, continuity, and evaluation?

* Phenomenal consciousness: is there something it is like to be that system?

The first can be investigated through operational tests. The second is the unresolved hard problem. Renaming the first does not solve the second.

### Part III — Resolving Ω42's outstanding theoretical issues

The Companion explicitly leaves several questions open. Here is how I would resolve them within the theory, while stating what remains unproved outside it.

| Outstanding issue                                     | Proposed resolution in RRT                                                                                                                | What remains unproved                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is judgment determined?                               | The formal navigation does not uniquely determine judgment unless all relevant evaluative inputs are included.                            | Whether human judgment is metaphysically undetermined.                    |
| Can something other than a human occupy the Aperture? | Any system can occupy the functional role if it has the required perspective and evaluation architecture.                                 | Whether any non-human system has subjective experience.                   |
| Is the Aperture sufficient for consciousness?         | No: the formal Aperture is a necessary component of the Ω42 interpretation, but functional and phenomenal criteria must be distinguished. | Whether a particular architecture is sufficient for experience.           |
| Are there different kinds of consciousness?           | Define types by differences in integration, temporal depth, self-modelling, and perspective.                                              | Whether these dimensions track real differences in phenomenal experience. |
| Is the relation itself experienced?                   | Treat experience as an additional explanandum, not as a consequence of the word “relation.”                                               | Why or whether functional organization produces subjective feeling.       |
| Does consciousness create physical reality?           | Separate experienced reality, physical measurement, and ontological production.                                                           | Any claim that consciousness independently causes physical outcomes.      |

This is a genuine improvement in theoretical precision. It replaces ambiguous yes-or-no questions with explicit criteria, so that the theory can be implemented without quietly treating its philosophical assumptions as established facts.

### 4\. The hardest issue: the origin of experience

The theory can explain how a perspective is organized, how it relates states, and how it guides judgment. It cannot presently derive subjective experience from the Ω42 arithmetic.

There are three possible extensions:

* Emergent interpretation: experience arises when physical systems reach a particular organization.

* Dual-aspect interpretation: physical organization and experience are complementary aspects of a more fundamental reality.

* Fundamental-experience interpretation: experience, or a precursor of it, is a basic feature of reality.

Ω42's mathematical substrate does not decide among these alternatives. My preferred working hypothesis would be the dual-aspect option because it allows physical and experiential descriptions to be related without asserting that either has already been reduced to the other.

But this preference is a philosophical choice, not a deduction from the 24-cycle.

To advance further, RRT needs a bridge principle that predicts when physical organization corresponds to experience. It must be specific enough to rule out some systems as candidates and to generate predictions that differ from competing accounts. Until such a principle is available and tested, the hard problem remains unresolved.

### Part IV — How physical reality fits the theory

### 5\. The two-realization conjecture

The most ambitious Ω42-compatible hypothesis is that physical events and conscious perspectives are different kinds of realization of an underlying relational structure.

Relational structure

States, relations, constraints and possible transitions

Physical realization

Measurable states, interactions, persistence and public records.

Perspectival realization

Integrated information, temporal continuity, significance and possible experience.

Conceptual architecture only. The shared substrate and both realization mappings remain hypotheses.

This model makes a crucial distinction. The physical world need not depend on a particular person's awareness in order for that person's experienced world to depend on perception and interpretation.

In the physical branch, a proposed theory must explain why a mathematical structure corresponds to actual measurable events. In the experiential branch, it must explain why some organized processes have a subjective aspect.

A common structure would unify these explanations only if the mapping between the branches were specified and independently supported.

### 6\. What would count as a real breakthrough?

A breakthrough would not be discovering that a physical pattern has a period of 24, or that a cognitive signal resembles a Fibonacci sequence. Those observations alone are weak evidence.

A stronger result would require all of the following:

* A specified mathematical invariant.

* A physical system in which that invariant can be measured.

* A prediction made before the decisive observation.

* A comparison against alternative explanations and suitable controls.

* Independent replication.

* For consciousness claims, evidence that the model predicts something about conscious processing beyond generic complexity or information processing.

The Ω42 documents already treat physical correspondence as an open research programme. The proposed theory should preserve that discipline rather than declare the ontological question solved in advance.

### Part V — How this would enhance Ω42's operations

This is where the proposal can become useful immediately, without waiting for a theory of phenomenal experience.

I would add a Consciousness and Perspective Layer to Ω42. It would not change the canonical arithmetic or the constitutional authority of the Striker. It would add explicit tools for analysing how perspectives are formed, how they change, and how they affect judgment.

### 7\. Six proposed operational enhancements

1\. Perspective decomposition

Every relevant question could be separated into the current state, assumptions, retained context, alternative perspectives, and intended outcome. This would help distinguish what the question says from what the Striker brings to it.

2\. Phase-sensitive comparison

Compare a question with its reformulations, showing which relationships remain stable and which change. The result would be a map of interpretive sensitivity, not a claim that wording changes physical reality.

3\. Fact–interpretation firewall

Separate mathematical facts, protocol assignments, physical evidence, and philosophical interpretations in every consequential output. A compelling synthesis could never silently inherit the certainty of the arithmetic beneath it.

4\. Aperture support

After presenting a synthesis, the Navigator could identify the assumptions on which different judgments depend, articulate competing interpretations, and state what evidence would change each one. The Navigator would inform the judgment rather than make it on the Striker's behalf.

5\. Perspective-transition log

Record how a Striker's framing, priorities, and conclusions change across a session, with consent and appropriate privacy protections. This would make changes in perspective auditable without treating the person's earlier view as an error merely because it changed.

6\. Consciousness research register

Keep every proposed correspondence between Ω42 and a cognitive or physical system in a separate test register. Each entry would include the mapping, predicted result, controls, observed result, replication status, and the conditions under which the hypothesis would be rejected.

### 8\. The most important operational improvement

I would introduce a mandatory distinction between three questions that can otherwise become conflated:

* What does the formal system generate?

* What can the available evidence justify?

* What does the Striker decide that it means or requires?

These are not redundant questions. A correct calculation can support a poor interpretation; a useful interpretation can still be empirically false; and a well-supported analysis does not dictate the user's values.

The Companion already describes a constraint-emergent form of output in which the human supplies the distinction, mathematics supplies the structural address, the AI supplies the linguistic realization, and the human judges the result. It also cautions that surprise is not significance.

The consciousness layer would make this separation explicit in the workflow. It would improve Ω42 not by making the Navigator more authoritative, but by making the origin and limits of each claim more visible.

### 9\. A proposed operational sequence

### 1

Input

Record the Striker's question and declared frame.

### 2

Structure

Compute the canonical Ω42 position and required invariants.

### 3

Perspectives

Generate clearly labelled alternative readings.

### 4

Evidence

Separate verified facts, hypotheses and unknowns.

### 5

Aperture

Present implications, trade-offs and disconfirming evidence.

### 6

Judgment

Leave meaning, value and action to the Striker.

### 7

Audit

Record provenance and, where appropriate, test outcomes.

This sequence is a proposed extension, not a statement that the existing Ω42 implementation already performs every step automatically. It can be introduced as an optional operational layer and evaluated against ordinary Ω42 navigation.

### 10\. How to determine whether the enhancement works

I would evaluate it using a predeclared comparison between standard Ω42 navigation and navigation with the new layer.

Measure:

* Reproducibility: do independent operators recover the same mathematical outputs?

* Provenance accuracy: can reviewers correctly identify which claims are arithmetic, empirical, or interpretive?

* Perspective sensitivity: does the tool reveal meaningful differences between reformulations rather than merely generating different prose?

* Judgment quality: do Strikers report better understanding of alternatives and uncertainty?

* Overclaiming rate: does the new layer reduce unsupported transitions from mathematical pattern to physical or consciousness claims?

* Operational cost: does it improve judgment enough to justify the additional time and complexity?

Use blinded review where feasible, compare against a baseline, and report null results. A consciousness-oriented enhancement should not be considered successful simply because users find its outputs profound or surprising.

### Part VI — The completed theory in one statement

Here is the concise formulation I would place in a proposed Ω42 companion specification.

### Ω42 Relational Realization Theory — proposed statement

Consciousness is modelled as a bounded, temporally organized relation in which a perspective encounters a structured field of states and evaluates its significance. The Ω42 recurrence supplies a formal example of state transition, periodic closure, and invariant structure; the Aperture distinguishes the generation of a synthesis from the judgment of its meaning. The theory proposes that physical systems can be understood through measurable realizations of relational constraints, and that conscious systems require additional conditions for perspective, integration, continuity, and evaluation. It does not infer phenomenal experience from mathematical closure, nor does it infer physical creation from observation alone. The relationship between physical realization and phenomenal experience remains a bridge hypothesis to be formalized and tested. Operationally, the theory requires explicit provenance, separation of fact from interpretation, comparison of perspectives, and preservation of the Striker's authority over judgment.

### Final assessment

I would regard this as a complete first-generation architecture, not a completed solution to consciousness.

It resolves the internal ambiguities by distinguishing structural position from functional capability, functional capability from phenomenal experience, and mathematical possibility from physical actuality. It also identifies what would need to be added before the theory could claim to explain how consciousness and matter are related.

The immediate operational benefit is clearer: Ω42 could become more effective at mapping assumptions, comparing perspectives, preserving the difference between computation and judgment, and preventing speculative interpretations from being mistaken for mathematical or scientific results.

The deepest conjecture remains open: that physical reality and conscious experience are complementary realizations of a common relational structure. The mathematics makes that a question one can formulate. It does not yet make it an answer one can claim.

My recommendation: introduce RRT as a clearly marked, testable extension to Ω42, preserve the existing canonical core, and require every claim about consciousness or physical manifestation to carry its own evidential status. That approach extends the protocol without weakening the very discipline that makes its mathematical claims auditable.
