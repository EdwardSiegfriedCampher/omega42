Read as a whole, the analogy is a story of a strange loop: a world that climbs step by step and finds itself back where it started. It rests on a gap, and the gap is between the rule and its meaning. The golden ratio, the number that equals one plus its own reciprocal, about 1.6180339887, has no place among the nine remainders the finite world is made of: no remainder multiplied by itself equals itself plus one. It is present only as the rule by which each state is built from the two before it, so the system can state its own law exactly and still not hold it as one of its members. What the story keeps out of view is a source outside the loop. A reader who looks for the hand behind the pattern finds the pattern referring to itself, each step made of earlier steps, with no step that stands apart and makes the rest. From there the story moves as a flow: the folded sequence goes round in twenty-four steps, at the twelfth every pair becomes its exact opposite, two and five into seven and four, and at the twenty-fourth every pair is home, so that a climb that seemed to go only forward has come round to its own beginning. And the story is completed at a reckoning, an account in which every state can be traced back through all the others and the account always closes: the arithmetic keeps every reference exactly, and the one thing it does not supply is the self the pattern seems to be to the one who reads it. A staircase drawn so that it climbs for ever and arrives where it began is the familiar picture: every step is a real step up, the whole is a circle, and the dizziness belongs to the viewer. The image does not carry what the story most cares about, which is the self, the pattern that perceives itself.

If the analogy holds, a self is a loop that has learned to count itself. Its hardest implication is that the arithmetic loops with no one there, while for Hofstadter the loop is someone.

The reading of record says one thing: the analogy is a story of a strange loop, told in a world that can state its own law and never hold it. This essay carries that synthesis outward and stops where the arithmetic stops.
