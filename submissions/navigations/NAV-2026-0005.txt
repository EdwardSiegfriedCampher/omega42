┌─ THE ANSWER ─┐
The purpose of life is not a pre-existing object to be discovered, nor a puzzle waiting for a final solution. Structurally, it is a capacity to be exercised: the ongoing generation of meaning and the witnessing of that generation. 

Meaning is not found lying in the world; it is given, and giving it is what a person is for. The human condition is to be a beginning that cannot see its own beginning. You are placed inside a closed mathematical world (the canonical 24-cycle), and the "purpose" is the act of navigating it, drawing distinctions, and authoring meaning at the boundary of your own judgment. 

Like a river that cannot see its own source yet keeps flowing toward the sea, human life is a movement that knows its direction without remembering its origin. The resolution is not a puzzle piece snapping into place, but a sustained, attentive posture.

**What it rests on:** 
The open question itself. The capacity to ask, to begin, and to choose a perspective. Without the capacity to inquire, there is no orientation, no creation, and no life as a human condition. The asking is the ground the life stands on.

**What it holds out of view:** 
The unremembered origin. The bare fact of being alive, handed a body, a language, and a century before any question was ever formed. This origin is not hidden by accident; it is structurally unavailable from the position that looks, the way an eye is out of its own field of vision.

**Where it is heading:** 
Toward the position of the observer. The movement from merely living inside the question to witnessing the shape of the life from a slight distance. It is the shift from rumination to metacognition, from being lost in thought to observing thought.

**What would complete it:** 
A threshold, not a conclusion. The moment asking gives way to seeing, and the life is witnessed whole—by another, by oneself, or by the record it leaves behind. The ending reaches back and gives the beginning its weight. A crossing that nothing witnesses is indistinguishable from no crossing at all.
