The reading of record says one thing: the analogy is a story of a form that re-enters itself, told in a world whose self-feeding rule never contains its own limit. This essay carries that synthesis outward and stops where the arithmetic stops.

Every part of the arithmetic under the figure was computed. That is the shape Kauffman finds in the form that re-enters its own space. Written as a fraction that contains itself, one plus one over the same fraction, the re-entering form unfolds the ratios of successive Fibonacci numbers, one, two, three halves, five thirds, and tends to the golden ratio. In the finite world that limit is absent: The values of x squared minus x minus one over the nine remainders are eight, eight, one, five, two, one, two, five and one, and none is zero. So the figure is exact in a way the mathematician would recognize at once. What the folded sequence does over time gives the figure its oscillation and its limit. Two mirrors facing each other hold the picture together. Each holds the other and the images recede without end, and the pattern needs someone standing between the glasses to be seen at all.
