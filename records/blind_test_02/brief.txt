BLIND TEST 02 -- BRIEF FOR AN INDEPENDENT MAKER (give this text alone, in a fresh session with memory off; attach nothing else)

An abelian anyon theory has 81 anyons labelled by pairs (a, b) with a, b in Z/9. Fusion is addition of labels mod 9.
The topological spin of the anyon (a, b) is h(a, b) = 4(a^2 + b^2)/9 mod 1.

1. Find every invertible linear map A of the labels (a 2 x 2 matrix over Z/9) that preserves every spin, h(Ax) = h(x),
   and every one that reverses every spin, h(Ax) = -h(x) mod 1 (a time-reversal symmetry). Give the counts.
2. For the spin-reversing maps of the largest order: give that order, and the cycle structure of one such map on the 81
   labels (how many cycles of each length).
3. For those maps, give the trace and determinant mod 9, and state any quadratic relation A^2 = sA + tI they satisfy.
4. Show your computation (code or working). Say whether any value was estimated rather than computed.
