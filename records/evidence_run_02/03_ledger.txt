E6  24-fold order in matter
  Literature: icositetragonal (24-fold) quasicrystals have been found numerically, by self-assembly in simulations of
    two-dimensional soft-matter models -- hard disks with a square-shoulder repulsion, mimicking dendritic micelles, give
    10-, 12-, 18- and 24-fold bond-orientational order (Dotera, Oshiro & Ziherl, Nature 506:208, 2014); the review "Multiple-scale
    structures: from Faraday waves to soft-matter quasicrystals" (IUCrJ, 2018) cites these and an Engel & Glotzer 2014 study as "numerically discovered
    icositetragonal (24-fold) quasicrystals". No experimental observation of 24-fold order was found; the highest
    experimental symmetry found remains 18-fold (Fischer et al., PNAS 108:1810, 2011).
  Tier: 2. The order is a static symmetry, not a state transition.
