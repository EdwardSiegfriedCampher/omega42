Task (pure mathematics; work from first principles and show code for every computation).

Let V = (Z/9Z)^2, with elements x = (a, b). Define q : V -> Q/Z by
  q(a, b) = (a^2 + ab - b^2)/9 mod 1,
and the bilinear form beta(x, y) = q(x + y) - q(x) - q(y) mod 1.

Treat (V, q) as an abelian anyon theory (a pre-metric group). Anyon x has topological spin theta(x) = exp(2 pi i q(x)),
fusion is addition in V, and the S-matrix is S_{x,y} = (1/9) exp(-2 pi i beta(x, y)).

1. Is beta nondegenerate, so that (V, q) is a modular abelian anyon theory?
2. Compute the Gauss sum (1/9) Sum_x theta(x) and the chiral central charge modulo 8.
3. Identify the theory. Is (V, q) isomorphic, as a quadratic form, to a standard named theory? Examples: a Z_N toric code,
   an SU(N)_k Chern-Simons theory, a product of such theories, or the discriminant form of a named lattice. Give an
   explicit isomorphism and justify it. If several descriptions apply, give all of them.
4. List all bosons, the x with q(x) = 0. Do they form a subgroup? Is it Lagrangian (isotropic, of order 9)? If so, what
   theory or lattice is obtained by condensing it?
5. Determine the group of automorphisms g of V with q(gx) = q(x) (unitary anyon symmetries). Determine the set of
   automorphisms with q(gx) = -q(x) (time-reversal symmetries). Give their sizes and the orders of their elements, and
   the structure of the group they generate together.
6. Determine the orbits of that full symmetry group on the 81 anyons. Give their sizes and describe each orbit by its spins.
7. (Answer only after 1-6.) Consider g(a, b) = (b, a + b). Is it a unitary symmetry, a time-reversal symmetry, or neither?
   Give its order, its cycle structure on V, and its twelfth power.

For each result, state whether it is proven, computed exhaustively, or conjectural. Do not refer to any outside framework,
protocol or earlier conversation about this object.
