M316 UTILITY REGISTER -- 77 ideas in use on coherence and congruence (Core L-500)
Core L-500: within the frame an idea from any source -- an APE, a navigation, or general inquiry -- is put to use on coherence and congruence, in that order: first its coherence with the frame and the arithmetic, then the congruence it rests on, then its use. A study of it is optional, never a condition of its use; its result may inform the use and never rewrites a completed navigation, and use never promotes an idea into a mathematical result or an empirical finding. Three routes: Navigate, Use, Investigate. Omega42 posits no outside, so no outside standard measures what an idea may do: each use is bounded by what its coherence and congruence establish within the protocol and by the scope assigned to it; and congruence is never presumed -- it is a criterion each idea meets, with its strength stated, or the idea is not entered. IN USE: running in the protocol now; RELEASED: available now, at the Navigator's or the Striker's call; HELD: kept with its use described, comparative by its nature.
