======= M316 FOUND ASSOCIATIONS (avenue W; Companion Part 61) =======
Companion Part 61; Core L-503: an association found by search is a perspective within the totality (F-04). A search runs by default as the CURRENT KNOWLEDGE layer of a full navigation and a holistic response, and on request (L-429, M248); it is declared first with what would count as not found, and placed in the Comparative Perspective Layer after the seal, with its source, its date, what it says, how it stands with a named provision -- AGREES, DIFFERS or EXTENDS -- and, for an agreement, its grade: CONSTRUCTIONAL where both sides state the same mathematics, SELF-ADJACENT for one Navigator in one session, INDEPENDENT otherwise. It is COMPARATIVE, never inside THE ANSWER, never an authority over a reading, and the premise is not graded by it (L-490). This register holds the associations about the protocol's own provisions; sources on a Striker's matter stay in that reading's CURRENT KNOWLEDGE layer.
