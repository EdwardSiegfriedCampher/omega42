COMPUTED [EXACT]: six faces with two shared edges each give 6 x 2 / 2 = 6 shared edges and 6 x 5 - 2 x 6 = 18 free edges; the
  surface is therefore open (M323 --faces 6 5: a closed solid of six pentagons is impossible). The face-adjacency graph is
  2-regular on six nodes, so it is either one 6-cycle (automorphism group of order 12) or two 3-cycles (order 72). The four
  point pairings joining two structures (FIGS. 3A-3B) fit two 3-cycles joined at vertices; the text alone does not decide it.
