# Stoicism (Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, and Epictetus, the Encheiridion, in the translations of George Long, 1862 and 1877, public domain; wording checked against lexundria.com, classics.mit.edu and standardebooks.org on 22 October 2026) -- frozen atlas, Core M309 step 1; chosen at the Originator's request of 22 October 2026 (records/frameworks/philosophy_selection.txt) and written before any statement was struck; quoted, a leading "For" removed and clauses taken alone
STO 1 | Constantly regard the universe as one living being, having one substance and one soul.
STO 2 | All things are implicated with one another, and the bond is holy.
STO 3 | There is one universe made up of all things, and one god who pervades all things.
STO 4 | All things from eternity are of like forms and come round in a circle.
STO 5 | The present is the only thing of which a man can be deprived.
STO 6 | The periodic movements of the universe are the same, up and down from age to age.
STO 7 | Observe constantly that all things take place by change.
STO 8 | Of things some are in our power, and others are not.
STO 9 | Wish the things which happen to be as they are, and you will have a tranquil flow of life.
