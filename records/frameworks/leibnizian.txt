# Leibniz (the Monadology, in the translation of Robert Latta, 1898, public domain; wording checked against hume.ucdavis.edu on 22 October 2026, where the translator is not named and the wording is Latta's as recalled, M273) -- frozen atlas, Core M309 step 1; chosen at the Originator's request of 22 October 2026 (records/frameworks/philosophy_selection.txt) and written before any statement was struck; quoted, clauses taken alone
LEI 1 | The Monad, of which we shall here speak, is nothing but a simple substance.
LEI 2 | The Monads have no windows, through which anything could come in or go out.
LEI 3 | God alone is the primary unity or original simple substance.
LEI 4 | It is a perpetual living mirror of the universe.
LEI 5 | They agree with each other in virtue of the pre-established harmony between all substances.
LEI 6 | There can be no fact real or existing, no statement true, unless there be a sufficient reason.
LEI 7 | Truths of reasoning are necessary and their opposite is impossible.
