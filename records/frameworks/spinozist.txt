# Spinozism (Benedict de Spinoza, the Ethics, in the translation of R. H. M. Elwes, 1883, public domain; wording checked against Project Gutenberg eBook 3800 on 22 October 2026) -- frozen atlas, Core M309 step 1; chosen at the Originator's request of 22 October 2026 (records/frameworks/philosophy_selection.txt) and written before any statement was struck; quoted from Part I and Part II, clauses taken alone
SPI 1 | By God, I mean a being absolutely infinite.
SPI 2 | That thing is called free, which exists solely by the necessity of its own nature.
SPI 3 | Besides God no substance can be granted or conceived.
SPI 4 | Whatsoever is, is in God, and without God nothing can be, or be conceived.
SPI 5 | From the necessity of the divine nature must follow an infinite number of things in infinite ways.
SPI 6 | God is the indwelling and not the transient cause of all things.
SPI 7 | Nothing in the universe is contingent.
SPI 8 | Things could not have been brought into being by God in any manner or in any order different from that which has in fact obtained.
SPI 9 | The order and connection of ideas is the same as the order and connection of things.
