# Buddhist tradition (the Dhammapada in the translation of F. Max Mueller, Sacred Books of the East 10, 1881, public domain; wording checked against Project Gutenberg eBook 2017 on 21 October 2026) -- frozen atlas, Core M309 step 1; chosen at the Originator's request of 21 October 2026 and written before any statement was struck; quoted, with the verse cited, a clause taken alone where the verse is longer and the translator's parenthetical words removed
BUD 1 | All that we are is the result of what we have thought.
BUD 2 | Pain follows him, as the wheel follows the foot of the ox that draws the carriage.
BUD 3 | Hatred does not cease by hatred at any time: hatred ceases by love, this is an old rule.
BUD 4 | Looking for the maker of this tabernacle, I shall have to run through a course of many births.
BUD 5 | Painful is birth again and again.
BUD 6 | But now, maker of the tabernacle, thou hast been seen; thou shalt not make up this tabernacle again.
BUD 7 | Self is the lord of self, who else could be the lord?
BUD 8 | Not to commit any sin, to do good, and to purify one's mind, that is the teaching of the Awakened.
BUD 9 | Pain, the origin of pain, the destruction of pain, and the eightfold holy way that leads to the quieting of pain.
BUD 10 | Nirvana the highest happiness.
BUD 11 | All created things perish.
BUD 12 | All created things are grief and pain.
BUD 13 | All forms are unreal.
