# A TENSION is a place where the correspondence pulls against itself: a role filled by a statement that the arithmetic contradicts in the respect the role names, or two roles that cannot both hold as filled. It is not a difference between an analogy and its object -- that a ratio is not a god, or a remainder not a soul -- which the frame already grants (F-02-P, L-490) and which the record does not repeat. The count stands beside the coherence score and is never summed with it (L-486); both are the Navigator's judgments, CDT-C, and a second judge of another architecture is required before either is quoted outside the record (M300).
# TYPES (L-492): EXACT -- the school's statement is itself a statement of arithmetic and the counterpart computes it; STRUCTURAL -- the counterpart has the same relational form (one law, no member equal to the source, return through the negative); ANALOGY -- a likeness of figure without shared form; INTERPRETATION -- a reading the arithmetic neither carries nor contradicts; OPEN -- the arithmetic is silent.
