# Claudius Ptolemy, Tetrabiblos, Book I (second century CE; the English of J. M. Ashmand, London, 1822, in the public domain; F. E. Robbins, Loeb Classical Library, 1940, in the public domain in the United States, its copyright not renewed) -- frozen atlas, Core M309 step 1, of the astrological tradition from its source; chosen at the Originator's request of 31 October 2026 to resolve the atlas left OPEN in Companion Part 134, and written before any statement was struck; paraphrased by the Navigator from Book I, not quoted; Ashmand's chapter XVI and Robbins's chapter 13 give the aspects
AST 1 | The motions of the heavens act upon the things of the earth.
AST 2 | The zodiac is divided into twelve signs of thirty degrees each.
AST 3 | The signs alternate in nature, one masculine and the next feminine.
AST 4 | Signs in opposition stand six signs apart and are discordant.
AST 5 | Signs in trine stand four signs apart and agree in their nature.
AST 6 | Signs in quartile stand three signs apart and are discordant.
AST 7 | Signs in sextile stand two signs apart and agree in their nature.
AST 8 | The signs fall into four triplicities, each of three signs in trine.
AST 9 | Each planet is beneficent or maleficent by its share of heat and moisture.
