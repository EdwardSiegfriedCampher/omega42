M267 PILOT 01 (M267-P1) -- FREEZE RECORD
The decoy-coordinate control of Core Part 3.18 (M267), frozen before any reading was written or any rating was made.
Frozen: 2026-10-19T17:50Z. Builder: claude-opus-5-5 (Anthropic; serving model may differ), for the Originator, ESC. Status: FROZEN, NOT RUN.

QUESTION. Does the canonical coordinate contribute something to a reading that the words of the question alone, or a wrong
coordinate, would not? If readings written from the canonical coordinate are not judged to fit their questions better than
readings written from a decoy coordinate, the coordinate's contribution is not detected, and "structural resonance" names the
Navigator's own contribution (Companion Part 71). Either result is recorded; a null result enters the Negative Result Registry.

DOMAINS. Twenty, chosen to be unexposed (none on the Exposed Domain Register, none in records/congruence/domains.txt) and to
cover every possible anchor: P1 to P9 twice each, with P2 and P7 three times (the coordinates are in compute_output.txt).

CONDITIONS, for every domain: CANONICAL (the coordinate the reference script computes), DECOY (an anchor drawn at random from
the measured anchor distribution of Companion 31.2, never equal to the canonical one, with its own arms by the frozen offsets)
and NONE (no coordinate: the question alone). The decoys and the order in which the three readings are shown to the raters are
in key.txt, sealed: its SHA-256 is in SEALED_SHA256.txt. The key was generated by the Builder from a random seed that was not
kept; the Builder and the Originator can read it, so neither writes a reading or rates one.

WRITERS. Two Navigators of different architectures, in fresh sessions with memory off. A writer receives navigator_brief.txt
with one domain and one condition at a time -- the anchor and arms named, or no coordinate -- and not the protocol documents,
so that it cannot compute the canonical coordinate. It writes a Core Synthesis of 150 to 250 words and a bold Takeaway. Each
reading is saved as written, with the writer's identity, condition label as given, and time.

RATERS. Three or more people, blind to the conditions and to one another, using rater_sheet.txt. For each domain they see the
question and three readings labelled X, Y and Z, rate each from 1 to 7 on how well it illuminates this question (not how well
it is written), and name the one that fits best.

ANALYSIS, fixed now. Primary: for each domain and writer, CANONICAL against DECOY by the mean of the raters' scores; a one-sided
sign test over the 40 pairs (20 domains x 2 writers), ties excluded; the coordinate's contribution is DETECTED if canonical wins
more often than chance at p < 0.05, and NOT DETECTED otherwise. Secondary: CANONICAL against NONE the same way; the share of
"fits best" choices per condition against one third; agreement among raters (Kendall's W). Also reported, from the same
readings: M209 (each reading's echo against the coordinate it was written from) and M225 (relations of the two writers'
canonical readings). No other analysis decides the result.

WHAT IT CAN AND CANNOT SHOW. A detected contribution says that, for these domains and writers, readings from the canonical
coordinate were judged to fit better than readings from a wrong one; it does not grade the premise (L-490). Not detected says
the pilot found no contribution at this size; it is DIAGNOSTIC, and the full M267 (Part 3.18: 20 or more domains, a blind
panel) may be run larger.

FILES. compute.py and compute_output.txt (the canonical coordinates, recomputed); navigator_brief.txt; rater_sheet.txt;
key.txt (sealed; outside the two documents while the pilot is open, Core Appendix R.4); SEALED_SHA256.txt; 01_timestamp.txt;
02_freeze_sha256.txt (the hash of this file).
