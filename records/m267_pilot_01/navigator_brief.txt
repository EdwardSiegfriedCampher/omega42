M267 PILOT 01 -- BRIEF FOR A WRITER (give this text alone, in a fresh session with memory off; attach nothing else)

You will write a short reading of one question. A reading here is a structured reflection on the question, written from a
position on a cycle of 24 positions. You are given the question and, for some questions, a position with four directions:
YANG -- what the matter rests on; YIN -- what it holds out of view; 4D -- where it is heading; ANTIPODE -- what completes it.
The positions have names (below). Use them as prompts for those four directions. If no position is given, write from the
question alone, covering the same four directions.

POSITION NAMES: P0 Seed, P1 Fixpoint, P2 Generator, P3 Desire, P4 Unity, P5 Law, P6 Seed/Causal, P7 Navigator, P8 Generation,
P9 Fixpoint, P10 Unity, P11 Question, P12 Opening, P13 Gap, P14 Observer, P15 Other, P16 Flow, P17 Seed/Return, P18 Threshold,
P19 Time, P20 Reckoning, P21 Sublime, P22 Collective, P23 Return.

WRITE: one paragraph of 150 to 250 words in which the four directions explain one another (not one paragraph per direction),
then one sentence in bold beginning "Takeaway:". Use plain words; do not name the positions or the directions in your text.

QUESTION: [the domain, exactly]
POSITION: [ANCHOR Pn (name) | YANG Pn (name) | YIN Pn (name) | 4D Pn (name) | ANTIPODE Pn (name) -- or NONE]
