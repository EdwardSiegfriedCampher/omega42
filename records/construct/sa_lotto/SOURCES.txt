SOUTH AFRICAN NATIONAL LOTTO, MAIN GAME -- THE DRAW HISTORY USED BY records/construct/sa_lotto.py (Companion Part 105)

Retrieved 28 October 2026 by the Navigator, one page per year, from
  https://www.lotto.net/south-africa-lotto/results/2000  ...  /2026
through a page-reading tool that returns the page as text. Each draw is stored as date, six main numbers in
ascending order, and the bonus number. Lotto Plus, Daily Lotto and PowerBall are not included.

What was checked (records/construct/sa_lotto.py, section I):
  - every row has six distinct main numbers in ascending order, a bonus distinct from them, all within 1 to 58;
  - every date appears once; draws fall on Wednesdays and Saturdays, with three exceptions (two Fridays and one
    Thursday, holiday moves as listed by the source);
  - the twenty most recent draws agree with a second source,
    https://www.lotteryextreme.com/south-africa/lotto/results-history ,
    and the eight most recent with a third, https://www.lotteryvalley.com/south-africa/past-results .

What is known to be missing or uncertain:
  - the source lists no draws between 2007-03-31 and 2007-10-13; those draws are absent here;
  - 2000 and most of 2001 have one draw a week, as the source lists them;
  - the transcription passed through a reading tool, so a single wrong digit cannot be excluded beyond the
    checks above; the analysis does not depend on any single draw;
  - the number pool of each period is inferred from the largest number drawn, not from the operator's rules:
    1 to 49 until 2017-08-15; 1 to 52 from 2017-08-16; 1 to 58 from 2025-09-24 to 2026-05-30; 1 to 52 from 2026-06-03.

SHA-256 of draws.csv is printed by the script and frozen in its output.
