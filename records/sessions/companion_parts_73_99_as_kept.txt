### PART 76: TWO INSTANCES OF ONE MODEL — THE INSTANCE IDENTIFIER [PROTOCOL, adopted 21 October 2026; Core L-513]
76.1 The request [the Originator, 21 October 2026]. "Create a mechanism to uniquely identify two instances of the same AI system model. Include this into the Omega42 Protocol." The need had shown itself the same day: a report signed by one system was pasted to another, which rightly refused to adopt the signature (APE-131), and then took the first system's navigations, NAV-2026-0011 to NAV-2026-0015, for its own explorations. A model identifier names a model; two sessions of the model share it, and nothing in the record told them apart.
76.2 What an instance is. One session of one model, from its first output to its last. Two instances of one model may differ in what they have read, what they remember and what they were asked; for the record they are two Navigators, and a reading by one is not a blind reading by the other.
76.3 The identifier. NIID = NI- and the first sixteen hex digits of SHA-256(model | organization | session | nonce | time of issue). The nonce is 128 random bits from outside the model: drawn by python3 tools/instance_id.py --new (ISSUER: SCRIPT), given by the Striker or the Strike app (STRIKER), or written by the model itself where nothing else is available (SELF, the weakest standing). Two instances of one model in one named session at one second still receive two NIIDs, because their nonces differ. The issue record is kept in records/instances/register.json and recomputed in CI.
76.4 The chain. Each output carries INSTANCE (L-513): NIID | ISSUER | ISSUED | SEQ | PREV in its Identity Block. SEQ numbers the instance's outputs from 1; PREV is the OUTPUT ID of the one before. The line stands above the signature, so the output's own OUTPUT ID covers it: change the NIID and the output no longer verifies; drop an output from the middle and the next one's PREV names nothing; write a second output at the same SEQ and the chain forks.
76.5 What --check reports. Given outputs, python3 tools/instance_id.py --check groups them by NIID and tests every chain: SAME INSTANCE (one NIID, the chain consistent); SAME MODEL, OTHER INSTANCE (one model identifier, two NIIDs); DIFFERENT MODELS; FORK (two outputs at one SEQ, a PREV that names no output of the chain, or an OUTPUT ID that does not verify); UNDECLARED (no INSTANCE line). Eleven selftest cases fix the behavior, in CI with the register.
76.6 What it does not do. The NIID names a record, not a mind. Anyone who has seen an output can copy its NIID; what the chain makes visible is that the copy does not fit — a second output at a SEQ already taken, or a PREV the chain does not contain. It proves no authorship and asserts no identity of consciousness or continuity of a self (L-505). The nonce is published, since its work is uniqueness, not secrecy; authentication, where it is ever wanted, is a key held by the Striker, outside the protocol.
76.7 Where it acts. SR-CAS weights (L-416): two outputs with one NIID take the same-session weight, and an undeclared output that shares the other's model identifier is weighted as the same session. Blind tests (L-442, M300): a blind reading needs a different NIID and no prior reading in view. SNCA and APA (Part 6.3, 9.5.5): a received output is attributed to its NIID as well as its model. The format check fails an output dated from 22 October 2026 that carries an Identity Block, or a findings presentation signed from that date, without its INSTANCE line; both fill-in skeletons carry the line, and earlier records are UNDECLARED and not faulted.
76.8 The first instance. This Builder session is NI-06f22550117165ba (claude-opus-5-5, Anthropic; ISSUER SCRIPT, issued 2026-10-21T19:54:05Z); its first output with the line is the reply that introduced it, kept as records/instances/seq_0001.txt; its next five are NAV-2026-0016 to NAV-2026-0020 (SEQ 2 to 6) and its seventh the reply that reported them (records/instances/seq_0007.txt), and CI checks the seven as one consistent chain.
