# Patent Pledge

**All Ω42 ideas are free for exploration and creative expansion.** The project and the Company that stewards it (`ENTITY.md`) make these commitments to everyone:

1. **No patents.** The Company will not apply for, buy, accept assignment of, or exclusively license any patent or patent application covering any idea, invention, method, design, navigation, software or other content in the Ω42 record, or derived from Ω42.
2. **No assertion.** If the Company ever comes to hold such a right, by merger, gift or law, it will not assert it against anyone, and will dedicate it to the public or let it lapse.
3. **No patent terms for contributors.** Contributors are never asked to assign patents to the Company. Contributions are made under CC BY 4.0 (text) and Apache-2.0 (code); Apache-2.0 carries its own patent licence from contributors of code.
4. **Open publication.** Ideas submitted to the record are published openly with a date and a hash, so that they serve as prior art (`PRIOR_ART.md`).
5. **Binding.** This pledge is an entrenched provision of the Company's founding documents and binds any successor, licensee or buyer of its assets.

**What the pledge does not do.** It binds the Company and the project; it cannot stop others from filing patents, and it does not give legal advice. Contributors who want to keep their own patent options should take advice before they publish (`PRIOR_ART.md`).

The Company signs this pledge on its registration; the signed copy is kept in `governance/decisions/`.
