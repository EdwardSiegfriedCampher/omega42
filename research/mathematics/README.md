# Mathematics of the ground

The Fibonacci law modulo 9, the ring Z[φ]/9, the five orbits, Pisano periods, the norm form, symmetry groups, codes and every exact property of the 81-state family.

## Open questions

- What else does the ring Z[φ]/9 say about the three long orbits?
- Which properties of modulus 9 hold for other moduli, and which are special?

## Instruments

MATH-GATE, M276, M291, M296, M298
