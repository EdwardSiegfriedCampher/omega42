# Coherence and congruence

The primary inquiry (Core L-489): the structure agreeing with itself, each reading with its coordinate, readings with one another across domains, wordings and Navigators, and the structure with the fields and traditions it meets.

## Open questions

- Which places in the Congruence Register gather questions from unrelated fields, and what do the readings there share?
- How stable is a reading's coherence line across Navigators and wordings?
- What new exact descriptions of the 81-state family agree with the ones already known?
