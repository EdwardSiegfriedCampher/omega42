# Open questions and research programs

The frontier list of the Speculative Foundations Program (`research/philosophy/SPECULATIVE_FOUNDATIONS.md`, Companion Part 234), drawn from the library. Proposed by ChatGPT 6 (OpenAI) from a review of the library, relayed by the Originator on 20 November 2026. Each layer below is related to the others through explicit mathematical mappings, never by assuming that a shared pattern is one physical or metaphysical law.

## Four layers

1. **The canonical construct:** 81 states, the Fibonacci law modulo 9, the 24-step loop, the orbits, the four directions.
2. **The extended framework:** finite fields F9 and F27, affine geometry, modular transformations, quadratic invariants, orbit classification, higher powers of three.
3. **The relational and transformational framework:** graphs, mappings, comparison operators, transformation groups, temporal dynamics.
4. **The domain layer:** physics, biology, consciousness, language, relationships, society, information.

## Programs

- **Quantum mathematics (QM-01, APE-485).** Quantum state geometry; invariant-preserving transformations; finite-field representations of quantum information; relational representations of entanglement; orbit structure between discrete and continuous descriptions; alternative descriptions of measurement. Guiding speculation: could quantum behavior be understood through the geometry of relationships and transformations rather than of isolated states? Pursued as a program, kept apart from what the cat-map construction shows.
- **Symmetry and Representation (APE-477).** Adinkra transformations as explicit state transitions; affine maps between Adinkra constructions; binary codes and ternary fields in one representation; systematic search for new classifications; links to E6, E7 and E8. The general instrument: the smallest transformation that turns one structure into another, and what survives it.
- **Pattern, Perception and Physical Structure (APE-162).** A three-way map, physical phenomenon ↔ mathematical structure ↔ perceived experience, each arrow described on its own: cymatic eigenmodes, symmetry breaking, mode structure and perception, recognition under change of scale. A resemblance starts an investigation; it is not evidence of a common mechanism.
- **Language and Meaning (APE-061).** Language as a system of transformations T: L → L′ (rephrasing, translation, abstraction, expansion, compression, metaphor, negation, substitution), and **Semantic Persistence**: how much of a question's meaning survives a transformation. Instruments: a semantic invariance explorer, a cross-language concept mapper, a framing-sensitivity instrument, an ambiguity detector, a conceptual distance calculator.
- **Consciousness and Meaningful Existence (ME-01 to ME-06).** Structural, historical, changing and meaningful existence, with the working model H(t+1) = F(H(t), R(t), A(t), E(t)) for history, relationships, agency and environment: a frame for exploration, not a reduction of experience to an equation.
- **Relational Reality (ME-04).** A Relationship Discovery Engine on a network G(t) = (V, E, W(t)) in which each relationship has its own state R_ij(t) = [trust, communication, power, dependency, mutual understanding]. Guiding speculation: is collective reality a property of individual minds, or a structure in its own right formed by the relationships between them?
