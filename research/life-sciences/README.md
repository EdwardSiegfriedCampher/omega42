# Life sciences

Part of the optional comparative programme: phyllotaxis, rhythms, genetics, physiology; and readings of questions about living things. Readings are never medical advice.

This area is part of the optional comparative programme (Core Appendix S). Its standards — freezes before comparison, declared criteria, null controls, recorded misses — are those of the Evidence Programme (L-464) and the Congruence Profile (L-466). Results are described as Congruence Depths and Profiles, never as grades of Ω42 or its premise (L-490).
