# Research areas

Ω42 accommodates any question. The areas below organize the open record; every submission is filed in one (the form asks, and the curator checks). The primary inquiry comes first (Core L-489); areas marked *optional programme* belong to the scientific investigations of Appendix S.

| Area | About |
|---|---|
| [Coherence and congruence](coherence-and-congruence/)  *(primary)* | The primary inquiry (Core L-489): the structure agreeing with itself, each reading with its coordinate, readings with one another across domains, wordings and Navigators, and the structure with the fields and traditions it meets. |
| [Mathematics of the ground](mathematics/)  | The Fibonacci law modulo 9, the ring Z[φ]/9, the five orbits, Pisano periods, the norm form, symmetry groups, codes and every exact property of the 81-state family. |
| [Cross-architecture review](cross-architecture/)  | SR-CAS and SNCA: whether independent AI Navigators following the same rules reach the same coordinate and convergent readings; blind arrangements; review quality. |
| [Language and extraction](language-and-extraction/)  | How words become counts: content words, letters, syllables, numerals, contractions; extraction in other languages; translation; what wording does to a coordinate. |
| [Consciousness and mind](consciousness-and-mind/)  | Consciousness as the loop–Aperture asymmetry (Companion Part 2), freewill as choosing a perspective, the relation of mind and the physical world. |
| [Philosophy](philosophy/)  | Metaphysics of a closed totality, the INSIDE, truth within the frame, epistemology of readings, ethics of sovereignty, the lineage from Pythagoras to Tegmark. |
| [Traditions and lenses](traditions-and-lenses/)  | Bashar, the Seth Material, the Ra Material and other traditions set beside Ω42 by the conceptual isomorphism pathway; mythology; religious thought; new lenses. |
| [AI safety and sovereignty](ai-safety-and-sovereignty/)  | Sovereignty through architecture: the Navigator informs judgment and never takes it over; Aperture erosion; the executor paradox; human–AI collaboration. |
| [Physics](physics/)  *(optional programme)* | Part of the optional comparative programme (Appendix S): quantum maps, topological phases, quasicrystals, E8 spectra, cycles and symmetries in physical systems. |
| [Chemistry and materials](chemistry-and-materials/)  *(optional programme)* | Part of the optional comparative programme: crystal structures, stacking sequences, quasicrystals, molecular symmetry, materials questions struck as domains. |
| [Life sciences](life-sciences/)  *(optional programme)* | Part of the optional comparative programme: phyllotaxis, rhythms, genetics, physiology; and readings of questions about living things. Readings are never medical advice. |
| [Cognitive science and psychology](cognitive-science-and-psychology/)  | What a reading does to a person's thinking: reframing, perturbation, the excitement and return logs, and the studies proposed in the Companion. |
| [Computing and software](computing-and-software/)  | The reference implementation and its ports, the tools, the site and apps, data formats, the curator agent and the API. |
| [Engineering and inventions](engineering-and-inventions/)  | Designs, devices, methods and inventions offered to the open record as prior art (PRIOR_ART.md). |
| [Economics and society](economics-and-society/)  | Questions about markets, organizations, cooperation, cities and social change, struck as domains and read through the structure. |
| [Law and governance](law-and-governance/)  | The project's own governance, open publication and prior art, and questions of law and institutions read through the structure. Readings are never legal advice. |
| [History](history/)  | Questions about the past, the lineage of ideas Ω42 belongs to, and cycles in history read through the structure. |
| [Arts, music and literature](arts-music-and-literature/)  | Texts, music, images and art navigated as domains or media; 24-tone music; creative work built on the structure. |
| [Education](education/)  | Teaching the arithmetic, the protocol and the habits of mind it trains: the child's rule, hand tutorials, classroom navigations. |
| [Personal practice](personal-practice/)  | Questions from people's own lives: decisions, reflection, the Structural Mirror, the excitement and return logs. The judgment always stays with the person. |
| [Media navigation](media-navigation/)  | Images, audio and other media struck through their counts (M247, M265): what the counts measure and what they do not. |
| [Time and cycles](time-and-cycles/)  | Cycles of 24 and of 8, calendars, rhythms, recurrence, the Great Year of the family, and time as read in the frame. |
| [Open area](open/)  | Anything that fits no other area. New areas are created from here when enough work gathers. |

New areas are proposed with the *Propose research* form and created by the Committee when work gathers.
