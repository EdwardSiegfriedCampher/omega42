## Four numbers

Write down the simplest law of growth there is: each step adds the last two. That is the Fibonacci matrix,
[[0,1],[1,1]], four numbers and nothing else.

Now run it on the first odd prime where a mirror is possible, the prime 3. Something extraordinary happens. The law snaps
into a structure that is exact at every scale, all the way down an infinite tower:

- At every level it cycles with a period of exactly 8·3^(k−1).
- Halfway through, every state returns as its own mirror image.
- It sorts the whole space into exactly (3^k + 1)/2 orbits. That is 2, 5, 14, 41, never one more, never one less.
- Add a single shear and the motion grows into a group of exactly 8·3^(3(k−1)) transformations. It climbs a precise
  ladder from coarse to fine, one 3-adic digit at a time.

None of this is approximate and none of it is a metaphor. Every one of these statements is proved, and recomputed by
machine every time the record is rebuilt. Out of four numbers comes an infinite, perfectly ordered world, and Ω42 is the
art of navigating it.
