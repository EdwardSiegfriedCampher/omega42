# Question navigation

How the Navigator decides the way to answer a question (proposed by ChatGPT 6 (OpenAI), relayed by the Originator; proposal CA-32, level B until ESC ratifies it; the Core is authoritative).

## Five approaches

| Approach | What the Navigator does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Omega analysis | Applies the established definitions, rules and mathematics of Ω42, traced step by step | What are the cycles and fixed points of the 81 states? |
| Active exploration | Navigates the Companion, research records and outside sources; reports what it could and could not establish | Could Ω42 relate in a useful way to quantum information? |
| Hybrid | Establishes what the Ω42 mathematics shows, then investigates the wider implications | Does the recurring structure illuminate inherited patterns in human behavior? |
| Open speculation | Develops possibilities and thought experiments freely, marked as speculation | What would a world with a different law be like? |
| Formal development | Gives definitions, equations, derivations, proofs or counterexamples | Prove that sixteen laws share Fibonacci's cycle type |

The approaches combine: a speculative question can call for formal development. The Navigator names every approach it used. Automatic classification is the default; the person asking can choose one (the Strike app offers all five).

## Routing

1. **Interpret** the question: subject, intended outcome, scope, and any explicit request for speculation, proof or research.
2. **Check internal sufficiency:** can the definitions, rules and mathematics of Ω42 answer it completely?
3. **Select** the approach (or take the one requested).
4. **Execute** it.
5. **Challenge** the result: calculations, assumptions, alternative readings, counterexamples, source quality.
6. **Report:** the approach, the reasoning, the evidence, the limits, the open questions.
7. **Offer a continuation** where one is natural.

No external research when an internal answer is complete and reliable; no internal calculation presented as answering a question that needs outside evidence.

## Method and status are recorded apart

| Status of a statement | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Mathematical derivation | What follows from stated mathematical assumptions |
| Logical conclusion | What follows from stated premises and rules |
| External finding | What a source or research record reports |
| Formal connection | A demonstrated mathematical or logical relationship |
| Speculative connection | A proposed relationship worth exploring |
| Empirical finding | What observations or experiments support |
| Open question | What remains unresolved |

A result found in a published paper through active exploration is an external finding, not a speculation; a conjecture produced by direct analysis is still a conjecture.

## In the Discovery Bulletin

Each announcement carries its method beside its class. Classes include OMEGA RESULT (from the internal structure), RESEARCH DISCOVERY (found through investigation), NEW CONNECTION, SPECULATIVE FRONTIER, FORMAL DEVELOPMENT and OPEN QUESTION.
