# Recognition

Every accepted contribution is credited by name or handle in the record, on the site and in the monthly newsletter. Points accumulate into levels shown on the contributor's public profile on the site.

| Contribution | Points |
|---|---|
| Accepted navigation | 1 |
| Accepted navigation of a domain not yet in the record | +1 |
| SR-CAS, SNCA or APA review | 2 |
| Applied Protocol Exploration (APE) | 3 |
| Prior-art disclosure | 3 |
| Merged code | 3 |
| Research proposal adopted into an area | 3 |
| Erratum that leads to a correction | 2 |
| Translation of a protocol section | 3 |

| Level | Points | |
|---|---|---|
| Striker | 1 | First accepted contribution |
| Navigator-Partner | 10 | |
| Cartographer | 30 | May be invited to review |
| Fellow | 75 | Eligible to vote in Committee elections after two contributions in 24 months (GOVERNANCE.md §3) |

Monthly recognitions in the newsletter: **New domain of the month** (a question that opened a new place in the Congruence Register), **Review of the month**, and **Disclosure of the month**. They are chosen by the Committee (before its formation, by ESC) with the reasons stated.

Points are computed from the record by `tools/site_build.py` and published in `community/contributors.json`; nothing is awarded by hand except the monthly recognitions.
