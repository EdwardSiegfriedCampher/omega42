# Verified professionals

Professionals — mathematicians, physicists, physicians, lawyers, engineers, philosophers, teachers, practitioners of the traditions Ω42 meets — can be listed as verified, so that readers can weigh their opinions knowing who is speaking.

**How to be verified.**
1. Open the issue form *Verify my credentials*. In the public form give only what you want public: name, field, title, affiliation (optional), ORCID or a professional page, and a short statement of expertise.
2. The form tells you the private route for evidence (an email address of the Committee's verification officer, or ORCID login confirmation). Never post documents in the public issue.
3. A verification officer checks the evidence against a public source (ORCID, a professional register, an institutional page) and records the result on the issue: VERIFIED with the date and the source type, or NOT VERIFIED with a reason.
4. On VERIFIED, the intake adds your entry to `community/professionals.json` with a registry number such as `PRO-0007`. The private evidence is deleted.

**What it gives you.** A badge on your posts on the site, the right to post in Discussions → *Professional Opinions*, eligibility to review submissions in your field, and a listing on the site's *Professionals* page.

**Your opinions are yours.** Verification confirms who you are and what you are qualified in; it does not mean the project agrees with you, or that you speak for an employer. Entries are reviewed every two years and may be withdrawn at your request.
