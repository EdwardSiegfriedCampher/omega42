# The monthly newsletter

**Who receives it.** The newsletter goes only to:
- **registered contributors** — anyone who registers through the *Register as a contributor* form, with no submission needed;
- **everyone who submits Ω42-generated content** — through the forms, or by the web-app's exchange, in which every reading is submitted;
- **users of the commercial applications**, who receive it whether or not they choose to submit.

Each of them chooses whether to receive it, confirms the address with the newsletter provider, and can unsubscribe from any issue. Everything the newsletter reports stays public in the open record — the Library, the Congruence Register, the APE Commons, the Prior Art Ledger and the forum; what eligibility governs is the letter itself: the month gathered, sorted and introduced, with its essays, commentaries and recognitions. `python3 tools/newsletter.py --eligible HANDLE` answers for a handle from `community/contributors.json`; commercial accounts are passed to the provider by the Company's account system.

**What it contains.** The record in numbers, from the Record page (`tools/dashboard.py`): each figure with what it counts, what it does not and, where it can be stated, its chance — counts of domains, never of validations (Core L-490); the null results of the month beside the meetings; instruments run on real material for the first time; registry movements at the revision that set them; a corrections column with stable links; the new meetings in the Congruence Register — unrelated questions landing on the same state; the month's accepted navigations, including the readings submitted from the web-app; philosophical essays written by any AI, which reach the newsletter through the APE pathway — marked exploratory, passed by the Applied Protocol Assessment (Core Part 9.5.5) and entered in the APE Commons — each labelled with its model; commentaries by verified domain experts, from the Professional Opinions category, marked as perspectives with credentials and declared conflicts shown; experimental results, each reported under its own investigation's question and never as a status of Ω42; reviews and SR-CAS results; new prior-art disclosures; protocol revisions and what changed; the debate summary; recognitions (`RECOGNITION.md`); research calls and open investigations subscribers can join.

**What it never does.** The newsletter and the curator's analyses are kept out of the corpus they report on (Companion 28.2), and nothing in them is fed into a navigation (Core M91-2, L-490).

**How it is made.** The host-AI compiles it: on the first day of each month the `newsletter` workflow runs `tools/newsletter.py`, which writes a draft from the record — counts, Register meetings and every section of submitted items, sorted by the curator — and hands it privately to the newsletter provider as a draft. It is never committed to the public repository. An editor adds the human parts (protocol revisions, debate summary, recognitions) in the provider and sends it to the eligible list.

**How it is sent.** GitHub cannot send email, so the project uses a newsletter provider (for example Buttondown or a self-hosted listmonk), with its address and key held as the repository secrets `NEWSLETTER_API_URL` and `NEWSLETTER_API_KEY`. The list is by invitation: a registration or an accepted submission brings a private invitation from the provider, and a commercial account brings one with the account. The site's subscribe form asks for the contributor handle or the commercial account and passes it to the provider, which confirms eligibility and the address. Protocol releases are not newsletter content alone: every release is also announced publicly in Discussions → Announcements and in the site's feed of the record.

**Subscriber services.** Subscribers can choose, in the provider's preferences, to also receive: alerts for new releases; a digest of new readings in chosen research areas; calls for reviewers; Committee election notices.
