# The Ω42 names, marks and content badges

## Who holds them

**Omega42**, **Ω42**, the Ω42 logo, the edition names (*Ω42 Open*, *Ω42 Professional*) and the content badges below are held by the Ω42 Company (`ENTITY.md`); the certification mark *Ω42 Compatible* is held by the Certification Body. Until it is registered, the Originator holds them in trust for it. The Company holds them for the project, under the locked provisions of its founding documents, and licenses their use to the Stewardship Committee.

## What a mark protects, and what it does not

A trademark protects a **sign of origin**: it tells people that something comes from, or is certified by, the project. It does **not** give anyone ownership of the ideas, readings, inventions or code in the Ω42 record. Those stay under the open licences (`LICENSES.md`), free for anyone to explore, extend and build on, and the Company holds no patents on them (`PATENT_PLEDGE.md`).

## Branding of Ω42-generated content

To keep Ω42-generated content recognisable and trustworthy, the Company owns these badges and sets the rules for their use:

| Badge | May be shown on | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Ω42 Navigation** | A navigation | Produced under the protocol and carrying the Navigator Identity Block and signature |
| **Ω42 Verified** | A navigation | Passed the output audit, the format check and the Congruence Record in the record, with its ID (for example NAV-2026-0007) |
| **In the Ω42 Record** | Any item | Accepted into the open record, with its ID |
| **Ω42 Prior Art** | A disclosure | Entered in the Prior Art Ledger, with its ID and hash |
| **Official edition** | Software or a service | Licensed by the Company |
| **Ω42 Compatible** (Script-capable or Text-only) | An AI system | Certified by the Certification Body after an audit with the Navigator Conformance Suite, for the named model version (`CERTIFICATION.md`) |

Anyone may use the first badge on their own Ω42 navigations. The others may be used only where the condition holds and the ID is given; the site lets anyone check an ID.

## Use without asking

- Saying that your work uses, extends, reviews, criticises or works with Ω42. The term *Ω42 Compatible*, used as a badge or a claim about an AI system, is reserved for certified systems.
- Naming the protocol and its version when you publish a navigation or a study.
- Using the names in articles, talks, teaching, research and art.
- Forking the open code under its licence, under a different name.

## Ask the Company first

- Naming a product, service, company, domain or app "Omega42" or "Ω42", or using the logo on it.
- Presenting anything as an official, certified or endorsed edition.
- Using the content badges where their condition does not hold.

Requests go to the Company at the contact on the site. The Company answers within 30 days and publishes its brand guidelines on the site.
