# The Ω42 legal entity

A legal entity will be established to give the project a durable home. In this document it is called **the Company**, until its registered name and form are chosen.

## What the Company does

1. **Sets the general direction** of the project: strategy, budget, partnerships, the roadmap, and the commercial edition (`COMMERCIAL.md`).
2. **Holds the brand.** It acquires and holds the trademark and branding rights in the names **Omega42** and **Ω42**, the logo, the edition names, and the marks that identify Ω42-generated content (`TRADEMARK.md`).
3. **Signs for the project.** It enters contracts (hosting, the newsletter provider, the archive, the fiscal host), employs or contracts staff, and is the legal contact for takedowns, privacy requests and the law.
4. **Operates the commercial edition** and reports its income and spending every year (`GOVERNANCE.md` §6).
5. **Establishes the Certification Body**, a dedicated legal entity that certifies AI systems *Ω42 Compatible* (`CERTIFICATION.md`). The Company owns it and binds it by the locked provisions, but does not direct its audit decisions: its auditors and its appeals panel are appointed by its own board, on which the Stewardship Committee holds the majority. It holds and licenses the certification mark and publishes the register of certified systems.

## What the Company does not do

1. **It owns no patent rights.** It does not file, buy, accept assignment of, or license patents on any Ω42 idea, invention, method, navigation or code, and it does not ask contributors to assign patents to it (`PATENT_PLEDGE.md`).
2. **It does not own the ideas or the content.** Everything published in the open record stays under the open licences (`LICENSES.md`). Trademark rights protect names and badges, the signs of origin; they give no ownership of the ideas or text behind them. All ideas stay free for anyone to explore, extend and build on.
3. **It does not close the record.** The protocol, the reference scripts, the free edition and every published contribution remain publicly available without payment.
4. **It does not decide what a reading means.** The judgment stays with the person (Core Part 7).

## Locked provisions

The Company's founding documents (articles, constitution or deed, depending on its form) carry these provisions as entrenched clauses. They can be changed only with the consent of the Overseer and of the Stewardship Committee by five votes:

- the no-patent rule and the Patent Pledge;
- the open licences of the record and of the free edition;
- the Overseer's constitutional reserve (`GOVERNANCE.md` §4);
- the purpose: to steward Ω42 as an open body of knowledge for everyone.

If the Company is wound up, its marks and any remaining assets pass to an organization with the same locked provisions, chosen by the Committee, or else are released for public use; they are never sold to anyone who would close the record.

## How it is governed

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Board of the Company** | General direction, budget, contracts, the commercial edition, the brand. Five directors: the Originator (or the Overseer's nominee), two directors appointed by the Stewardship Committee, and two independent directors with legal or financial experience. |
| **Stewardship Committee** | The protocol, the record, acceptance of submissions, recognition, moderation appeals (`GOVERNANCE.md` §3). The Board does not overrule it on these. |
| **Overseer (ESC)** | The constitutional reserve (`GOVERNANCE.md` §4), including consent to any change of the locked provisions. |
| **Certification Body** | Audits and certifies AI systems *Ω42 Compatible*; its own board, with a Committee majority, appoints the auditors and the appeals panel (`CERTIFICATION.md`). |

Until the Committee is formed, ESC appoints the directors the Committee would appoint.

## Choosing the form

The Board's founding members choose the form and the country with a lawyer. Forms that fit the locked provisions include a non-profit company, a company limited by guarantee, a foundation, or a company with an entrenched public-benefit purpose. If the commercial edition needs a trading company, it is owned by the non-profit body, and the locked provisions bind both. This document is a design for the project, not legal advice.

## Steps

1. Choose the form and the country; draft the founding documents with the locked provisions.
2. Register the Company. The Originator transfers the existing rights in the names and logo to it, under the locked provisions.
3. File trademark applications for **Omega42** and **Ω42** in the classes the Board chooses (for example software, online services, education and publications), and a certification or collective mark for the content badges if the law allows.
4. Establish the Certification Body and file the certification mark **Ω42 Compatible**; publish its fees and audit rules before the first audit (`CERTIFICATION.md`).
5. Publish the Patent Pledge, signed by the Company.
6. Record each step in `governance/decisions/`.
