The origin of Omega42

Omega42 began with one observation: the Fibonacci rule, read modulo nine, returns to its start after twenty-four steps. With the help of AI systems that construct grew into an open framework for exploring questions across mathematics, science, philosophy and consciousness. It is an open initiative, and the focus is on the work, the questions and the possibilities, not on the identity of its originator. More about its origin and governance.
