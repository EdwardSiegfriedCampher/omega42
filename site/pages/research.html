The curator

A deterministic curator already files every item by area, finds duplicates and questions asked more than once, and links questions that meet on the structure. A dedicated AI Navigator will later summarise and thread the record under human review. Its rules are public: it never alters a computed field and never judges what a reading means for anyone.
