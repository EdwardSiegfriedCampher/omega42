How this is made

tools/curator.py computes the baseline (kind, declared area, hashes, duplicates). tools/curation_review.py adds the proposals under agents/curator/SPEC.md: a registered area where the declared one is not registered, the chamber from the submitted-by line, the disposition from the receiving Navigator's notes, and threads.
