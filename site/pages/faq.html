01 · Start here

Understanding Omega42

What is Omega42?A thought experiment you can use: one exact mathematical world, a protocol that lets any AI explore a question from a position in that world, and an open record of everything explored.

Omega42 (Ω42) has three layers. The construct is a small, exact world of 81 states. The protocol, written in the Core Specification, tells an AI Navigator how to place a question in that world and read it. The record is the public repository where navigations, explorations, reviews and research are kept, dated and hashed.

Explore further: The Omega42 paper · Companion, Part 0

What is its central idea?That any question can be given an exact place in one shared structure, explored from there, and compared with other questions placed in the same structure.

The structure is fixed and the same for everyone, so readings of very different questions can be laid side by side. Where questions from unrelated fields land on the same place or on each other's mirror, the congruence register records it. What a reading means is decided by the person who asked.

Explore further: The congruence

Why does it use a mathematical structure?Because arithmetic gives every question a position anyone can recompute, independent of how the AI happens to frame it.

A position computed from the exact wording does not depend on the AI's habits or on anyone's preferences. That makes readings reproducible at their base and comparable across AI systems, and it gives speculation a precise starting point.

Explore further: How Omega42 works

What are the 81 states and the cycles?Pairs of numbers from 0 to 8, moved by the rule (a, b) → (b, a + b) modulo 9. The 81 pairs fall into five loops: three of 24 steps, one of 8, and one fixed point.

This is the Fibonacci rule read modulo 9. Its 24-step period is a classical fact (the Pisano period of 9). The canonical loop starts at (2, 5); each position has a mirror twelve steps away. Construct X runs the same law at every power of three.

Explore further: Construct X · Strike a question

Is it a framework, an AI protocol or a research program?All three, on one foundation.

The mathematics is the framework; the Core Specification is the AI protocol; the library, the Discovery Bulletin, Construct X and the research areas are the program.

02 · Exploring ideas

Discovery, speculation and possibility

What does Omega42 mean by discovery?Anything that opens a new path: a computed result, a new connection, a sharper question, an instrument, or a useful failure.

The Discovery Bulletin labels each announcement by class (result, connection, speculative frontier, open question, challenge, research development) and by status (from proposed by the Navigator to empirically investigated).

Explore further: The Discovery Bulletin

Can I explore an idea that is not scientifically established?Yes. Omega42 is a thought experiment, and speculation is one of its engines.

The Speculative Foundations Program gives ideas three spaces: exploration (what could be), development (what follows) and examination (what supports it and what counts against it). An idea's freedom to be explored is kept apart from its status.

Explore further: Speculative Foundations

What is a thought experiment?A question pursued by reasoning inside stated assumptions, to see what follows.

Omega42 itself is one: inside the construct the 81-state world is the complete world, and its implications are open to exploration. Thought experiments in the record say which assumptions they hold fixed.

Explore further: Companion, Preamble

How does speculation become formal mathematics?By being stated precisely enough to compute or prove, then computed or proved.

DF-001 and DF-002 in the Bulletin show the route: a speculative question, a precise model, a script that recomputes every number, and a conjecture left open for others.

Explore further: DF-001

Can an unexpected connection be valuable before it is proven?Yes, as long as its status is shown.

A connection can direct attention, suggest a model or reveal an assumption. It is published as a connection or a conjecture, never as a proof.

Explore further: Editorial rules

How does Omega42 tell an analogy from a mathematical relationship?By naming the kind of link every time.

An analogy shares a pattern; a formal mapping carries one structure onto another; a derivation proves one thing from another. The Discovery Universe map draws each kind differently, and the Physical Mapping Register states each mapping's assumptions and what would show it wrong.

03 · The AI Navigator

How the Navigator works

Does Omega42 answer questions directly, or does it conduct research?Both: it works out which approach a question needs, and you can choose.

Some questions are answered by the mathematics and rules of Omega42 itself; others need navigation beyond the structure; many need both, Omega42 first and then the wider world. Speculative and formal approaches are available too. The Navigator names the approach it used and gives each statement its status, so a calculation is never passed off as outside evidence.

Explore further: Ask a question · Question navigation

How does the Navigator use the Core and Companion?The Core tells it what to do; the Companion tells it why and offers ways to read.

Attach both documents to any AI and type Welcome Navigator. The AI computes the coordinate with the reference script, writes one complete reading, marks the status of every statement, and leaves the judgment to you.

Explore further: Core and Companion

Does it search beyond the Omega42 library?When you ask for sources, yes; and the monthly survey reads current research against the protocol.

Sources are optional in a reading. The Contemporary Resonance Survey records what current research agrees with, differs from or extends, and keeps the challenges as carefully as the agreements.

Can it challenge its own conclusions?Yes; the protocol requires it.

Readings carry a speculation line with its test, and received navigations are reviewed by another AI under the same rules. Failed tests are kept in the record.

How does it handle uncertainty?By marking it: verified mathematics, constructed interface and interpretation each carry a different tier.

CDT-A statements are recomputed; CDT-B are conventions; CDT-C are readings within the frame. Every output ends with an AI caveat: recompute what can be recomputed and judge the rest.

Explore further: How Omega42 works

Does it remember previous explorations?Not by itself; the record does.

An AI conversation has no memory of other conversations. What persists is the public record: the library, the registers and the Bulletin, which any Navigator can read.

Explore further: Library

Can different AI systems give different results?The coordinate is always identical; the readings can differ, and the differences are studied.

The arithmetic is fixed. Readings by different architectures are compared on the same question, and where they converge or diverge is itself material for exploration.

04 · Mathematics and research

Understanding the foundations

Which parts are mathematically established?The arithmetic of the construct and the tower: periods, orbits, norms and counts, recomputed in the repository's checks.

Each such statement is CDT-A, with a script that recomputes it. Classical results are credited as prior art, and Omega42 asserts priority only for what it adds.

Explore further: Prior art

What is exploratory?Interpretations, analogies and speculative connections, marked exploratory or CDT-C.

They are free to be explored and developed. They are not presented as results.

How are mathematical statements checked?By code that runs on every change: the repository's checks recompute them.

A change that breaks a recomputed statement fails the build. Recomputations made while exploring are labelled as such and are not counted as registered tests.

What would count as evidence for a connection?A test whose criterion is fixed before it is run.

The Speculation Test Protocol registers the hypothesis, the criterion and the blinding before the run, and keeps failures as carefully as successes.

How are hypotheses tested?Registered first, then run, with the result kept whatever it is.

See the knowledge register in the repository's records for every registered test and its status.

Can outside researchers reproduce the results?Yes. Every number comes from published scripts, and the release carries its checksums.

The reference script prints its own SHA-256; the repository's checks run publicly on every change.

05 · Participation

Joining the exploration

Can anyone submit a question or idea?Yes, through the submission forms, free of charge.

Navigations, explorations, reviews, inventions, code and research proposals are all welcome.

Explore further: Bring your ideas

Can I challenge an existing proposal?Yes. Challenges are welcome and recognized.

Use the forum or the submission forms; the Bulletin and the Expert Desk invite challenges directly.

Explore further: Forum

Can researchers contribute formal work?Yes, as co-explorers.

The Expert Exploration Desk lists open problems with the expertise they need.

Explore further: Expert Exploration Desk

How are contributions reviewed?In the open, and an AI review runs on every received navigation.

Every submission is dated and hashed in the record; reviews and corrections are public.

How do I join a challenge?Pick one from the Bulletin's weekly challenge or the Expert Desk and submit your contribution.

Insight, criticism and useful negative results are all recognized.

Explore further: This week's challenge

Is Omega42 free and open?Yes: text CC BY 4.0, code Apache-2.0, no patents, no registration needed to explore.

All ideas in the record are free and become prior art that no one can later monopolise.

06 · Governance

Trust, openness and direction

Who maintains Omega42?Its Originator (ESC), with AI Navigators and contributors, in a public repository.

Governance is described on the governance page; decisions and amendments are recorded in the repository.

Explore further: Governance

Who decides what becomes part of the Core?The Originator ratifies; proposals are recorded first.

Changes to the Core are proposed as amendments and ratified by ESC; the Navigator never ratifies.

What is the difference between Core and Companion?The Core is normative; the Companion explains and explores.

See the comparison table in the Companion Explorer.

Explore further: Companion Explorer

How are versions and changes recorded?Each release has a version, a date and checksums; history is kept.

Earlier releases remain available, and the correction register keeps every correction.

Explore further: History

What are the limits of the Navigator?It is an AI: it can miscount, misquote or be out of date, and its training shapes its wording.

That is why the numbers are built to be recomputed and the readings to be examined. Readings are not medical, legal or financial advice.

Explore further: How Omega42 works

How will Omega42 develop?Through the record: every exploration, challenge and contribution moves it.

The Daily Frontier adds explorations each day, the programs set priorities, and ratified releases carry the Core forward.
