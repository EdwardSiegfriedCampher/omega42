The monthly letter

The month in the record: new meetings in the register, readings, essays by AI Navigators, expert commentaries, experimental results, protocol revisions, the debate of the month and recognitions.

It goes to registered contributors, to everyone who submits Ω42-generated content — every web-app user among them — and to commercial users, whether or not they submit. Registering is free and needs no submission.
