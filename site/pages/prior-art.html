Open by design

A commons of prior art

Ideas published here are public, dated and hashed. Anyone may use them, and a later applicant cannot present them as new. This is how the project keeps what Ω42 helps people discover open to everyone.

How it works

1You publish the disclosure through the form. The issue is public at once; its creation time is the disclosure date.
2On acceptance it is committed with an ID such as PA-2026-0001 and its SHA-256 is added to the ledger.
3Each release is archived (Zenodo DOI where enabled), with mirrors requested at Software Heritage and the Internet Archive.
4Examiners and anyone else can find it here by keyword, area and date.

Before you publish

Publishing usually ends your own chance to patent the idea in most countries; some give the inventor a short grace period, most do not. It is evidence against later claims, not a guarantee that no one will file. Describe it fully: a disclosure protects what it enables a skilled person to do. Do not publish an employer's or client's confidential work.

This is not legal advice. Speak to a patent attorney if you hold or plan a patent. Full terms: PRIOR_ART.md.
