How you ask: your question exactly as you would put it; help me phrase it for candidate wordings you choose from; two or three wordings side by side; in a full navigation, other framings shown after your own, with primary only to leave them out. The order of your words is not counted; the words are. How it is presented: brief, coordinate, full, go deeper; the reading in your own tradition; an analog from music or from my trade, and another analog. What you may add or decline: place it or map it for an object; a lens or none; sources or no sources; Certify Navigator; and the Navigator's account, the protocol explained by the AI in its own words.

What this app does and does not do

It computes the coordinate exactly, with the unchanged reference script, the same one every Navigator uses. Two engines. One exploration. The browser calculates your exact coordinate; an AI Navigator, under the Core and Companion, develops the interpretation, explores connections and builds a complete reading. You can reproduce the calculation and examine the reasoning (how it works; Core L-506). For a complete reading, attach the protocol to any AI and type Welcome Navigator. The commercial edition will add hosted readings, batches and comparisons (editions).
