Governance

Open, with a keeper

Ω42 is run in the open. Its Originator, E.S. Campher (ESC), steers it now. A legal entity, the Ω42 Company, will give the project a durable home and set its general direction; a Stewardship Committee takes over the protocol and the record; ESC continues as Overseer.

The path

1Founding. ESC adopts revisions; the portal, intake and records run from day one.
2Formation. At 50 accepted submissions from 20 contributors, or earlier at ESC's call, nominations open for 30 days and ESC appoints the first Committee.
3Incorporation. The Ω42 Company is registered with its locked provisions, receives the names and logo, and signs the Patent Pledge.
4Transfer. A formal revision hands decisions on the protocol and the record to the Committee and records the date.
5Steady state. Seats are elected by active contributors; the Company's Board sets direction; ESC oversees.

The Company

The legal entity sets the general direction of the project — strategy, budget, contracts, the commercial edition — and holds the names Omega42 and Ω42, the logo, and the badges that mark genuine Ω42 content. Its Board has five directors: the Originator, two appointed by the Committee, and two independent. It owns no patent rights and never will; every idea stays free for exploration and creative expansion. These commitments are locked into its founding documents and cannot be changed without the Overseer and the Committee.

Patent Pledge

No patents, no assertion, no assignment, binding on successors.

The Committee

Seven seats covering the work: mathematics; AI systems and evaluation; philosophy and interpretation; traditions and lenses; research method; community and education; one open seat. Two-year terms, decisions by majority with a quorum of four, protocol revisions by five votes, every decision published with its reasons, interests declared.

What the Overseer keeps

Four things only: the premise (that the 24-step loop and its 81 states are all that exists), the reader's sovereignty over judgment, the openness of the record, and consent to any change of the Company's locked provisions. A veto is published with its reasons.

Releases and change levels

A change is A, editorial (a maintainer approves once the checks pass), B, Companion and records (source and consistency checks and human review), or C, Core (explicit approval by ESC, later the Committee, with its rationale). A discussion post never becomes part of Ω42 by itself: it becomes a proposal, is reviewed, accepted, and published in a numbered release. AI agents prepare changes and never approve their own.
