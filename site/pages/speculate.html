Speculative Foundations Program

Explore without boundaries. Investigate with every method.

Ω42 is a thought experiment, and speculation is one of its engines. An idea does not need to be established before it can be explored, and a possibility does not need to be conventional before it can be investigated. Never confuse what cannot currently be demonstrated with what cannot legitimately be explored.

What could be?Exploration

Unrestricted. No idea is turned away for being unusual, unproven, untestable or at odds with current theory.

What follows?Development

Logical and mathematical consequences: definitions, structures, derivations, computations.

What supports it, and what counts against it?Examination

Arguments, evidence, internal consistency, comparison with other frameworks. Examination informs an idea; it does not close the door on it.

An idea's status is kept apart from its freedom: an idea can be fully free to be explored and still stand as a conjecture (Companion Part 234).

The Exploration Register

Ideas in development

EX-001 · Historical existence and the transformation of patterns

Existence has three layers: structural, historical and meaningful (APE-510 section 6).

Open: Whether meaningful existence is an accident of structure and history or an expression of a deeper feature of existence (EX-003).

EX-002 · Foundations of existence

Existence may be relational rather than object-based: an entity's identity is partly constituted by its relations (APE-510 section 5).

Open: Whether identity in general, not only in this model, is relational.

EX-003 · Meaning and purpose

Meaning may be constructed by conscious beings; may emerge from relations between living systems and environments; may follow from information processing and self-organization; or may be a feature of reality that complex systems increasingly express. These are not exclusive (APE-510 section 6).

Kept open. None is selected. Meaning is never graded (Core L-495) and the Meaning Registry is never read (L-497).

EX-004 · Possible realities

What if the law of Omega42 were a different two-term law?

Open: What distinguishes the Fibonacci law among the 16 beyond its coefficients (its norm, its eigenvalues phi and -1/phi, its unit discriminant 5).

EX-005 · Consciousness and experience

The distinction between observer and observed may be emergent rather than fundamental (APE-510 section 4).

Open: Whether experience itself is captured by any reading.

EX-006 · Historical existence and the transformation of patterns

A world in which choices fade versus a world in which they are conserved.

Open: Which of the two describes a given human pattern; registrable as H-033.

Featured speculation

Why does nature pick Fibonacci?

Of the 54 invertible two-term laws on the numbers modulo nine, sixteen reach the full 24-step loop, and Fibonacci, with coefficients (1, 1), is the smallest of them. Could an economy of coefficients explain which patterns we meet: among laws that give the same longest cycle, the world shows the cheapest? Develop it, challenge it, or propose a rival.
