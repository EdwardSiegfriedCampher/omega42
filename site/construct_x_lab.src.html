Orbit Classifier

Ω42's step T(a, b) = (b, a + b) on a clock of 3^k marks. The address Λ = (r, N(u)² mod 3^(k−r)), where 3^r is the largest power of 3 dividing the state and N(a, b) = a² + ab − b², is a complete orbit invariant at every depth (Core 10.5.7, CA-11). On the nine-mark clock the canonical orbit of (2, 5) is the one orbit with Λ = (0, 4).

Temporal Observatory

Lagged-Increment Library: each Lagged-Increment Model, lag interval d from 2 to 24, forecasts the next value from the change over the last d steps. The lag is chosen on the first 60%, scored on the last 40% against the trend-only forecast, and tested against 199 surrogates. Both nulls are reported. The block null passed its first test on new real data (H-006d, Mauna Loa CO₂: candidate base lag 12, block p = 0.01) and its length of 24 is calibrated on AR(1) noise (CX-012a); sunspots keep their original verdict under the rule that required both nulls (H-006b). Every scan also scores five ordinary forecasters on the held-out part, because seasonal-naive beats the Lagged-Increment Model when there is no trend (CX-011), and loses to it once trend and season combine (H-017b).
