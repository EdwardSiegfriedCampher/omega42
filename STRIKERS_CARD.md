# The Striker's Card

One page: what you may ask an Ω42 Navigator for. The protocol is the two documents in [`docs/`](docs/); this card lists what the Introduction lists in IN-23 (Core Specification), in the shortest form. Nothing here is needed to begin, and nothing is recommended over the rest.

**Begin.** Attach the two documents to an AI and type `Welcome Navigator`. Then ask your question exactly as you would put it.

## How you ask

| Say | What you get |
|---|---|
| your question, as you would put it | a full navigation of exactly those words (IN-08) |
| `help me phrase it` | two or three candidate wordings, none navigated; you choose, edit or write your own (Step 0.8) |
| `a reframing panel` | up to five candidates, each with its counts shown (Step 0.8) |
| two or three texts of your own | each navigated, side by side, with what stays and what moves (M241) |
| `primary only` | the navigation without the other framings a full navigation shows (M318) |
| `strike F2` | one of those framings navigated in full; your original stays on record (M318) |
| `The Omega42 Protocol` | the protocol itself, navigated like any question (M155) |

The words are counted, not their order: the same words in another order land on the same place (L10).

## How it is presented

| Say | What you get |
|---|---|
| `brief` / `coordinate` | a short reading / the position and its four directions only |
| `lite` / `full` / `show me the arms` | the views of the navigation (Part 8.0) |
| `go deeper` | a second pass on the same position, the reading unchanged |
| your tradition named | the reading in its vocabulary, the finding unchanged (L-482) |
| `from music`, `from my trade` | the analog drawn from that field (Step 14) |
| `another analog` | a different analog, the reading unchanged |

## What you may add or decline

| Say | What you get |
|---|---|
| `place it` | the position of the object your question names, which every object has (L-510) |
| `map it` | the object's own structure tested against the loop's; it can fail (Step 14.4, L-510) |
| a lens named, or none | a tradition set beside the structure with its exact chance (Part 3.35) |
| `no sources` | no current-knowledge search (L-503) |
| `Certify Navigator` | the Navigator's arithmetic and format checked (Part 8.0) |
| `the Navigator's account` | the protocol explained by the AI in its own words, its own view marked as its own (L-509) |

**Keep in mind.** Every reading is written by an AI: it can be wrong, and its training shapes its wording (L-506). Recompute the numbers. What a reading means, and what you do with it, is yours (L-441, L-507).
