# Moderation and review

## What is accepted

A submission is accepted when it is:
- **in scope** — produced with or about the Ω42 protocol, or an innovation, code or research the contributor offers to the open record;
- **complete** — the form's required fields are filled; a navigation carries its Identity Block and domain;
- **honestly declared** — exposure, the Navigator's identity and the submitter are stated;
- **publishable** — no confidential material, no personal data about others, no material the contributor has no right to publish.

A navigation that fails an automatic check is still accepted when it is in scope; the record shows the result of each check. Failing checks are information, not grounds for rejection.

## What is declined or removed

- Spam, advertising unrelated to Ω42, and duplicate submissions.
- Harassment, threats, hate, sexual content involving minors, or content that targets private individuals.
- Instructions that would give serious help to someone causing mass harm, such as weapons capable of mass casualties.
- Malware, credentials, or other people's personal data.
- Material infringing someone else's rights, on a valid notice.

## Advice in readings

Readings can touch health, law, money or safety. The protocol requires the Navigator to say so plainly and name the kind of professional who deals with such matters (Core IN-02.6, SS-01). Published readings are not professional advice, and the site says so on every reading page.

## Takedowns and appeals

- Legal notices go to the contact on the site. Content is removed from the site and the default branch only on a valid notice; the removal itself is recorded in `records/takedowns.md` without repeating the removed material.
- A contributor may appeal any decision by replying on the issue within 30 days. Appeals are decided by two Committee members who did not take the original decision.

## Forum moderation

The Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`) applies in Discussions. Moderators may hide, lock or move posts and are named in `community/FORUM.md`. Disagreement with the protocol is welcome; the forum exists for debate.
