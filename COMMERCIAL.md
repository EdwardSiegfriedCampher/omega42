# Free and commercial editions

Ω42 has three ways in. The protocol, the reference scripts and the open record belong to everyone and are never placed behind payment.

## Free, with no condition (always)

- The latest protocol documents, every earlier release, and their hashes. The two documents are the whole release (Core L-493): anyone holding them holds every tool.
- The **Strike** app on the site: type a question and get its coordinate, its four directions, its state, its mirror and its phase, computed in your browser by the reference script itself. Your question is not sent anywhere.
- Instructions for running a full navigation with any AI ("Welcome Navigator"), on your own account.
- Publication, recognition, the forum, the Library, the Congruence Register and the Prior Art Ledger.
- The monthly newsletter, for registered contributors and for everyone who submits Ω42-generated content (`community/NEWSLETTER.md`). Registering as a contributor is free and needs no submission.
- All reference tools, run locally or in CI.

A question you want to keep to yourself goes through one of these routes.

## The Ω42 web-app: free, in exchange for the reading

The full navigation on the site — a hosted Navigator, with the audit, the format check and the Congruence Record attached — is free to everyone on one condition, shown in plain words and agreed to before the first strike: **the reading you receive is submitted to the open record.**

What happens to a submitted reading:

1. **It is checked on arrival** by the intake (Companion Appendix N.2): the arithmetic recomputed, the format check, the frame scan and the Aperture audit.
2. **It is placed beside every other reading.** Its domain enters the Congruence Register, so later readings can show where questions meet — on the same state, on its mirror, or resting where another stands — each against its chance. The arithmetic never changes; the record grows with every reading.
3. **It is sorted** by the host-AI, the curator (`agents/curator/SPEC.md`): by research area, coordinate, state, voice and half, and by the lenses each reading selected.
4. **It feeds the monthly newsletter** (`community/NEWSLETTER.md`), which every web-app user, as a submitter, may choose to receive.

Your side of the exchange (`PRIVACY.md`):

- You mark each question **public**, **professional** or **personal** when you strike it. A public or professional reading is submitted in full. A **personal** reading joins the record only as a *redacted-domain record*: its coordinate, state and domain hash, with no text — the hash is over your exact words, so a redacted wording would be a different question, and the record does not pretend otherwise. A personal reading enters the research corpus only if you opt in to that separately (Companion 28.2).
- Names and personal details are removed from a public or professional copy before submission, and you see the copy that will be published.
- You may withdraw your consent at any time; new readings are then not submitted, and you may ask for your submitted readings to be removed from the site, the Congruence Register and the research corpus. Copies others have already made under the open licence cannot be recalled; attribution is changed as you ask.
- Attribution is yours to choose: your name, a handle, or none.

## Commercial software derived from Ω42

Commercial applications built on the protocol — by the Company or by licensees of the *Official edition* badge — **do not require submission of their outputs.** A user of a commercial application may opt in to submit a reading to the open record, reading by reading or as a standing choice; nothing is submitted without that opt-in. Commercial users receive the monthly newsletter whether or not they submit. The protocol inside a commercial application is the same protocol: the reference arithmetic, the Aperture and the judgment of the person are unchanged (Core Part 7).

Applications under consideration, with the protocol as their hub:

| Application | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Hosted Navigator | Full navigations on a script-capable Navigator, with the audit, the format check and the Congruence Record attached. |
| Batch and API | Navigate lists of questions, compare wordings at scale, export records; a documented API with keys and quotas. |
| Research workspace | Team projects, shared registers, private drafts, and tools for the studies of Core Appendix S. |
| Register explorer | Maps of where questions meet on the structure, across the open record and a team's own readings. |
| Journal and Return Log | Strike the same wording again later and record what changed, knowing the structure did not (Core M311). |
| Navigation audit | Checks any AI's navigation against the protocol (Core M314). |
| Lens studio | Registers new lenses and tools for selection by relevance (Core L-494) and records their role (Core M313). |
| Cross-architecture panels | SR-CAS across several AI systems, with blind arrangements. |
| Readings in your tradition and language | Cultural rendering (Core L-482) and translation. |
| Support and training | Onboarding, courses, workshops, and consulting by verified professionals. |

Commercial customers receive services and the right to use the edition's name and badges; they receive no ownership of ideas, and the Company grants no patent rights because it holds none (`PATENT_PLEDGE.md`).

## Ω42 Compatible: certification of AI systems

An AI company may choose to have its AI system certified **Ω42 Compatible**. Certification is run by the Certification Body, a dedicated legal entity (`ENTITY.md`), under `CERTIFICATION.md`. It is voluntary, and it is never required to run the protocol.

## Money

Proceeds of software sales and of certification go first to the **upkeep and maintenance of the facility** — the hosted web-app and Navigator, the site, the record and its archive, the curator agent, and the newsletter — and with it the free routes above. What remains funds research grants and the Stewardship Committee's work. Income and spending are reported annually by the Company (`GOVERNANCE.md` §6). Pricing is set by the Company's Board and published before launch; no price is fixed in this document.
